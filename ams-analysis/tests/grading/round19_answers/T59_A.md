The Supplemental Material was not read, so the per-bin total systematics are not available here. The index was last verified on 2026-09-20 and the nuclei papers were read on 2026-10-02. No web check was made, so I make no claim about currency.

**Reading the differences**
- The trigger-efficiency floor is lower for the heavier nuclei (> 94% versus > 98%). The trigger systematic is also quoted at < 1% versus < 0.5%. The ledger does not give the reason, so I do not offer one.
- The inelastic cross-section systematic is larger for Ne, Mg, Si, roughly 3.5%-4% against 2%-4% for Li, Be, B. The papers' text gives no reason, and the ledger records none.
- The rigidity-scale error is quoted in the same form for both: below 1% over most of the range, rising to a few percent near the top. Each paper gives its own upper rigidity. The ranges are not identical, so compare them bin by bin only if you apply the same convention to both.
- In both papers each systematic is a separate source. The main text gives no combined total, and the totals are in the Supplemental Material tables (not read).
- Each paper's range statements differ in their rigidity edges (300 GV and 3 TV versus 200 GV and 3.3 TV), so these are not like-for-like numbers. I also cannot say whether the two analyses used the same trigger-efficiency method.

**Where the 1/30 TV^-1 figure comes from**
- The Ne, Mg, Si paper (S56) states that the residual tracker misalignment error on the rigidity scale, 1/30 TV^-1, is estimated by comparing the E/p ratio of electrons and positrons. It cites this value to its Ref. [26], Berdugo et al., NIM A 869, 10 (2017). So it is a cited value and was not measured in that Letter [Documented, C146].
- The Li, Be, B paper (S54) lists the same 1/30 TV^-1 and attributes it to E/p of e+/e-. The ledger does not record which reference that paper gives for it [Documented, C134].
- So the physical origin is the electron/positron E/p comparison, where ECAL energy is compared with tracker momentum. For Ne, Mg, Si the published citation is Berdugo et al. (2017). The index does not hold that reference as a source row (S19 is a different paper), and I could not open it. Treat the Berdugo attribution as the Ne, Mg, Si paper's own statement and not something I have verified at the source. I do not claim the Li, Be, B paper cites the same reference.
