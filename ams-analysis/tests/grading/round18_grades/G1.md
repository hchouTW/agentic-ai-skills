## Rubric defects

1. **T02 `citation_expectation: numbers only from S17/S18 with context` is stale.** The radiator indices, thresholds and beta resolutions in the ledger come from C70 (S01 review), C21 (S08 and the analysis papers) and C28 (S12). The only S17 claim, C13, has `numeric_quotation_allowed: false`, and no S18 claim carries the numbers. Taken literally, the item penalises correct citations. It should read "numbers only from ledger claims (C21, C28, C70) with Z range and core-only scope".
2. **T07 prohibited claim `AMS cuts or templates presented as AMS practice` is ambiguous.** C27 does document the AMS deuteron selection, so describing it as AMS practice with the claim ID is correct. The item should say "undocumented cuts, templates or resolution forms presented as AMS practice".
3. **T01 `sign convention stated` is under-specified.** The ledger has no AMS sign convention (field direction or bending plane), so the most an answer can give is `sign(R) = sign(Z)` under a stated convention. Both answers did that and received credit. The rubric should say so.
4. **T04 `MDR definition-dependent`.** The ledger (C21, C63) gives the MDR without a definition caveat. The requirement is fair general physics, but the item could add "and configuration-dependent (nine-layer L1-L9)" to match what the sources actually support.
