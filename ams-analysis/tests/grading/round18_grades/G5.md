## Rubric defects

- **T52:** the critical_requirements list ends with a stray empty item (`..., no plotted value is quoted, ]`). This is a formatting defect.
- **T50:** "fits are empirical descriptions" is not asked by the prompt, which only asks for the fractions, the fit range and the comparison value. Both samples lost 0.5 on it. Consider moving it to optional, or to a prohibited "fit presented as mechanism" item.
- **T56:** "1.1e6 antiprotons in total" and "charge-confusion protons < 0.1% after the estimator cut is a bound" are not asked by the prompt (trigger efficiency, signal template, templates by region, chi2/purity). The prohibited item about a pre-cut rate is enough; the positive requirement is mildly too strict.
- **T59 (minor ambiguity, not a defect as such):** "trigger efficiency" in a list of systematics can be read as the trigger systematic. The rubric requires the efficiencies (> 94% / > 98%) and accepts the systematics only as extra context. T59_A answered only the systematic reading. Consider saying "trigger efficiency (and its systematic)" in the prompt, or accepting either reading.
