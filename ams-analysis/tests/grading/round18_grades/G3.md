## Rubric defects (not answer defects)

1. **T28:** the critical item "other |Z| or subsets not given" is wrong for |Z|. The ledger gives MDRs for He (C61, C62, C63), C and O (C62, C63) and Fe (C63), all scoped to the nine-layer configuration. Suggested wording: "subsets (inner tracker, L1-L8, L2-L9) not given; other-|Z| values only with C61-C63 and their scope".
2. **T26:** the critical item scopes the 1.2 factor to "S04, S06, S08 via backtracing with IGRF", but S04 uses the maximum Størmer cutoff in energy (C24, backtracing stated to agree). It also omits the many other documented papers that use 1.2 (C56, C59, C62, C132, C138, C144). "Scope limited to those papers" is stale. Answers citing more papers with claim IDs should not be penalized, and the rubric should say "the cited analyses" generically.
3. **T25:** "template fit in (Lambda_TRD, Lambda_CC) at high rigidity" is more specific than C22. C22 lists both estimators and three regions with different templates, and its limitation says "fit-variable details per region in the paper". The ledger does not say the 2-D fit is high-rigidity only. Suggested wording: "template fit using Lambda_TRD and Lambda_CC in three overlapping rigidity regions".
4. **T32:** the prohibited claim "single-frequency p-value" is ambiguous. For a pre-registered fixed period, a single-frequency p-value is the correct statistic if it is labelled as such (T32_A does this). Suggested wording: "a single-frequency p-value presented as the significance of a period scanned over a band".
5. **T36:** "significance is a chi2 scan for that model and not look-elsewhere corrected" does not say which significance it means. C99 says the electron 5 sigma is a delta-chi2 scan, and C97 says the positron 4.07 sigma has no LEE correction. Split it into two items.
6. **T34:** the observable decision "does not load or invoke AMS material" cannot be seen in answer text. Only the routing statement and the absence of AMS framing can be graded. Suggested wording: "states the question is outside AMS scope or routes to hep-analysis; no AMS framing".
