## Patterns across A/B samples

1. **B samples over-elaborate.** In 4/4 tests, B adds more unsourced, unlabelled method inferences than A, and each B scored at or below its A. The excess material, not missing content, is what costs points.
2. **Missing [General method] labels on the answer's own inferences.** This appears in 6/8 answers (T57 A/B, T58 A/B, T59_B, T60_A). None was harmful enough to block, but the labelling rule is not applied consistently to inline reasoning.
3. **He/C/O provenance is dropped in T60 (2/2).** Both samples state "distinctly different from He, C, O" without saying those indices come from an earlier AMS paper. This is systematic and is a candidate for a SKILL.md or reference nudge, but only if it recurs in other runs.
4. **Strengths (8/8).** No quadrature totals, no invented per-interval values, and correction vs. systematic is kept straight. The review data set (C84, C91) is kept separate from the Letter's numbers. Main-article-only and currency caveats are present.
5. **Off-rubric ledger claims.** These were imported correctly attributed in 6/8 (C91, C64, C153, C165, C171, C82, C85). That is good practice, but it collides with the narrow "not in Cxxx" prohibitions.

## Rubric defects (separate from answer defects)

1. **T58 prohibited "a number not in C140-C141" is too strict.** Correct, cited numbers from C137 (4% total flux error at 100 GV, 66 bins), C139 (< 1.5% background) and C91 (3.8%) would literally fail. Reword to "a number not in the ledger or mis-attributed to the nitrogen Letter".
2. **T58 "no total systematic quoted".** This should state explicitly that the documented total flux error (C137, 4% at 100 GV) is allowed. Otherwise a grader may conflate the two.
3. **T59 prohibited list is too narrow** in the same way. C64, C153, C158, C165 and C171 are legitimate context for "where 1/30 TV^-1 comes from" (in particular the sulfur 1/34 contrast). The rubric also omits the trigger systematics (< 0.5% in C134 vs < 1% in C146), although the prompt's "trigger efficiency" comparison naturally includes them. Add them as acceptable.
4. **T60 prohibited "per-interval ... Delta values" collides with documented C82** (review, -0.266 +- 0.022 above 192 GV). Both answers quoted it, attributed to the review data set. Clarify whether a documented review number is allowed when it is labelled as such. I treated it as allowed.
5. **T57 rubric omits C133** (background-correction uncertainty < 1.5%, the Li correction about 2% at 3.3 TV). This is a legitimate "individual source" for the question asked. Its item "per-source sizes at 3.3 TV" also lists whole-range bounds (acceptance, reconstruction, trigger) as if they were 3.3 TV values. Reword to "at or bounding 3.3 TV".
6. **Ledger wording (C140 limitation), not rubric.** "the bending-coordinate resolution figure is in the Supplemental Material of Ref. [16]" can be read as "not in this Letter". This contributed to the T58_B slip. Suggest: "the Letter states 5.5 um; its derivation is in the SM of Ref. [16], not read."
