**AMS nitrogen flux paper (S55, PRL 121, 051103; main article only, Supplemental Material not read)**

**Selection** [Documented, C138]
- Tracker: downward-going event with a reconstructed inner-tracker track passing through L1 (also through L9 for R >= 1.3 TV). Track fit chi2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate. Rigidity is measured with the tracker in the permanent-magnet field.
- Geomagnetic cutoff: measured rigidity must exceed 1.2 times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff within the AMS field of view. The cutoff is found by backtracing from the top of AMS out to 50 Earth radii with the most recent IGRF model. Varying the factor from 1.0 to 1.4 gives a negligible systematic (< 0.1% below 30 GV) [Documented, C141].
- Charge: the charge measured on L1, the upper TOF, the inner tracker and the lower TOF (and L9 for R > 1.3 TV) must all be compatible with Z = 7.
- Exposure: only periods with the detector in normal conditions, pointing within 40 degrees of the local zenith, and the ISS outside the SAA [Documented, C137].

**Purity** [Documented, C138]
- Overall purities are 90%-95%, depending on rigidity.
- Charge confusion from non-interacting nuclei is negligible (< 0.1%) over the whole rigidity range.
- The per-cut definitions and charge-selection figures are in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.

**Backgrounds from heavier nuclei interacting in the detector** [Documented, C139]
- Interactions between L1 and L2 (O, F, Ne in the TRD and upper TOF material):
  - Method: data-driven. The L1 charge distribution of events selected as nitrogen by the inner tracker is fitted with N, O, F and Ne charge templates. The templates come from L2 using non-interacting events, requiring L1 and L3-L8 to measure the same charge.
  - Size: < 5% over the entire rigidity range.
- Interactions above L1 (O, F, Ne in materials above L1):
  - Method: estimated from simulation.
  - Size: < 3% below 200 GV, rising to 6% at 3.3 TV.
- The overall uncertainty from total background subtraction is < 1.5% over the entire rigidity range.
- The simulation validation of the nuclear interactions is in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.

**Caveats**
- These are one paper's choices (nitrogen, 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, May 2011 to May 2016). They are not a rule for other AMS analyses.
- The quoted percentages are the paper's own bounds. I have not extended them to other species.
- The recorded 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events are after background subtraction [Documented, C137].
