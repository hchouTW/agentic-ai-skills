The ACC (Anti-Coincidence Counters) is AMS-02's scintillator veto system. [Documented, C13, C69]

- **Layout.** Sixteen curved scintillator panels sit inside the magnet bore around the Tracker. Wavelength-shifting fibres carry the light to PMTs. The vertical edges are tongue-and-groove, so a particle crossing a seam is seen in two panels. [Documented, C69]
- **Purpose.** It rejects particles that enter or leave the tracker volume through the sides, outside the nominal acceptance, and events with extra activity. Event-level veto is its only role. [Documented, C13, C69]
- **Trigger.** For |Z| = 1 the level-1 trigger requires four TOF signals and no ACC signal. [Documented, C69]
- **Published efficiency.** The side-entry rejection efficiency is 0.99999, valid only for |Z| = 1 in the cited analyses and configuration. In the review (C69) it is a pre-flight long-duration test result. [Documented, C21, C69]
- **What it does not do.** It measures no charge sign, |Z|, β or energy, and it does not identify particle species. It is not an independent measurement of anything else. [Documented, C13]

Caveats for analysis use:
- A tighter veto loses valid events to accidental or ECAL-backsplash activity. That loss is an efficiency, possibly energy- and time-dependent, and must be measured. [General method]
- The veto's effect must not also be folded into the geometric acceptance, which would double count it. [General method]
