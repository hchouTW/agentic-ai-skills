**Verdict: not publishable as described. The structure N/(A ε ΔR T) is the right skeleton, but the single bin-by-bin MC factor is unjustified and the recipe leaves the main AMS-specific corrections undefined. Fix the items below before it counts as a flux.** [General method; AMS practice labels are from the skill's documented-practice section, e.g. C31 (proton flux) and C30 (migration size)]

**Defects, in priority order**

1. **Bin-by-bin factor under steep-spectrum migration (blocking).**
   - A bin-by-bin factor is the diagonal estimator, Φ_i = (n_i - b_i)/(ΔR_i A_i ε_i T_i). It is valid only if migration is negligible. A steeply falling proton spectrum with finite rigidity resolution (and non-Gaussian tails) feeds low-R events upward into higher reconstructed bins. The bias grows with rigidity and depends on the spectral shape.
   - Resolution core "looks small" is not a migration study. Public AMS flux analyses unfold the migration instead. For phosphorus the correction is about +25% at 3 GV and -21% at 1.2 TV [Documented, C30]; I give this only as a scale of the effect, not as a proton number.
   - The MC factor also inherits the MC input spectrum, so the correction is circular. If the MC spectrum differs from the true one, the factor is biased.
   - Proposal [Proposal]: use the response R_ij = P(R_meas in bin i | R_true in bin j, selected), with μ_i = Σ_j R_ij A_j ε_j T_j Φ_j ΔR_j + b_i. Either unfold or forward-fold. Keep the bin-by-bin factor only if you show negligible migration with the response matrix.

2. **No stated estimand or variable.**
   - It is unclear whether the target is a flux in rigidity R or in kinetic energy, and whether the bins are in reconstructed or true variable.
   - Define the target as Φ(R) for primary protons at the top of the instrument, in m^-2 sr^-1 s^-1 GV^-1. State the bin-centre prescription. For a steep spectrum an arithmetic bin centre is wrong.
   - Any conversion to T/A needs Z and A, and the Jacobian, not just relabelled bins.

3. **"Acceptance times livetime" is too loose, and double counting is possible.**
   - Acceptance, efficiency, livetime and geomagnetic transmission are distinct. Exposure is the time-integral of effective acceptance times livetime times transmission. It is not simply A × T if either varies in time.
   - The MC factor must not re-apply acceptance or efficiency already inside A or ε, and the response must not include them a second time. Give each effect one `effect_id` and list where it is applied once.
   - Define the acceptance from a generation record: surface, solid angle, spectrum, weights, and a reweighting to the target spectrum that is not circular.
   - Efficiencies must be conditional factors, not a product of marginals. Check factorization with the joint efficiency and two orderings. Data/MC scale factors need a named control sample and are species- and rigidity-specific.

4. **Backgrounds are missing: "after cuts" is not a count of protons.**
   - Needed: charge-migration and adjacent-Z contamination, He and D fragmentation contributions, electron or positron and TRD-related lepton contamination (rigidity dependent), and albedo or upward-going particles at low R.
   - Use n_i - b_i with a background ledger and covariance from template fits or other estimators.

5. **Geomagnetic selection and quality time not mentioned.**
   - State the cutoff model and safety factor, and vary them as a systematic. The documented AMS practice for the public proton flux is rigidity above 1.2 times the maximum cutoff in the field of view, with a scan of the factor [Documented, C31; scans in C23/C27/C49]. Treat this as a reference point for your own choice, not a rule.
   - Also state normal-detector-condition time, the zenith-pointing limit and the SAA exclusion.
   - Include survival and interaction probability through the upstream material, with a stated cross-section model and its variation.

6. **Statistical and systematic treatment is absent.**
   - Needed: Poisson statistics (exact intervals where counts are low, e.g. at high R); a systematic ledger (acceptance, trigger and selection efficiency, rigidity scale, resolution and tails, unfolding, cutoff, material model, background template); and full bin-to-bin covariance. Adjacent bins are correlated through unfolding and shared systematics. Add independent sources in quadrature only where they really are independent.
   - Mark any rigidity-scale or resolution input that you do not have as [Unknown/needs input].

7. **No validation plan.**
   - "Agreement with MC" or with the published spectrum is not validation. State the metric and the trigger for changing the method or adding an uncertainty.
   - Needed: MC closure with the nominal spectrum, closure with a reweighted spectrum, a distorted-spectrum stress test, response perturbations (resolution, tails, scale), and pulls from Poisson pseudo-experiments.
   - Cross-checks: loose/tight cuts, alternate track configurations, time-period splits, detector-region splits, and data-driven versus MC efficiency.
