**Short answer:** In the 2016 AMS antiproton paper (PRL 117, 091103, S08) the charge-confusion protons were not removed event by event. They were separated statistically with a template fit, using a dedicated charge-confusion estimator. Everything below is [Documented, C22] and covers the main article only. The Supplemental Material was not read.

**Method [Documented, C22]**
- Sample: the negative-rigidity sample, which holds antiprotons, electrons, pi-, and protons whose charge sign was reconstructed wrongly.
- Fit variables: two estimators.
  - Lambda_TRD is the per-layer TRD log-likelihood ratio of the electron hypothesis to the proton hypothesis. It separates electrons and pi- from the hadron-like antiprotons.
  - Lambda_CC is a BDT charge-confusion estimator. Its inputs are the track chi-squared, rigidities from different layer combinations, hits near the track, and the TOF and tracker charges. It separates real negative-rigidity particles from tracks whose sign was flipped by the tracker.
- Fit regions: three overlapping rigidity regions, each with its own templates (low 1.00-4.02 GV, intermediate 2.97-18.0 GV, high 16.6-450 GV).
- Templates:
  - The antiproton signal template comes from the high-statistics proton data. It was checked against MC and against p-bar/p data at 2.97-18.0 GV.
  - The electron/pi- and charge-confusion-proton templates come from data and MC.
  - The charge-confusion MC was checked with 400 GV proton test-beam data.
- Charge-confusion rate: the fitted charge-confusion component gives the contamination of the negative sample. No tail rate is quoted here, because the index lists none for protons.

**Context**
- Charge sign comes only from the Tracker and magnet. TRD, TOF, ECAL and RICH do not measure it, so the discrimination rests on tail-sensitive tracker information and cannot use the Gaussian core of the rigidity resolution.
- Downstream steps [Documented, C23]:
  - Iterative unfolding, stopped when consecutive iterations agree within 0.1%.
  - Systematics: template shapes contribute 12% at 450 GV and under 1% below 30 GV, and the event selection was varied about 1000 times.
- The 2025 solar-cycle paper (S09) used the same strategy, with a per-rigidity-region template fit to separate antiprotons from electrons and pions. The template statistical fluctuation is a 1.5% systematic at 1 GV (C121).
  - The S09 selection and fit details (C175-C177) are in its Supplemental Material text, which is not transcribed here, so the details in this answer are S08's.
