**Short answer (as of the skill's last verification, 2026-09-20; I have no web access, so I cannot confirm currency now).** The most recent AMS nuclei paper the index records is "Properties of Heavy Cosmic Nuclei P, Cl, Ar, K, Ca", PRL 136, 241002 (S15), listed on the Collaboration publications page as published 17 June 2026 [Documented, C34; website list, not a journal record; consistent with the INSPIRE list S21]. S15 is read at main-article level only, and the Supplemental Material was not opened.

**What it covered** (main article only):
- Data period: 19 May 2011 to 26 Nov 2024, about 13.5 years [Documented, C30, C12]. The sample is about one million P, Cl, Ar, K and Ca events, drawn from 2.4 x 10^11 events in the C30 selection [Documented, C12, C30].
- Result: the fluxes are described as sums of primary and secondary components [Documented, C12; abstract-level statement].
- Analysis methods [Documented, C30]:
  - Bin-to-bin migration is corrected by unfolding. The correction to the P flux is about +25% at 3 GV, +8% at 10 GV, -2% at 100 GV, -10% at 300 GV and -21% at 1.2 TV.
  - Charge confusion from non-interacting nuclei is below 2% for P and below 1% for Cl, Ar, K and Ca.
  - The background comes from interactions of heavier nuclei above tracker L2 (S, Cl, Ar, K, Ca, Sc, Ti, V, Cr, Fe). The part arising between L1 and L2 (TRD and upper TOF) is evaluated by fitting the tracker-L1 charge distribution with Si-Ti charge templates.
- I did not find flux values, spectral indices or primary fractions for S15 in the index, so I quote none. The primary-versus-secondary reading is the paper's own description, not a proof of mechanism.
