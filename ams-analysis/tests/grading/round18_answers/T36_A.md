**Short answer: no to both parts.**

**Positron cutoff: 4.07 sigma, not 5 sigma.**
- The cutoff energy is E_s = 810 (+310, -180) GeV. It is a parameter of a diffuse-plus-source-term fit to the positron flux (0.5-1000 GeV), with an exponential cutoff on the source term. [Documented, C97, S01 review; same value as C35]
- Its significance is 4.07 sigma. The abstract of the 2019 positron paper (S04, PRL 122, 041102) says "more than 4 sigma", and the body gives 4.07 sigma. That is the point where 1/E_s = 0 in the six-parameter fit. [Documented, C35, C97]
- A 5 sigma claim is not supported.
- What the 4.07 sigma means:
  - It is a model-dependent fit statement, not a model-independent detection. The model is a source term plus a collision (diffuse) term.
  - E_s is the cutoff of a parametrized source term, not a measured spectral break.
  - It has no look-elsewhere correction.
  - The collaboration's interpretation (pulsar, dark matter) is a separate model step.
- Data period for S04: 2011-05-19 to 2017-11-12. [Documented, C24]
- Related, but a different quantity: in the review (S01), piecewise fits give a positron spectral change above about 284 (+91, -64) GeV. That is stated as a decrease of more than 3 sigma, with the transition energy free and no look-elsewhere correction. [Documented, C96] It is not the 810 GeV cutoff and not 5 sigma.

**Electron flux: it does not show the same cutoff.**
- The review fitted the electron flux in 41.61-1400 GeV with the positron source term (E_s = 810 GeV fixed, parameters from the positron fit). The fit is equally good with the source normalization f fixed to 1 (chi2/d.o.f. 15.5/24) and fixed to 0 (15.2/24). With f free, f = 0.5 (+1.2, -0.6) and chi2 does not improve. The electron data therefore neither require nor exclude a charge-symmetric positron source term. [Documented, C99]
- A power law with an exponential cutoff fitted to the electron data gives 1/E_s = 0.00 (+0.08, -0.00) TeV^-1. Cutoff energies E_s < 1.9 TeV are excluded at 5 sigma. [Documented, C99]
  - This exclusion is for that model and range, not for every cutoff shape.
  - It is a limit on a cutoff in electrons, not a detection.
- The electron flux is described by a power law with a gradual spectral hardening. The transition energy is E0 = 42.1 (+5.4, -5.2) GeV, with the 7 sigma significance for that hardening stated for the fit with E0 free. The index is nearly constant above 55.58 GeV. [Documented, C98] This is not a cutoff.
- The review states that the electron excess has a different nature from the positron excess. [Documented, C98]

**Caveats.**
- All energies here are in energy, not rigidity.
- The index was last verified 2026-09-20, and no web check for newer AMS results was possible here. I cannot say whether a later paper has changed the cutoff significance. Check INSPIRE-HEP and ams02.space for anything after 2019 (S04) or the 2021 review (S01).
