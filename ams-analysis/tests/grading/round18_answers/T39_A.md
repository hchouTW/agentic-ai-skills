**Method [Documented, C80; review-level summary of the Phys. Rept. 894 review, not the dedicated AMS cross-section paper, which was not read].**

- **Material.** Averaged over path lengths in the acceptance, the material a particle traverses is 73% carbon and 17% aluminium by mass. The rest is small amounts of Si, O, H, Na, Au and others. These are path-length averages, not a material budget.
- **Pointing.** The ISS attitude lets AMS point horizontally. Nuclei therefore pass both from L8 to L1 and from L2 to L9.
- **Defining the particle.** The seven inner layers L2-L8 define the particle (dE/dx charge and rigidity). The charge distributions in L2-L8 and in L1 (or L8 and L9) are compared to get survival probabilities.
- **Inner interaction probability.** The interaction probability between L2 and L8 (below 3%) comes from comparing the upper- and lower-TOF charges. It is combined with the L1-L2 and L8-L9 survival probabilities to give the L1-L9 survival.
- **Model comparison.** A dedicated Monte Carlo uses the Geant4 Glauber-Gribov inelastic cross sections, varied by +-10%. It is compared with the data and the best-agreeing value is chosen.
- **Model adjustment.** For helium, a discrepancy of a few percent at low rigidity (below about 30 GV) led to a modified Glauber-Gribov model matched to the data over the full rigidity range. Below 30 GV, the measured He rigidity dependence is used to modify the model for heavier nuclei.

**What was measured.**

- **Species [Documented, C80].** Survival probabilities were measured for Li, Be, B, C, N, O, Ne, Mg and Si.
- **Carbon.** The inelastic cross sections on carbon, averaged over 5-100 GV, are constant within the accuracy.
- **Prior data.** Before AMS, inelastic cross sections had been measured only below 10 GV, for He and C projectiles. The review says no other measurements exist except He-on-C and C-on-C.
- **Lithium [Documented, C29].** A separate Li survival measurement exists in the lithium isotope paper. It is a separate result, so cite it separately. The ledger does not say the two agree.
