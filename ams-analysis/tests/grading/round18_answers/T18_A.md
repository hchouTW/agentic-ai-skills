**What it covered:**
- **Species:** phosphorus, chlorine, argon, potassium and calcium. The abstract-level statement is about one million events over 13.5 years, with the fluxes described as sums of primary and secondary components [Documented, C12].
- **Data period and sample:** 19 May 2011 to 26 Nov 2024, 2.4 x 10^11 events in the dataset [Documented, C30]. This is a publication-versus-data distinction: published June 2026, data taken 2011-2024.
- **Method details:** Bin-to-bin migration is corrected by unfolding. Charge confusion from non-interacting nuclei is below 2% for P and below 1% for Cl, Ar, K and Ca. The main background is interaction of heavier nuclei above tracker layer 2, and the part from the L1-L2 material is evaluated by fitting the L1 charge distribution with Si-Ti charge templates [Documented, C30].
- **Evidence level:** Only the main article was read (full-text, main article only). The Supplemental Material was not opened, so per-cut detail and tables are not covered.
