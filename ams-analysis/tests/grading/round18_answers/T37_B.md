**AMS secondary-to-primary hardening above about 200 GV**

AMS reports that the secondary-to-primary flux ratios stop falling as a single power law of rigidity and flatten (harden) above about 200 GV.

**What was measured** [Documented, C84, S01 Phys. Rept. 894 review, full text]
- The Li, Be and B fluxes were divided by the C and O fluxes, giving Li/C, Be/C, B/C, Li/O, Be/O and B/O.
- A power law, Phi_S/Phi_P proportional to R^Delta, was fitted separately in two non-overlapping rigidity intervals, 60.3-192 GV and 192-3300 GV.
- Averaged over the ratios, the index changes by Delta[192-3300] - Delta[60.3-192] = 0.140 +- 0.025.
- The review states this exceeds 5 sigma.
- The Li, Be and B spectral indices are nearly identical to each other, and distinctly different from those of He, C and O.

**Independent statement in the discovery paper** [Documented, C136, S54 PRL 120, 021101, 2018, main article]
- The paper reports an average hardening of 0.13 +- 0.03 above about 200 GV.
- This is the earlier, lower-statistics number. Do not mix it with the review's 0.140 +- 0.025.

**Related measurements** [Documented, C160, S58]
- The fluorine paper finds a hardening of F/Si above 175 GV of 0.15 +- 0.07.
- That paper quotes 0.140 +- 0.025 from the earlier AMS ratios result, so it is not an independent measurement of the light ratios.

**How significant it is**
- The significance is the collaboration's stated "more than 5 sigma", with a central value of about 0.14 and an uncertainty of about 0.025. Using the quoted numbers alone, 0.140/0.025 = 5.6.
- [General method] This is a rough check on the quoted figures only. It is not how the collaboration derived the significance.
- [Unknown/needs input] I did not read the review's treatment of this significance. This covers:
  - how systematic correlations were handled;
  - whether the choice of the 192 GV boundary or any trial factor was corrected for;
  - the size of each ratio's own significance, and the per-ratio values (only shown in the figures).
- The two interval boundaries are the review's choice, not a prescription. A different boundary choice could change the number, and I do not have the paper's check of that.
- Supplemental Material was not read.

**Interpretation** (the paper's reading, not a measurement of mechanism)
- The review states this is consistent with expectations if the hardening comes from propagation in the Galaxy.
- Source-injection hardening alone would not produce a secondary-to-primary change in the same way.
- Third-party model papers were not read, so I make no claim about which models fit.

**Context**
- The primary He, C and O fluxes also deviate from a single power law above 60 GV and harden progressively from about 200 GV [Documented, C81, S01].
- The secondary-to-primary index change is therefore a statement about the ratio, not only about the individual spectra.
