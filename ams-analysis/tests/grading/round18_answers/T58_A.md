Source: AMS nitrogen flux Letter, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), S55; nitrogen flux 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, first five years on the ISS. Evidence level: main article only. The Supplemental Material was not read. No web check was done, so currency is not claimed (index last verified 2026-09-20).

**Rigidity resolution** [Documented, C140]
- The tracker bending-coordinate resolution is 5.5 um, stated as in agreement with simulation. The Letter gives only the number. Its derivation is in the Supplemental Material of the paper's Ref. [16], which I did not read.
- The maximum detectable rigidity (MDR) is 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty.
- The amplitudes of the non-Gaussian tails of the resolution function carry a 10% uncertainty. The tails matter for migration, so a Gaussian-core resolution is not enough.
- The resulting systematic error on the flux from the rigidity resolution function is below 1% under 150 GV and 3% at 3.3 TV.

**Bin-to-bin migration** [Documented, C140]
- Migration is corrected by unfolding. The Letter describes the procedure only by reference to its Ref. [20]. I did not read that reference, so I cannot say which algorithm, regularization or response construction was used.
- In the flux formula Phi_i = N_i / (A_i eps_i T_i dR_i), N_i is the event count corrected for migration.
- The correction (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i, with aleph_i the observed count, is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, -5% at 200 GV and -6% at 3.3 TV.
- Other sample facts [Documented, C137]: the flux uses 66 rigidity bins, and the Letter quotes 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events after background subtraction (C137, with a total flux error of 4% at 100 GV).

**Systematics quoted in the main text** [Documented, C141]
- **Absolute rigidity scale** (tracker misalignment, magnetic field map, temperature): below 0.6% up to 100 GV, rising to 5% in the last bin (1.3 to 3.3 TV).
- **Inelastic cross sections** (N+C and N+O): below 3% up to 100 GV, 4% at 3 TV.
- **Other terms quoted in the text:**
  - Trigger efficiency: below 0.7%.
  - Data/MC acceptance corrections: below 3% up to 500 GV and below 5% at 3.3 TV.
  - Reconstruction and selection: below 1%.
  - Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: below 0.1% under 30 GV.
  - Background subtraction (C139): below 1.5%.
- These are the values printed in the main text. The full error breakdown is tabulated in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.

**Caveats**
- [General method] The rigidity-scale and resolution terms act at the same high-rigidity end, and the Letter does not say how they are combined or whether they are correlated across bins. Do not add them as independent terms.
- The size of the unfolding or response-matrix systematic is not separated out in the text I read. Only the rigidity-resolution-function effect is quoted (the less-than-1% and 3% figures above).
