**Source:** AMS nitrogen flux paper, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), S55. Main article only was read (full-text level, 2026-10-02). The Supplemental Material, which holds per-cut definitions and the simulation validation, was not read. I had no web access, so this is not a currency check. Claim IDs below are from the ledger.

**Selection [Documented, C137, C138]**
- Data: 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events after background subtraction, 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016. Trigger efficiency is > 98%.
- Tracker:
  - The event is downward-going with an inner-tracker track through L1. For R >= 1.3 TV the track must also pass through L9.
  - Track fit chi2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate.
  - Rigidity comes from the tracker in the permanent-magnet field.
- Geomagnetic cutoff: the measured rigidity must exceed 1.2 times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff in the AMS field of view. The cutoff is found by backtracing from the top of AMS out to 50 Earth radii with the most recent IGRF model.
- Charge: the charge measured on L1, the upper TOF, the inner tracker, the lower TOF and (for R > 1.3 TV) L9 must all be compatible with Z = 7.

**Purity [Documented, C138]**
- Overall purity is 90-95%, depending on rigidity.
- Charge confusion from non-interacting nuclei is negligible (< 0.1%) over the whole range.

**Backgrounds from heavier nuclei interacting in the detector [Documented, C139]**

| Interaction location | Nuclei | Size | Estimation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Between L1 and L2 (TRD and upper TOF) | O, F, Ne | < 5% over the entire rigidity range | Data-driven: fit the L1 charge distribution of events the inner tracker selects as nitrogen, using N, O, F and Ne charge templates. The templates come from L2 and use non-interacting events in which L1 and L3-L8 measure the same charge. |
| Above L1 | O, F, Ne | < 3% below 200 GV, rising to 6% at 3.3 TV | Simulation |

- The overall uncertainty from total background subtraction is < 1.5% over the entire rigidity range.
- The below-L1 background is simulation-based, so its reliability rests on how well the simulation models nuclear interactions. The paper's validation of this is in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.
- The paper does not give a single "background fraction" that adds the two components. Do not sum the 5% and 3-6% figures into one number.
