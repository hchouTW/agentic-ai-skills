Source: AMS PRL 120, 021101 (2018), Li, Be, B paper; main article only (Supplemental Material not read), full-text level, read 2026-10-02. No web check was made, so I make no claim about currency or later papers.

**Data sample, range, period** [Documented, C131]
- Li, Be and B fluxes in 1.9 GV to 3.3 TV (67 bins), from 5.4 x 10^6 nuclei: 1.9 x 10^6 Li, 0.9 x 10^6 Be, 2.6 x 10^6 B.
- Collected May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016 (first 5 years), out of 8.5 x 10^10 cosmic-ray events.
- Selection (C132): downward-going track through L1, rigidity above 1.2 x the maximum geomagnetic cutoff, charge Z = 3, 4, 5 on L1, inner tracker and upper TOF.

**Background from interactions above tracker L1** [Documented, C133]
- This is estimated from simulation, for the carbon-fibre and aluminium-honeycomb support structures above L1.
- Li, Be, B: 5%, 8%, 5% at 2 GV, and 2%, 13%, 8% at 3.3 TV.
- Intermediate rigidities are not given in the main text.
- The uncertainty on the fluxes from these background corrections is < 1.5% over the whole rigidity range.
- Separate from this, the L1-L2 interaction background is < 0.5% for Li and Be and < 3% for B (template fit of the L1 charge). Charge confusion of non-interacting nuclei is negligible.

**Constants** [Documented, C135]
- Li/B = 0.72 +- 0.02 (chi2/dof = 51/53) above 7 GV.
- Be/B = 0.36 +- 0.01 (chi2/dof = 27/35) above 30 GV.
- The paper also quotes Li/Be = 2.0 +- 0.1 above 30 GV.
- Li and B have identical rigidity dependence above 7 GV, and all three fluxes above 30 GV. The 7 and 30 GV boundaries are approximate ("~") in the text.

**Explanation of the Be rigidity dependence** [Documented, C135]
- The paper attributes the different Be rigidity dependence to the most likely significant presence of the radioactive isotope 10Be (half-life 1.4 My).
- This is the paper's own interpretation ("most likely"), not a measurement. The isotope content was not measured in this paper.
