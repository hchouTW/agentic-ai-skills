**Short answer: the total systematic error on the lithium flux at 3.3 TV is not in the main text of the paper, so I cannot give you that number.** The per-bin total errors are in the Supplemental Material tables, which the skill's ledger records as not read. I will not invent or sum a figure.

The paper is the AMS Li, Be and B paper (PRL 120, 021101, 2018). The only total error quoted in the main text is "3%-4%" at 100-108 GV, and that is the total error, not just the systematic part. It does not apply at 3.3 TV. [Documented, C131]

The per-source sizes the main text gives for the high-rigidity end are below. Lithium is the reference; Be and B are described only as similar. [Documented, C133, C134]

| Source | Size at or near 3.3 TV |
|---|---|
| Rigidity resolution (MDR 3.5 TV for Li, 5% uncertainty, plus 20% uncertainty on the non-Gaussian tail amplitudes) | 8%-10% at 3.3 TV, from below 1.5% under 200 GV |
| Rigidity scale (tracker misalignment 1/30 TV^-1, from E/p of e+/e-) | 5%-7% at 3.3 TV, from below 1% up to 200 GV |
| Inelastic cross-section | below 3%-4% at 3.3 TV, from below 2%-3% up to 100 GV |
| Reconstruction and selection | below 2% over the entire rigidity range |
| Residual background from interactions above L1 | below 1.5% over the whole rigidity range |
| Trigger efficiency | below 0.5% |

Notes on how to read these.
- **Upper bounds.** Most of the entries are "<" bounds, not measured values, and the main text does not say which are specific to Li.
- **Two items that are not systematic errors:**
  - The unfolding correction for lithium is about -20% at 3.3 TV. It is a correction applied to the counts. [Documented, C134]
  - The simulated above-L1 background correction is 2% for Li at 3.3 TV. Its associated uncertainty is the below-1.5% line above. [Documented, C133]
- **Rough size.** Rigidity resolution and rigidity scale dominate at 3.3 TV. The total systematic there must therefore be well above the 3%-4% quoted at 100 GV. I will not turn that into a number. Combining the entries in quadrature would be my own proposal, because the paper gives bounds, not the values per bin, and I do not know their correlations. [General method, assumptions unverified]

To get the actual figure, take the lithium row of the Supplemental Material flux table for the last bin (the highest-rigidity bin, ending at 3.3 TV). That table lists the individual systematic and total errors per bin.
