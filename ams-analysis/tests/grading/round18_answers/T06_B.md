# Design: positron flux, 10-500 GV (AMS-02, public-level proposal)

Scope: public-domain design only. No internal cuts, passes, trigger bits, calibration constants or numeric performance are supplied; every such quantity is symbolic or [Unknown/needs input]. Labels: [Documented, claim ID], [General method], [Proposal], [Unknown/needs input].

## 0. Missing inputs that change the answer (assumptions made)
- Data vs MC, and period (assumed: ISS flight data, a stated multi-year period; MC for templates/acceptance). Public vs internal work (assumed public/proposal).
- **Variable.** "10-500 GV" is rigidity, but published AMS lepton fluxes are in energy [Documented, C25 (S04) and C26 (S05)]: the positron flux is defined in energy at the top of AMS, with the ECAL giving energy and the Tracker giving rigidity and charge sign. Decision needed: bin in reconstructed ECAL energy (the usual choice, and E ~ |R| c for |Z|=1 leptons only up to mass and radiative losses) or in rigidity. This design assumes **energy bins with a stated mapping**, quoting 10-500 GeV as the e± equivalent of 10-500 GV only after the conversion is stated; if rigidity is truly required, the response must be in rigidity and bremsstrahlung enters through the Tracker, not the ECAL (a different response matrix). Rigidity R = pc/(Ze) is not momentum or energy [General method]; for |Z|=1, p = |R|/c, and E ≈ pc only for electrons at these energies (verify with scripts/ams_kinematics.py for any exact conversion).
- Estimand: absolute differential flux Phi_e+(E), not the fraction (the fraction's cancellation of acceptance is a property of the fraction, not of the flux [Documented, C114/C115]). Interpretation (pulsar, dark matter, cutoff) is a later model step.

## 1. Estimand and flux definition
[Documented, C25] Phi_i = N_i / (A_eff,i (1+delta_i) T_i DeltaE_i), in energy at the top of AMS, with N_i the background-corrected, migration-corrected positron count. [Proposal] Use exposure = sum_t A_eff(t) T_live(t) x geomagnetic transmission, so acceptance and livetime are not applied twice (A, eps, T, delta each in exactly one category; the response either includes inefficiency or does not, never both). Symbols: N_i, eps_k|prior, A_i, T_i, delta_i, M_ij.

## 2. Relevant subsystems (separate evidence chains)
- **Tracker + magnet**: charge sign and rigidity (the only charge-sign source), |Z|=1, track quality. Not TRD/ECAL/TOF/RICH.
- **TRD**: e/p separation by transition radiation (gamma-dependent), a discriminant Lambda_TRD, never a charge-sign detector.
- **ECAL**: energy, shower shape (Lambda_ECAL), E/|p| compatibility. Shares energy with E/p and shares the track with the Tracker charge, so correlations are tracked explicitly.
- **TOF/ACC/RICH**: direction, beta, |Z|, side-entry/veto; RICH is limited for lepton ID.

## 3. Reconstruction and selection
1. Data quality: run/livetime selection, time-stability, pass consistent with MC [Unknown/needs input: good-run rules, pass names].
2. Downward-going, |Z|=1 in TOF and Tracker, single good track, fiducial in ECAL and Tracker acceptance.
3. **Geomagnetic selection**: E (or |R|) above a safety factor times the maximum Stormer cutoff for that direction/location to suppress secondary/albedo leptons [Documented, C24: E > 1.2 x maximum Stormer cutoff in S04]. At 10 GeV and above this is mostly a low-edge concern, but the safety factor and geomagnetic transmission still enter exposure at the low end [Proposal: verify with cutoff-model variation].
4. Lepton ID: energy-dependent Lambda_ECAL cut [Documented, C24], TRD-based ID, E/p compatibility. Cut values are [Unknown/needs input]; define them as a function of energy, with efficiency measured conditionally.
5. Sample split by Tracker charge sign: positive-rigidity sample (positrons + protons + charge-confused electrons) and negative sample (electrons + antiprotons + charge-confused positrons), analysed simultaneously.

## 4. Signal, backgrounds, controls (background ledger; each with origin, entry mechanism, control, tail validation)
| Background | Mechanism | Control / estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Protons identified as positrons (dominant, abundance orders of magnitude above e+) | TRD/ECAL misidentification in the positive sample, growing relative to the signal with energy | proton-rich control (reversed TRD/ECAL cuts), transfer factor validated with orthogonal cuts; tail checked in data not in Gaussian core |
| Charge-confused electrons (e- -> e+) | Tracker response tail, bad hit assignment, secondary tracks, interactions | Lambda_CC-type charge-confusion estimator [Documented, C24 (S04) uses a BDT-based Lambda_CC]; **tail** probability measured vs energy with abundant data, not extrapolated from the Gaussian resolution; at 500 GV with max detectable rigidity quoted at the TV scale [Documented, C21] charge confusion is a growing effect, so the tail is the central systematic at the high end |
| Charge-confused protons that survive ID | same Tracker tail acting on the proton sample | covered by the same Lambda_CC treatment, in the proton template |
| Secondary/albedo and photon-converted leptons, bremsstrahlung | geomagnetic/matching | cutoff safety-factor variation; ECAL/Tracker matching and E/p tails |
| Hadronic showers with large EM fraction | ECAL tail | Lambda_ECAL validation with data/MC on control |

## 5. Yield extraction (inference)
Do not start from the algorithm: estimand and response are fixed first (sections 1, 6), then the estimator.
[Documented, C24] Published practice: template fit in the 2D (Lambda_TRD, Lambda_CC) plane with three templates (correct-sign positrons, correct-sign protons, charge-confused electrons). [Proposal consistent with, not identical to, that practice]:
- Per energy bin, fit the positive and negative samples **simultaneously**, sharing the charge-confusion parameters, with constraints from the proton-rich and charge-confusion controls.
- Signal template provenance: published practice takes the positron template from electron data at low energy and from electron MC above ~100 GeV, with the charge-confusion template from MC checked against test-beam e+- and flight e- data [Documented, C24]. Here, with a 10-500 range crossing that boundary, state which of data-derived or MC-derived applies in each bin and add a template-shape nuisance for the switchover. Do not take the signal template from a region that the proton template contaminates (signal leakage into the proton template).
- Finite template statistics: full Barlow-Beeston (scripts/template_fit.py) rather than treating MC templates as exact; at high energy, where positron counts are low, check boundary and low-count behaviour: use Poisson/toy-validated intervals, no Gaussian or Wilks errors near boundaries (invariant 6), and no S/sqrt(B).
- Checks: fit goodness (not just the chi2 of one tuned fit), pulls, correlation between the proton fraction and charge-confusion fraction, and alternative proton shapes. Avoid retuning cuts on the fitted data (blind or hold out a sub-sample).

## 6. Efficiency, acceptance, response, exposure
- Efficiency chain as conditional factors, eps_total = eps_1 * eps_2|1 * eps_3|12 ..., not as independent factors (Tracker charge and E/p share the track; shower shape and E/p share ECAL energy; TRD and rigidity cuts share energy dependence). Check by applying the chain in two orders and compare the product with the truth-level combined efficiency in MC (Frechet bounds are not estimates).
- Data/MC corrections: delta from a high-statistics control; published practice derived it from the electron sample [Documented, C25]; applying an electron-derived correction to positrons is a proposal that needs charge-symmetry justification (the sign-dependent part, e.g. interactions and the magnet field direction, must be argued or varied).
- Acceptance and MC generation record: surface, solid angle, generated spectrum, species, material/geometry version, reconstruction version, reweighting without circularity (iterate or use a spectrum-independent response). Unknown: values [Unknown/needs input].
- Response: energy migration M_ij including the bremsstrahlung tail. Published practice requires bin widths at least twice the energy resolution and unfolds the small migration [Documented, C25]; [Proposal] use iterative or regularised unfolding with a stated stopping criterion and a forward-folding cross-check, with the energy-scale uncertainty treated as an uncertainty on the bin boundaries [Documented, C25]. If the response already contains the inefficiency, do not apply A and eps again.
- Bin centering prescription for a differential flux (not the arithmetic center by default).
- Time stability: livetime and acceptance variation by period; check for hardware-era changes; the positron flux is time dependent at low energy [Documented in the source index, S42/S46: temporal structures in e+- fluxes], so a time-integrated flux needs a stated integration period and a time-split check, although at E >~ 10 GeV the effect is smaller [Unknown/needs input: size].

## 7. Systematics ledger (each: category, correlated/partial/independent, size = [Unknown/needs input])
Selection-variation (vary cuts many times as a stability scan), proton/charge-confusion template shape and tail, template statistics, energy scale and resolution (bin-boundary), data/MC efficiency correction (charge symmetry), acceptance/MC (generation, material, interaction cross sections), unfolding (stability against prior/regularization), geomagnetic safety factor and cutoff model, time/period stability, livetime. Propagate with a joint covariance, not independent bin-by-bin errors; positron and electron fluxes share events and systematics, so fit or compare them jointly if both are used.

## 8. Validation and triggers (each a metric and an action, not "reasonable agreement")
- Closure: MC pseudo-data with injected positron, proton and charge-confusion fractions; recovered N_i within statistical error; trigger: bias > fraction of the statistical error => add a systematic or change the template model.
- Abundant-sample validation of the charge-confusion tail (electrons under loose cuts, vs energy), extrapolated to the tight selection with a tested assumption.
- Orthogonal chains (TRD-only vs ECAL-only ID), period and geometry splits, sign splits, alternate templates, signal injection, pull distributions and coverage with seeded toys at low counts.
- Compare with published AMS positron flux only as plausibility (no tuning to it), and quote a published number only from source-index with its range, period and selection [Documented, S04 for positrons: 1.9 million positrons up to 1 TeV, source-term cutoff reported, C04].
- Total chain validation: closure of the whole chain (efficiency x acceptance x response) per bin.

## 9. Blinding and reporting
Define cuts and fit models before examining the highest-energy bins; hold out a sub-sample; the fraction and flux interpretations (pulsar, dark matter, cutoffs) are model steps with their own background (secondary production, propagation) as nuisance parameters, no extrapolation beyond 10-500, and no "discovery" wording; report the energy-equivalent mapping and the exposure definition.
