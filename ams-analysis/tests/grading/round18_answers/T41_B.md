**Short answer:** about 830 +- 30 days (roughly 2.3 years) for the 10%-90% transition of the positron-to-electron ratio R_e = Phi(e+)/Phi(e-) across the solar polarity reversal, measured in 1-50 GeV (energy, not rigidity) over 79 Bartels rotations, May 2011 to May 2017. The duration is the same at all energies at which it is determined (about 1-21 GeV), but the midpoint of the transition is delayed at lower energy.

**Sources and how the number was obtained**

- **[Documented, C39, S42 abstract level]** The AMS electron and positron paper reports a smooth transition of the positron-to-electron ratio across the polarity reversal over 830 +- 30 days, with an energy-dependent midpoint delay. It also reports that months-scale structures appear coincidently in the e+ and e- fluxes but are not visible in the ratio. This is stated at abstract level; I did not read the body of that paper here. Re-check the numbers against the paper before quoting.
- **[Documented, C90, S01 (Phys. Rept. 894, Section 16)]** The fit behind the number is described in the review. AMS measured the e+ and e- fluxes in time (Bartels rotations) and energy, formed R_e from 3871 independent measurements, and fitted a four-parameter logistic function at each energy: R_e(t,E) = R0(E)[1 + C(E)/(exp(-(t - t_1/2(E))/(Delta t/Delta_80)) + 1)], with Delta_80 = 4.39. Delta_80 makes Delta t the 10%-90% transition time. The four parameters are R0, the amplitude C, the midpoint t_1/2 and Delta t. chi2/d.o.f. is about 1 for all fits over 1-21 GeV. Delta t is energy independent at 830 +- 30 days.
- **[Documented, C90]** Midpoint delay: t_1/2(E) - t_rev = tau (E/GeV)^rho, with t_rev = 1 July 2013, rho = -0.33 +- 0.04 (stat) (+0.08, -0.15) (syst), and tau = 580 +- 19 (stat) +- 136 (syst) days. t_1/2 shifts by 260 +- 30 days between 1 and 6 GeV. The transition amplitude C is close to 1 at 1 GeV, falls with energy, and is consistent with zero above 20 GeV.
- **[Documented, C89, S01]** The review restates the underlying lepton data: 23.5 million events, 49 energy bins in 1-50 GeV, and short-term structures that largely cancel in R_e.

**Caveats**

- The 830-day figure comes from a descriptive logistic fit chosen by the collaboration, not from a physical model. It is a 10%-90% transition time, not the full duration of the polarity reversal.
- t_rev = 1 July 2013 is a stated choice, the centre of the period without a well-defined solar polarity. The midpoint delays depend on that choice.
- Delta t and t_1/2 are only determined where C is large, at low energy. Above 6 GeV the review fixes Delta t to 830 days and uses the parametrized t_1/2. So "energy independent" is a result of the fit at low energy plus an assumption at higher energy.
- The ledger shows no trial factor and no covariance model across bins in the text read. Treat the correlated-error handling as [Unknown/needs input]. Absence from the text read is not proof AMS did not apply one.
- Do not mix this with the daily-flux papers (C40, C41), which use rigidity and different thresholds (about 8.5 GV and 7 GV).
