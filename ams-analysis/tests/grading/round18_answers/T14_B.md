**Not automatically. Treat cancellation as a hypothesis to test per energy bin, not an assumption.**

**What may cancel.** [Documented, C114] The PRL 113, 121101 positron-fraction analysis states that the acceptance is the same for positrons and electrons and cancels in the fraction. That is a property of that analysis, checked against Monte Carlo. It is not a general rule. It also does not carry over to separate fluxes: PRL 113, 121102 builds the fluxes from Phi = N / (A_eff eps_trig T DeltaE), with its own charge-confusion correction and TRD-template fit (C115, C116).

**Why the cancellation is only partial.** [General method] A ratio cancels a factor only if that factor is identical for numerator and denominator in the same bin. Here:
- **Charge-sign asymmetry.** The magnet bends e+ and e- in opposite senses. Any dependence of tracking efficiency, hit-pattern or geometric acceptance on bending direction, or on a charge-asymmetric part of the geometry or material, does not cancel. Low energy is the most exposed, and so are geomagnetic-cutoff and field-of-view effects.
- **Proton background.** Protons are a positive-sign background only. The positron sample has proton contamination and the electron sample does not, so the TRD, ECAL and E/p selections have different purities and different background corrections for the two.
- **Charge confusion.** e- reconstructed as e+ and e+ as e- is a charge-sign migration, `M(e+<-e-)` and `M(e-<-e+)`, not an efficiency. The two directions are generally not equal in effect, because the parent populations differ by a large and energy-dependent ratio. Tracker tails must be validated, since the Gaussian core is not enough.
- **Conditional efficiencies.** The TRD, ECAL-shape and E/p cuts share ECAL energy and the Tracker track, so correlations differ between the samples. Check each efficiency as a conditional factor, not as a product of marginals.
- **Variable.** The fraction is binned in energy, but the Tracker measures rigidity. Energy-scale and migration effects can differ between the numerator and denominator samples.

**Classify each nuisance** as correlated (shared, cancels), partial or independent. Do not call a residual "negligible" without a number.

**How to verify (a [Proposal]).**
1. In MC, compute eps(e+)/eps(e-) and A(e+)/A(e-) per bin, for each conditional step and for the combined chain, with closure in two selection orderings.
2. Check data against MC with a charge-symmetric control, or with e+ and e- selected by an orthogonal chain. Use abundant samples for the tails.
3. Run an orthogonal check by varying cuts, as in the published selection-systematic scan (C108, C114).
4. Fit the positive and negative samples simultaneously. They share templates and charge-confusion parameters, and the joint covariance of the fraction and the fluxes is carried.
5. Whatever residual remains after the check becomes an explicit uncertainty with a stated trigger. "Reasonable agreement" is not validation.

**Verdict.** Assume neither full cancellation nor none. Show that the e+/e- efficiency and acceptance ratio is unity within a quoted tolerance. Charge confusion and proton contamination are corrected explicitly, once, not folded into the "cancelling" efficiency. This covers public-method reasoning only. AMS internal efficiencies and cut values are not available here, and none are quoted.
