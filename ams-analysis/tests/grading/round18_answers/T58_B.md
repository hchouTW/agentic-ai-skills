**Rigidity resolution.**
- The tracker bending-coordinate resolution is 5.5 μm, in agreement with simulation [Documented, C140]. The Letter only states this number. Its derivation is in the Supplemental Material of a cited reference, which was not read.
- The maximum detectable rigidity (MDR) is 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty [Documented, C140].
- The non-Gaussian tails of the resolution function are given a 10% uncertainty on their amplitude [Documented, C140].
- The resolution is therefore treated as a Gaussian core plus tails, and both parts are varied in the systematic study. A Gaussian-only treatment would not be adequate.

**Bin-to-bin migration.**
- Migration is corrected by unfolding. The procedure is described by reference to an earlier AMS paper, not in this Letter [Documented, C140].
- Events are counted as N_i after migration correction, with Φ_i = N_i/(A_i ε_i T_i ΔR_i).
- The correction (N_i − ℵ_i)/ℵ_i, where ℵ_i is the observed count in bin i, is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, −5% at 200 GV and −6% at 3.3 TV [Documented, C140].

**Systematics, as quoted in the main text.**

| Source | Size | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| Rigidity resolution function | < 1% below 150 GV, 3% at 3.3 TV | C140 |
| Absolute rigidity scale (tracker misalignment, magnetic-field map, temperature) | < 0.6% up to 100 GV, 5% in the last bin (1.3 to 3.3 TV) | C141 |
| Inelastic cross sections (N+C and N+O) | < 3% up to 100 GV, 4% at 3 TV | C141 |

Other terms quoted in the main text [Documented, C139, C141]:
- Trigger efficiency: < 0.7%.
- Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: < 0.1% below 30 GV.
- Data/MC corrections to the effective acceptance: < 3% up to 500 GV and < 5% at 3.3 TV.
- Reconstruction and selection: < 1%.
- Background subtraction: < 1.5%.
- Total flux error: 4% at 100 GV.

**Caveats.**
- The rigidity-scale and cross-section terms are the dominant high-rigidity systematics, with the scale term reaching 5% in the last bin. Do not treat them as independent of the resolution term without checking, because the rigidity scale and the unfolding both act on the same rigidity axis.
