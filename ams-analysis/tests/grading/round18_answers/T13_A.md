Not as a measured number. 98% is 0.99 x 0.99 = 0.9801, which is valid only if A and B are independent for your population. [General method] Independence has to be shown, not assumed.

**What the two numbers allow.** With no independence assumption, the joint efficiency is only bounded (Frechet): max(0, eps_A + eps_B - 1) <= eps_AB <= min(eps_A, eps_B). Here that gives 98% <= eps_AB <= 99%. So 98% is a floor, not an estimate. The product eps_A * eps_B is the central value only if the two cuts are independent. Because the two bounds are 1 point apart, the likely loss is small, but the quoted number can be off by up to about 1% (absolute).

**Why independence is doubtful.** Selections often share information. Both may use the Tracker charge estimate. A shower-shape cut and an E/p cut share the ECAL energy. A TRD cut and a rigidity cut share the rigidity dependence. Track quality correlates with geometry and hit patterns. Correlation can push the joint efficiency above or below the product. A single efficiency is usually also not constant across rigidity, so quoting one 99% is itself questionable; the factorization must be checked per rigidity bin.

**What to do instead.**
1. Write the chain as conditional efficiencies, eps_total = eps_A * eps_B|A. Measure eps_B on the sample that already passed A, not on the full sample.
2. Compare the measured joint efficiency with the product in MC, bin by bin and in time slices (combined-efficiency closure). The difference is a factorization-bias systematic or a reason to measure the joint efficiency directly.
3. Do not stop at MC. Both numbers come from MC only, so they are not a data efficiency. Validate them with data-driven methods (tag-and-probe, or an orthogonal control sample from a different subsystem) and assign a data/MC correction and its uncertainty. The probe independence and tag bias must be evaluated.
4. Make sure no effect is corrected twice (for example in the veto, the efficiency and the response).

**Answer for a quote.** Say "eps_AB >= 98% (Frechet floor); product 98.0% assuming independence, not yet verified", or give the closure-validated joint value with its systematic. [Proposal]
