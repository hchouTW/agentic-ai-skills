**Scope.** Numbers below are the values printed in the main text of each Letter, as recorded in the skill's source-index. Each applies only at the rigidity stated. The Supplemental Material tables were not read, so no total error budget is given. Browsing was unavailable, so I make no currency claim; the index was last verified 2026-09-20.

| Item | Ne, Mg, Si (PRL 124, 211102; S56) | Li, Be, B (S54) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger efficiency | Efficiency is above 94% for all three nuclei over the whole rigidity range [Documented, C144]. The systematic error is below 1% [Documented, C146]. | Efficiency is above 98% for 3 <= Z <= 5 [Documented, C132]. The systematic error is below 0.5% [Documented, C134]. |

**Comparison.**
- Ne-Mg-Si has a lower trigger efficiency and a larger trigger systematic (below 1% against below 0.5%).
- Ne-Mg-Si has a larger inelastic cross-section systematic at low rigidity, below 3.5% against below 2-3%.
- The inelastic cross-section systematics are similar at the highest rigidities, with a slightly higher Li-Be-B value at 3.3 TV.
- The rigidity-scale systematic is the same size in both papers (below 1% up to 200-300 GV, 5-7% at the top). Ne-Mg-Si quotes a single 6% value at 3 TV.
- Do not read these as a like-for-like difference, because the rigidity ranges, the nuclei and the quoted wording differ.
- For Li-Be-B, the 5-7% range is stated across the three nuclei. Do not interpolate between quoted rigidities.

**Source of 1/30 TV^-1.**
- It is the residual tracker misalignment error on the rigidity scale.
- It is estimated by comparing the E/p ratio of electrons and positrons.
- It is not derived in the Ne-Mg-Si Letter. That Letter quotes it from Ref. [26], Berdugo et al., NIM A 869, 10 (2017) [Documented, C146].
- The Li-Be-B Letter gives the same value and the same E/p origin [Documented, C134]. The index records the Berdugo citation for the Ne-Mg-Si Letter and for the Fe Letter [Documented, C146, C153], but not for Li-Be-B.
- The rigidity-scale error also has a second contribution, from the magnetic-field map and its temperature corrections [Documented, C146].
- The value is not universal. The sulfur Letter quotes 1/34 TV^-1 [Documented, C171]. Do not carry 1/30 across papers without checking.
- I have not read the Berdugo paper here, so it is unverified beyond what the Letters say.
