**Conversion (carbon, Z = 6)** [General method]

Rigidity R (GV) and kinetic energy per nucleon T/A are related by:
- pc = |Z|·|R| = 6R (GeV)
- E = sqrt((pc)² + (mc²)²), T = E − mc², T/A = T/A_nucleons

Inputs I assumed, since you gave none: Z = 6, and A = 12 (12C, rest energy about 11.175 GeV, which is not exactly 12 × 0.9383 GeV). Elemental carbon is mostly 12C, but 13C and 14C are minor admixtures. Assuming A = 12 for the whole elemental flux is an assumption with its own uncertainty. If you have an isotope-composition model, use it; otherwise carry a systematic for the A assumption. Also state that the flux is at the top of the instrument.

Example values from `scripts/ams_kinematics.py` (Z = 6, A = 12, mc² = 11.1749 GeV). The edges are illustrative, not AMS bins:

| R (GV) | T/A (GeV/n) |
|---|---|
| 2 | 0.435 |
| 10 | 4.155 |
| 100 | 49.08 |
| 1000 | 499.07 |

At high R, T/A is approximately 0.5·R (since Z/A = 0.5). At low R, the conversion is nonlinear.

**Flux conversion (not just relabeling the axis)**
- The flux transforms with a Jacobian: Φ(T/A) = Φ(R) · dR/d(T/A).
- For binned data, convert the bin edges, and do not just relabel the x-axis. The width of each bin changes.
- The exact bin-average factors (the ratio of the R-bin width to the T/A-bin width) for my example bins are:
  - 2 to 10 GV: 2.151
  - 10 to 100 GV: 2.0034
  - 100 to 1000 GV: 2.00003
- These are close to Z/A × (1/0.5) = 2 at high R, but differ at low R (about 8% above 2 in the lowest bin).
- Use the factor for your own bin edges. I can run the script on them if you send the edges.

**Remaining choices to state in the result**
- Which variable the bins and the flux are in (top of instrument, as above).
- Resolution and migration: unfold in rigidity first, then convert. Do not convert the reconstructed rigidity bins and then unfold in T/A.
- If you also correct for the geomagnetic cutoff, apply it in rigidity (R > 1.2 × cutoff in public AMS flux analyses, [Documented, C20]), then convert.
- Not covered here: AMS carbon flux values. I did not quote any.
