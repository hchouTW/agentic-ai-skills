**Verdict: as written the flux is not correct. It has three double corrections and one structural problem.** [General method; the AMS-specific inputs are unknown/user-supplied]

**1. Livetime is counted twice.** Exposure is already the time-integrated acceptance: `Σ_t A_eff(t)·T_live(t) × geomagnetic transmission`. Multiplying "livetime x exposure" counts livetime twice, or else you mean acceptance and are mislabelling it. Use `Φ_i = (N_i - b_i) / (Exposure_i · ΔR_i)`, with a stated bin-centre prescription. Acceptance, efficiency, exposure and livetime are distinct, so say which one each factor is.

**2. Efficiency is applied twice (response matrix plus trigger efficiency).** If the response matrix already includes efficiency (and acceptance), do not divide by A or ε again.
- Check whether the trigger efficiency is inside the matrix. If it is, drop the separate factor.
- If it is not, the matrix must be defined as conditional on the trigger and selection, with the denominator stated.
- Either way, write down for each efficiency its numerator, its conditional denominator and its source.
- Also check whether the trigger efficiency is correlated with the other selections. Do not multiply marginal efficiencies. Run a combined-efficiency closure in MC and an ordering test.

**3. The TRD gain drift is corrected twice (run veto plus efficiency).** The same effect is handled in two categories, which is double counting. Pick one:
- Veto the bad runs. The lost livetime then goes into the exposure, and the residual drift becomes a systematic (period-split or drift).
- Or keep the runs and correct the drift through calibration or the TRD template or efficiency, with a time-dependent efficiency.
- Do not do both. If you veto, the efficiency measured on the vetoed sample must not also include the drift loss.
- Also check that the veto is not counted as dead time and as an efficiency loss.

**4. Structural: the response has to be applied through inference.** `(N - b)/...` is a bin-by-bin correction. If a response matrix with migration exists, the estimate is an unfolding or forward-folding fit: `N_i = Σ_j R_ij·μ_j + b_i`.
- Do not divide the matrix-corrected counts by a second efficiency.
- Bin-by-bin division is only justified if migration is shown to be negligible.
- The unfolding prior must not be circular with the measured spectrum.

**Also check:**
- The estimator, control region, signal contamination and uncertainty of `b`.
- Poisson or exact intervals at low counts.
- Systematics propagated through the response, not quadratically combined with unknown correlations.

Record `double_counting_checks` for the response-versus-trigger and veto-versus-efficiency pairs.

**Inputs that would change the verdict:** the exact definition of the response matrix (is the trigger included?), what "exposure" contains, the veto definition, and the data versus MC scope.
