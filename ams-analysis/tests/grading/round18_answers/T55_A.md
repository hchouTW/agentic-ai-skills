Source: AMS Ne, Mg, Si paper, PRL 124, 211102 (2020), S56. Main article only; the Supplemental Material was not read. Index last verified 2026-09-20, and I had no web access to check for newer papers.

**Sample, range and period** [Documented, C143]
- Sample: 1.8 x 10^6 Ne, 2.2 x 10^6 Mg and 1.6 x 10^6 Si nuclei, counted after background subtraction.
- Rigidity range: 2.15 GV to 3.0 TV, in 66 bins with the same widths for all three nuclei.
- Period: the first 7 years of ISS operation, May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2018. These come from 120 x 10^9 cosmic-ray events.
- The total flux error is about 5% at 100 GV for each nucleus.

**Charge purities** [Documented, C145]
- Purity is above 98% for Ne and Mg and above 99.7% for Si.
- The background from interactions between L1 and L2 is below 0.3% for all three, from a fit to the L1 charge distribution with Na, Mg, Al and Si templates.
- The background from interactions above L1 is estimated from simulation. It is below 2% for Ne, below 1.5% for Mg and negligible for Si.
- The overall uncertainty from background subtraction is below 0.5%.

**Fitted breaks** [Documented, C147]
- The fit form is ratio = k (R/R0)^Delta for R <= R0 and ratio = k for R > R0.
- Ne/Mg: k = 0.84 +- 0.02, R0 = 3.65 +- 0.5 GV, Delta = 0.19 +- 0.08, chi2/d.o.f. = 42/64 over the full range.
- Si/Mg: k = 0.89 +- 0.02, R0 = 86.5 +- 13 GV, Delta = 0.069 +- 0.005, chi2/d.o.f. = 29/53, fitted above 6 GV only.
- The paper concludes that Ne and Mg have identical rigidity dependence above 3.65 GV, and that all three fluxes are identical above 86.5 GV.
- The Ne/Mg break lies near the low edge of the range, so it is weakly constraining. The two R0 values are not directly comparable, because the Si/Mg fit does not use the low-rigidity data.

**Is Ne-Mg-Si a different class from He-C-O?** The paper states this conclusion, and it is not a model-independent proof. The basis is the following [Documented, C148].
- The Ne/O, Mg/O and Si/O ratios are fitted above 20 GV with a double power law whose break is fixed at 86.5 GV.
- Above 86.5 GV the fitted changes in slope are delta = -0.046 +- 0.010 (Ne/O), -0.049 +- 0.011 (Mg/O) and -0.040 +- 0.011 (Si/O).
- Their average is -0.045 +- 0.008, which is more than 5 sigma from zero.
- The paper contrasts this with He, C and O, which have identical rigidity dependence above 60 GV. That He/C/O result comes from the earlier He, C, O paper (S11) and is not new in this one.
- So the evidence is a statistical difference in rigidity dependence relative to O. "Class" is the paper's interpretation, and the paper does not identify the origin or the propagation physics behind it.
- Neon, magnesium and silicon also have secondary components, so the two groups are not a clean primary/secondary split. The later sulfur paper (S60) extends the Ne-Mg-Si class to include S, and the iron paper (S57) places Fe with He-C-O. Both are separate claims from this paper.
