**1. Antiproton hysteresis significance, every bin below 11 GV [Documented, C178; also C123]**

Table SA of the Supplemental Material gives six rigidity bins. In each bin the method picks two intervals of 13 Bartels rotations with the same normalized proton flux, one before and one after 2014-2015. The intervals are chosen to give the most significant difference in the normalized antiproton flux. For 1.00-2.97 GV these are May 2012 to May 2013 (A) and February 2015 to February 2016 (B). The table gives the ratio Phi_pbar^A/Phi_pbar^B and the significance sigma_hys of its difference from unity:

| Rigidity bin (GV) | Phi_pbar^A / Phi_pbar^B | sigma_hys |
|---|---|---|
| 1.00-2.97 | 1.22 +- 0.04 | 5.5 |
| 2.97-4.88 | 1.14 +- 0.02 | 5.8 |
| 4.88-5.90 | 1.12 +- 0.02 | 6.9 |
| 5.90-7.09 | 1.07 +- 0.02 | 4.0 |
| 7.09-8.48 | 1.10 +- 0.02 | 5.6 |
| 8.48-11.0 | 1.06 +- 0.01 | 4.2 |

Caveats:
- The significance method is only cited from an earlier AMS paper.
- The ledger finds nothing in the text about how errors enter, how the correlation between the chosen intervals is treated, or how the "most significant pair" selection (a look-elsewhere effect) is handled. Treat these as the collaboration's quoted numbers, not as validated global significances.
- The ratio is not monotonic in the table (1.07 at 5.90-7.09 GV, then 1.10 at 7.09-8.48 GV), although the text says it decreases with rigidity.
- The text says the antiproton ratio differs from unity by more than 5 sigma at 1.00-2.97 GV, falling to 4 sigma at 8.48-11.0 GV. The table shows 5.5 and 4.2 for those two bins.
- Related result: the antiproton-electron ratio difference is sigma_diff = 7.4, 4.4, 1.9, 2.8, 0.5 and 1.1 in the same six bins. The text reads this as more than 4 sigma below 4.88 GV, i.e. a charge-sign effect.

**3. Antiproton-electron slope k at 1.00-1.92 GV [Documented, C179]**

k(e-, pbar) = 1.78 +- 0.12 at 1.00-1.92 GV. It is defined by V_e- = k(e-, pbar) V_pbar (Eq. S1). Here V = (Phi - <Phi>)/<Phi> is the relative variation of each flux per Bartels rotation, and <Phi> is the 11-year time-averaged flux. The fit is done per rigidity bin.

- k is greater than unity with significance over 4 sigma below 7.09 GV (Fig. S7 caption). The antiproton flux is therefore modulated less than the electron flux.
- k decreases with rigidity to 1.31 +- 0.08 at 5.90-7.09 GV.
- The relation is compatible with linear in the six bins from 1.00 to 7.09 GV that are shown.
- The per-bin fit errors and chi2/d.o.f. are in Fig. S6 and were not read.
- The spectral-shape reading of the result is the collaboration's interpretation, not a transport-model result.
