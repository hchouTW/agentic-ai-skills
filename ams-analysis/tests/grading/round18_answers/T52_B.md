**Neutron monitors, proton and helium time-structure supplement** [Documented, C181; S41, PRL 121, 051101, Supplemental Material Fig. SM 2 caption; full-text level, caption only]: AMS compares the relative variation of its proton integral flux, integrated above a minimum rigidity (the figure uses 2.40, 3.64, 6.47, 10.10 and 21.10 GV), with the relative variation of the rate of the Oulu (Finland) neutron monitor. Both are normalized to their averages over May 2011 to May 2017. The AMS error bars are the quadratic sum of statistical and time-dependent systematic errors. The caption states that the neutron monitor's time-dependent variation matches the AMS proton flux only when the flux is integrated over rigidities greater than 6.47 GV. This is the caption's statement for this one monitor and these thresholds. It is not a general statement about neutron monitors, and I give no values read from the plotted points.

**Duration of the positron-to-electron ratio change, electron and positron time-structure supplement** [Documented, C182; S42, PRL 121, 051102, Supplemental Material captions; full-text level, captions only]: the caption says the duration of the transition, from 10% to 90% of the change in magnitude, is independent of energy at 830 +- 30 days. The positron-to-electron ratio Re was fitted with the logistic parameterization, for several energies, across the solar polarity reversal. The midpoint of the transition depends on energy, and the reversal time t_rev is the authors' choice. The same 830 +- 30 days also appears in the main abstract (C39, S42) and in the Phys. Rept. review (C90, S01).
