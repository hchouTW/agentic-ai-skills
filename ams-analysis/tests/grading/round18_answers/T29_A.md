# Measurement brief: helium-4 flux, 2 GV to 3 TV, public AMS data

Labels: [Documented, Cn] = public AMS source claim; [General method]; [Proposal]; [Unknown/needs input]. No selection cuts, efficiencies, acceptances, livetimes or systematic sizes are invented; internal AMS inputs are not available here.

## 0. Key flag: "helium-4" over 2 GV-3 TV is not what AMS has measured
- The published AMS isotope result separates 4He from 3He only over 2.1-21 GV (100 million 4He; 18 million 3He, May 2011-Nov 2017) [Documented, C09, C86]. Above ~21 GV the velocity measurement no longer separates the isotopes in the public analysis.
- The published helium flux covers 1.9 GV-3 TV and is an elemental (|Z| = 2) flux, 3He + 4He together [Documented, S07 range; C81 (review, 125M He, 1.9 GV-3 TV); C62 (five-year paper, 67 bins 2.2 GV-3.0 TV)].
- So the estimand must be chosen: (a) elemental He flux 2 GV-3 TV (measurable; recommended primary), (b) 4He-specific flux only 2.1-21 GV (needs the isotope fit), or (c) "4He flux to 3 TV", which is the elemental flux times an assumed 4He fraction. Option (c) is a [Proposal] with the assumption stated; it is not a measurement. The 3He/4He ratio above 4 GV follows C(R/4 GV)^Delta, Delta = -0.294 +/- 0.004 [Documented, C86]; extrapolating it to TV is an unvalidated extension. The AMS He flux treats the 3He/4He composition as a systematic (0.3%, ratio varied 0 to 0.2) [Documented, C60]; that is a bound for that analysis, not a measurement of the 4He fraction at TV rigidities.

## 1. Estimand
Differential flux Phi(R) of helium (|Z| = 2; A = 4 assumed for any kinetic-energy conversion, with 3He as a stated uncertainty) in m^-2 sr^-1 s^-1 GV^-1, top of the instrument (no solar-modulation correction), vs absolute rigidity R = pc/(|Z|e) in GV, true-rigidity bins, 2 GV to 3 TV. Target: flux. Level: flux. Charge sign: positive (He nuclei; negative-rigidity Z = -2 would be antihelium, a separate search).

## 2. Variables and conversion [General method]
p = |Z| R/c with |Z| = 2; E, T and T/A need A and mass (4He 3.7274 GeV; 3He 2.8084 GeV). Quote R-based bins; if T/A is wanted, convert with the Jacobian and exact bin edges using scripts/ams_kinematics.py, not by relabeling. Variable and bin edges: [Unknown/needs input] (the published papers use their own bins, e.g. 67 bins in C62; adopt them or choose bins with width >= the resolution core plus tails).

## 3. Data scope [Unknown/needs input]
Public AMS data only means published tables/figures in the papers (no event-level data are public here). Period: first 30 months (S07) or five years (S11, C62) or longer; hardware era and the track configuration must be stated. The user must say whether this is a re-derivation exercise on user-held data, a reproduction/forward-fold of published results, or a design for an internal analysis.

## 4. Subsystem roles
Tracker: rigidity and |Z|; sign. TOF: direction, beta, |Z|, trigger. ACC: side-entry veto. RICH: |Z| and beta, essential for the 3He/4He mass separation (< ~21 GV) but not for the elemental flux. TRD: |Z| support. ECAL: not a standard nucleus charge estimator; used for the rigidity-scale check via e+- E/p [Documented, C64].

## 5. Selection (ledger, each row with a conditional denominator; values not given here)
Downward-going, tracker track, |Z| = 2 consistent across L1, upper TOF, inner tracker, lower TOF (interaction rejection), normal conditions, outside SAA, rigidity above a safety factor times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff in the field of view. Documented practice, not a rule: S07/C59 (L1-L9 track, 1.2 times IGRF cutoff, 40 degrees zenith), cutoff factor scanned 1.0-1.4 as a systematic [Documented, C59, C60]. Other AMS helium analyses use different choices (daily helium: L1 only, data-derived cutoff, C54), so there is no single AMS standard. Cut values: [Unknown/needs input].

## 6. Backgrounds and controls
Wrong-|Z| charge migration from p (published below 1e-4 in S07) and from heavier nuclei interacting at the top of the instrument (below 1e-3 in S07) [Documented, C59; scope: first 30 months]; fragmentation/interaction in material; albedo and sub-cutoff secondaries at low R; at high R, charge-sign confusion from tracker resolution tails. Each needs an estimator, a control region (data-driven where possible) and a closure test. Tails must be validated independently of the Gaussian core [invariant 4].

## 7. Efficiency, acceptance, exposure, livetime, response (kept distinct)
N_i = sum_j R_ij A_j eps_j T_j Phi_j dR_j + b_i. Trigger efficiency (prescaled-sample limited at high R), selection efficiencies as conditional factors (not multiplied as independent), acceptance from MC with data/MC correction, exposure including livetime and geomagnetic transmission per rigidity (do not double count livetime inside acceptance), survival against inelastic interactions (measured in flight with horizontal-pointing data and Glauber-Gribov scaling in the published helium work [Documented, C60]). Response matrix with tails: the published helium resolution is a Gaussian core plus non-Gaussian tails with MDR 3.2 +/- 0.16 TV [Documented, C61, C62, C63]. Because 3 TV is at/near the MDR, the top bins are migration-dominated: the diagonal estimator is invalid there; unfolding or forward folding is required. The published migration correction magnitude is analysis-specific (C62).

## 8. Inference
Unfold (or forward-fold) the raw counts with the response; compare two unfolding procedures as a systematic [Documented, C60]. Isotope option (b): template fit in velocity-vs-rigidity with finite-template (Barlow-Beeston) treatment [General method; analysis structure documented in C28 for S12]. High-R bins with few events: exact Poisson/toy-validated intervals, no Gaussian or S/sqrt(B) [invariant 6].

## 9. Systematics (registry; one row per effect, each with an applied_via)
Trigger; cutoff factor; acceptance; inelastic cross sections; 3He/4He composition (elemental flux); rigidity resolution (core and tail amplitude); unfolding; absolute rigidity scale (largest at the highest energies) [Documented, C60, C62, C64]. Sizes: quote only from the cited papers with their scope; otherwise symbolic. Propagate through migration or nuisance parameters (resolution and scale effects are never a final-value scaling); combine independent sources in quadrature and keep bin-to-bin correlations in a covariance [Documented, C33 for the quadrature practice].

## 10. Validation (metric, threshold, action) [Proposal]
MC closure of an injected spectrum (pull and chi2; add an uncertainty or change the method if bias exceeds a pre-stated fraction of the total error); time-period and detector-region stability; alternative track configurations (L1-L8 vs L2-L9 rigidity comparison for high R); loose/tight charge cuts; comparison with published S07/C62 fluxes is plausibility only, not proof. Thresholds: [Unknown/needs input].
