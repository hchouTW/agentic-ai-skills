**Nitrogen paper** (AMS, PRL 121, 051103, 2018; source S55, main article only, Supplemental Material not read; verification level full-text main article, read 2026-10-02)

**Sample, range, period** [Documented, C137]: 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events (after background subtraction), rigidity 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV in 66 bins, from the first five years on the ISS, May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016 (during which AMS collected 8.5 x 10^10 cosmic-ray events). The total flux error is 4% at 100 GV. Do not confuse this with the Phys. Rept. review's 3.9 million nitrogen nuclei (C91, a different source, S01); the 2.2 x 10^6 figure is the Letter's own.

**Two-component fit** [Documented, C142]: the nitrogen flux is fitted over the whole rigidity range as a weighted sum of a primary reference (oxygen) and a secondary reference (boron):

Phi_N = Phi_PN + Phi_SN, with Phi_PN = (0.090 +- 0.002) x Phi_O and Phi_SN = (0.62 +- 0.02) x Phi_B, chi2/d.o.f. = 51/64.

The same fit above 60 GV gives (0.083 +- 0.005) x Phi_O + (0.66 +- 0.04) x Phi_B, chi2/d.o.f. = 18/25. The secondary share falls from about 70% at a few GV to below 30% above 1 TV. The paper uses the fit to infer the N/O abundance ratio at the source without Galactic propagation; that is the paper's own statement, not an independent measurement.

**What comes from the nitrogen paper itself and what does not**
- From the nitrogen paper: the nitrogen flux (events, range, period above), the two fitted coefficients 0.090 +- 0.002 and 0.62 +- 0.02 with their chi2/d.o.f., the above-60 GV variant, the secondary-share trend, and the nitrogen spectral index (C142).
- Not from the nitrogen paper: the O flux and the B flux used as the two fit shapes. These are the published fluxes from earlier AMS papers (Refs. [8,9] of the Letter: the He, C, O paper and the Li, Be, B paper); the same applies to the He, C, O and Li, Be, B spectral indices used for comparison. The nitrogen paper contributes only the nitrogen flux and the two fitted coefficients.
- Caveat [General method]: the fit is a descriptive two-component form. It assumes O has a purely primary shape and B a purely secondary shape, so the coefficients are conditional on that choice, and a good chi2 does not test it; it is not proof of a mechanism.
- The Phys. Rept. review quotes slightly different numbers (0.092 +- 0.002 and 0.61 +- 0.02, chi2/d.o.f. = 59/64, 3.9 million events; C91). Those are the review's values for its own, overlapping analysis; do not mix them with the Letter's values.
