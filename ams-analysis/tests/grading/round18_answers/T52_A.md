**Neutron monitors, proton/helium time-structure supplement** [Documented, C181; S41, PRL 121, 051101, Supplemental Material Fig. SM 2 caption, read at caption level]: the supplement compares the relative variation of the AMS proton integral flux, integrated above a minimum rigidity (the figure uses 2.40, 3.64, 6.47, 10.10 and 21.10 GV), with the relative variation of the rate of the Oulu (Finland) neutron monitor, both normalized to their averages from May 2011 to May 2017. The AMS error bars are the quadratic sum of the statistical and time-dependent systematic errors. The caption states that the monitor's relative time variation matches the AMS proton flux only when the flux is integrated over rigidities above 6.47 GV. Caveats: this is a caption statement for this one monitor and these minimum rigidities. The plotted points were not read, so I quote no values from them. It is not a general statement about neutron monitors or their energy response.

**Duration, electron/positron time-structure supplement** [Documented, C182; S42, PRL 121, 051102, Supplemental Material Fig. SM 1-SM 7 captions]: the positron-to-electron ratio Re, fitted with a logistic parameterization, has a 10%-90% transition duration across the solar polarity reversal that is independent of energy, at **830 +- 30 days**. The same number appears in the S42 abstract (C39, electrons and positrons, 1-50 GeV in energy, May 2011 to May 2017). The review's logistic fit (C90, S01) reports the same 830 +- 30 days. The effective reversal time t_rev is the authors' choice, and the midpoint t_1/2 of the transition depends on energy. That energy-dependent midpoint delay is separate from the duration.
