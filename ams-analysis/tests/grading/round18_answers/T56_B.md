**Scope.** Source: PRL 134, 051002 (S09), Supplemental Material text, read per the ledger (claims C175, C177). The paper's Supplemental Material text also contains the details below.

**Trigger efficiency [Documented, C175].** The antiproton trigger efficiency is 80% above 10 GV, increasing to 83% at 1 GV. This is not the trigger-efficiency systematic. That systematic is below 1% over the whole rigidity range (C121). It is also not the trigger efficiency of other AMS papers.

**Signal template definition and why [Documented, C177].**
- Only the negative-rigidity sample is used. It contains antiprotons, electrons, pi- (and negligible K-) made in interactions in the detector material, and charge-confusion protons.
- Signal and background yields are found per rigidity bin by fitting signal and background templates to data with floating normalizations.
- The signal template is always defined from the high-statistics proton data sample. The reason given is that the template variables are distributed the same for antiprotons and protons when both are reconstructed with the correct charge sign. This was verified with Monte Carlo and with antiproton and proton data.
- Charge-confusion protons are reduced to a negligible level (below 0.1%) in all bins by a charge-confusion estimator. That is a bound after the estimator cut, not a rate before it.

**Templates below and above 2.97 GV [Documented, C177].** Two rigidity regions use different templates.
- 1.00-2.97 GV:
  - The fit variable is the mass distribution computed from the inner-tracker rigidity and the TOF velocity.
  - A TRD-estimator cut is applied.
  - Electron and pion templates are defined per rigidity bin from data selected with the TRD, RICH and ECAL.
- 2.97-41.9 GV:
  - The fit variables are the TRD estimator and the RICH velocity.
  - The pi- background is removed by a rigidity-dependent RICH-velocity cut. Mesons are present only below 10 GV.
  - The electron template is defined per bin from data selected with the ECAL. Monte Carlo is used to verify that the electron template shapes inside and outside the ECAL acceptance are identical.
- In total 1.1e6 antiprotons are identified from 1.00 to 41.9 GV.
