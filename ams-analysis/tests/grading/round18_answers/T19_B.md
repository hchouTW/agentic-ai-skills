**TRD proton rejection at 100 GeV**

There is no single "TRD rejection factor at 100 GeV". A rejection factor only means something together with the e± efficiency, the energy range and the selection it was measured with.

- [Documented, C67] The AMS review (a review-level source, not a dedicated TRD performance table) states that the TRD proton rejection measured on orbit at 90% e± efficiency is above 1000 over 2-200 GeV/c, and that it improves when the e± efficiency is tightened to 65%.
- 100 GeV/c lies inside that range, so "above 1000 at 90% e± efficiency" is the supportable statement. It is a lower bound.
- I will not give a point value at 100 GeV, a number at another efficiency, or a rejection curve. The 65% improvement is not quantified in the source I can cite, and quoting a value would be inventing it.
- In practice the TRD estimator (Lambda_TRD, a likelihood ratio of the e± to proton hypothesis over the 20 layers) is not used as a hard cut alone. It is combined with ECAL E/p and fitted as templates. Quoting the TRD number as the total e/p separation would overstate or misstate the analysis.
