The ACC (Anti-Coincidence Counters) is AMS-02's veto system. [Documented, C13, C69]

- **Layout:** sixteen curved scintillator panels, 800 mm long, inside the magnet bore around the Tracker. Wavelength-shifting fibres carry the light to PMTs, each PMT serving two adjacent panels. Tongue-and-groove vertical edges mean a particle crossing a seam is seen in two panels. [Documented, C69]
- **Purpose:** reject particles that enter or leave the Tracker volume sideways, outside the nominal acceptance. [Documented, C69]
- **Trigger role:** for Z = 1, the level-1 trigger requires four TOF signals and no ACC signal. [Documented, C69]
- **Efficiency:** side-entry rejection of 0.99999. This is a pre-flight long-duration test result quoted in the analysis papers and the review. The sources do not give its per-condition validity. [Documented, C69, C21]

Limits [General method]:
- The ACC is a veto only. It does not measure charge sign, |Z|, β, rigidity or energy.
- Its veto inefficiency is a conditional efficiency. It should be measured, not folded into geometric acceptance a second time.
- At high energy, backsplash from ECAL showers or accidental activity can veto good events. That makes the inefficiency energy- and time-dependent.
