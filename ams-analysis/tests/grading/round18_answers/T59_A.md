**Scope.** Both papers are PRLs, read at main-article level only; Supplemental Material was not read, so the numbers below are only the sizes quoted in the main text. Offline claim-ledger check; no current-source verification (index last verified 2026-09-20). Each size is a per-source bound, not a total systematic.

| Item | Ne, Mg, Si (PRL 124, 211102; S56) | Li, Be, B (PRL 120, 021101; S54) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger efficiency | < 1% over the whole rigidity range [Documented, C146] | < 0.5% [Documented, C134] |
| Inelastic cross-section | < 3.5% up to 100 GV, < 4% from 100 GV to 3 TV [Documented, C146] | < 2%-3% up to 100 GV, < 3%-4% at 3.3 TV [Documented, C134] |
| Rigidity scale | < 1% up to 300 GV, 6% at 3 TV [Documented, C146]. This is the total absolute scale error, which also includes the magnetic-field-map and temperature contribution | < 1% up to 200 GV, rising to 5%-7% at 3.3 TV [Documented, C134] |

- **Trigger efficiency.** The Li, Be, B bound is tighter (< 0.5% against < 1%).
- **Inelastic cross-section.** The Li, Be, B bounds are lower below 100 GV (< 2%-3% against < 3.5%). At the highest rigidities the two are similar (< 3%-4% against < 4%). The Ne, Mg, Si value is a single quoted bound for all three nuclei.
- **Rigidity scale.** The Li, Be, B bound is looser below 200-300 GV (< 1% in both) and about the same size at the top. The two papers do not quote their scale error at the same endpoints, 3 TV against 3.3 TV, and the Ne, Mg, Si range in the claim ledger is only to 3 TV. The Li, Be, B 5%-7% is a range over its three nuclei. These are not like-for-like numbers.
- **Not comparable one-to-one.** The papers' rigidity ranges, 2.15 GV-3.0 TV for Ne, Mg, Si [C143] and 1.9 GV-3.3 TV for Li, Be, B [C83], differ. They also differ in nuclei and in material crossed, and the inelastic bounds are quoted at different rigidity points. No combined total can be formed from these per-source bounds.

**Where 1/30 TV^-1 comes from.** It is the residual tracker misalignment, expressed as an error on 1/R. It is estimated by comparing the E/p ratio for electrons and positrons, where E is the ECAL energy and p the tracker momentum. The Ne, Mg, Si paper does not measure it itself. It quotes it from an earlier reference, Berdugo et al., NIM A 869, 10 (2017) [Documented, C146]. That paper's own claim says "1/30 TV^-1 is a cited value, not measured in this Letter". The Li, Be, B paper states the same value, "tracker misalignment 1/30 TV^-1 from E/p of e+/e-", without a reference in the ledger claim [Documented, C134]. I did not read the NIM A paper, and it has no ledger entry, so what it contains is [Unknown/needs input]. The sulfur paper uses 1/34 TV^-1 [Documented, C171], so the figure is not one fixed constant across papers. The same 1/30 value is also quoted in the iron and fluorine papers.
