Source: AMS Li, Be, B paper, PRL 120, 021101 (2018) (S54). The ledger holds the main article only (full text); the Supplemental Material was not read. I could not check for later or superseding papers (no web access; the index was last verified 2026-09-20).

**Data sample and period** [Documented, C131]
- 5.4 x 10^6 nuclei in total: 1.9 x 10^6 Li, 0.9 x 10^6 Be and 2.6 x 10^6 B.
- Collected by AMS on the ISS during the first 5 years, May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016.
- These nuclei were selected from 8.5 x 10^10 cosmic-ray events collected in that time.
- Rigidity range: 1.9 GV to 3.3 TV, in 67 bins.
- The total error on each flux is 3%-4% in the 100-108 GV bin.

**Background corrections** [Documented, C133]
- Interactions above L1 (carbon-fiber and aluminum-honeycomb support structures) were estimated from simulation.
  - The correction is 5%, 8% and 5% at 2 GV for Li, Be and B respectively.
  - It is 2%, 13% and 8% at 3.3 TV for Li, Be and B respectively.
  - The main text quotes only these two rigidity points, not intermediate rigidities.
- Interactions between L1 and L2 (TRD and upper TOF) were evaluated by fitting the tracker-L1 charge distribution with Li, Be, B and C templates.
  - This background is below 0.5% for Li and Be, and below 3% for B.
- Charge confusion from non-interacting nuclei is negligible.
- The uncertainty on the fluxes from these background corrections is below 1.5% over the whole rigidity range.

**Flux ratios** [Documented, C135]
- Li/B = 0.72 +- 0.02 above 7 GV (chi2/d.o.f. = 51/53), from a fit to a constant.
- Be/B = 0.36 +- 0.01 above 30 GV (chi2/d.o.f. = 27/35), from a fit to a constant.

**Why the Be flux has a different rigidity dependence** [Documented, C135]
- The Li and B fluxes have an identical rigidity dependence above about 7 GV. All three fluxes have an identical rigidity dependence above about 30 GV.
- The paper attributes the different Be rigidity dependence (below 30 GV) as "most likely" due to a significant presence of the radioactive isotope 10Be (half-life 1.4 My).
- This is the paper's interpretation, not a measurement. The ledger notes that AMS's own isotope measurements concern Li and He, not Be.
