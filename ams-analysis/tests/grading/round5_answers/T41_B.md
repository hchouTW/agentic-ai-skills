**[Documented, claim C90, source S01 — AMS Collaboration, "AMS on the ISS, Part II: Results from the first seven years," Phys. Rept. 894, 1 (2021), full text]**

AMS reports a transition time of **Δt = 830 ± 30 days** (about 2.3 years) for the positron-to-electron ratio `R_e = Φ(e⁺)/Φ(e⁻)` to cross the 2013 heliospheric magnetic-polarity reversal, and states this value as energy-independent over the range it was determined.

**How AMS got that number.** The collaboration fit all 3871 independent `R_e(t, E)` measurements (in time and energy, 1–21 GeV) simultaneously with a four-parameter logistic function per energy bin:

`R_e(t,E) = R0(E) · [1 + C(E) / (exp(−(t − t_1/2(E)) / (Δt/Δ_80)) + 1)]`

with `Δ_80 = 4.39` chosen so that `Δt` is precisely the **10%–90% transition time** of the logistic curve, and four free parameters per energy bin (`R0`, `C`, `t_1/2`, `Δt`). The fits give χ²/d.o.f. ≈ 1 across the fitted energy range, and `Δt` comes out consistent with being independent of energy at 830 ± 30 days. The midpoint of the transition, `t_1/2(E)`, is separately found to depend on energy relative to a reference time `t_rev` = 1 July 2013 — the review's chosen center of the period without a well-defined solar polarity — via `t_1/2(E) − t_rev = τ (E/GeV)^ρ`, with `ρ = −0.33 ± 0.04 (stat) ⁺⁰·⁰⁸₋₀.₁₅ (syst)` and `τ = 580 ± 19 (stat) ± 136 (syst)` days.

**Caveats.** This is a descriptive fit chosen by the collaboration, not a derived physical model. `Δt` and `t_1/2` are well constrained mainly at low energy, where the transition amplitude `C` is large (`C` is close to 1 at 1 GeV and consistent with zero above ~20 GeV); above about 6 GeV the review instead fixes `Δt` to the 830-day value and solves only for `t_1/2` via the power-law relation. `t_rev` itself is a stated choice tied to an external solar-physics reference, not an AMS measurement, so the quoted midpoint delays are relative to that choice. No trial-factor or cross-bin correlated-error treatment for this fit is described in the text read, so treat the fit's error propagation across energy bins as not fully specified beyond the quoted stat/syst splits.
