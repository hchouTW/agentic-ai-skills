**[Documented]** The one public, context-attached TRD proton-rejection figure is: **proton rejection above 1000 at 90% e± selection efficiency, over 2–200 GeV/c** (Phys. Rept. 894 review, Section 1.3; TRD estimator Λ_TRD, the per-layer e±-vs-proton log-likelihood ratio over 20 layers). 100 GeV falls inside that 2–200 GeV/c range, so this is the documented number to anchor a 100 GeV assumption to. The same source notes rejection improves further if you tighten to 65% e± efficiency, but no number is given for that operating point.

A few caveats before you use it as an assumption in an analysis:

- **This is TRD alone**, at one specific efficiency working point, in a review-level summary — not a universal constant. AMS's actual published positron flux analysis (PRL 122, 041102, 2019) doesn't use a serial TRD cut at all; it does a 2D template fit in (Λ_TRD, Λ_CC) where Λ_CC is a charge-confusion (not proton-rejection) discriminant, after an energy-dependent ECAL preselection. So "TRD rejection ×1000" is not simply multiplied into that analysis's residual proton background — the two are different analysis strategies, and you shouldn't chain this review figure with a template-fit result as if they compose.
- **ECAL adds separate rejection**, stated as "independent of the TRD rejection" in the review: combining Λ_ECAL with E/p > 0.7 gives roughly an additional factor of ~3 in proton rejection when tightening from 90% to 65% e± efficiency. No absolute ECAL-alone rejection number is given, only that incremental factor.
- Any number you quote should carry its efficiency and momentum range — a "proton rejection" without those is not a usable AMS performance claim.

**Recommendation for a 100 GeV positron analysis:** as a starting assumption you can use TRD-alone rejection >1000 at 90% e± efficiency [Documented, this is the closest sourced value for 100 GeV], but design the actual selection/template fit around the real discriminant shapes (Λ_TRD, Λ_ECAL, Λ_CC) rather than treating 1000 as a hard number to plug into a background estimate — validate the achieved rejection on your own control sample and its non-Gaussian tail, since charge/species misidentification is a tail phenomenon, not something the Gaussian core of any one estimator's distribution can certify.
