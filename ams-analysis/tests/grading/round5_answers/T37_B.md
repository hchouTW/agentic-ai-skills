**[Documented, claim C84, source S01 — AMS Collaboration, "AMS on the ISS, Part II: Results from the first seven years," Phys. Rept. 894, 1 (2021), full text]**

The hardening AMS reports is for the secondary-to-primary ratios formed from the light secondary nuclei (Li, Be, B) over the light primaries (C, O): Li/C, Be/C, B/C, Li/O, Be/O, B/O. A single power law `Φ_secondary/Φ_primary ∝ R^Δ` was fit to each ratio in two non-overlapping rigidity intervals, 60.3–192 GV and 192–3300 GV. Above **192 GV** (the review's stated boundary — not a round "200 GV," so if you need the exact reported threshold use 192 GV) the spectral index hardens on average by

`Δ[192–3300 GV] − Δ[60.3–192 GV] = 0.140 ± 0.025`

which the review states as exceeding **5σ** significance for the average hardening across the six ratios.

Caveats worth carrying into any downstream use of this number:
- This is a review-level (Phys. Rept.) summary and fit, not the original standalone measurement paper for any one ratio (e.g., the earlier B/C paper, PRL 117, 231102, reports a different quantity — index ≈ −0.333 above 65 GV — from an earlier, smaller dataset; don't merge the two).
- How the quoted 5σ was computed (correlations across the six ratios, any look-elsewhere/trial correction across interval choices) is not stated in the text read here, so treat the significance construction itself as [Unknown/needs input] beyond the quoted number — take "5σ" as the collaboration's stated figure, not as independently re-derived.
- The two interval boundaries (60.3, 192, 3300 GV) are the review's analysis choice, not a general prescription.
- The review interprets the hardening as consistent with a Galactic-propagation origin (versus source-injection hardening, which would affect primaries and secondaries similarly) — that interpretation is a modeling statement, separate from the measurement itself.
