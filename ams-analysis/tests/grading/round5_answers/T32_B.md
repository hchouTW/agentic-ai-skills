# Design: 27-day-binned e⁺/e⁻ ratio, 5–50 GV, searched for a 1-year periodicity

## 1. Estimand

Time-resolved **positron-to-electron ratio** `f_k(R) = N_{e+,k}(R) / N_{e-,k}(R)` (state explicitly whether you mean this ratio or the positron *fraction* `e+/(e++e-)` — they have different denominators and different systematic cancellation properties; both are direct measurements, the choice is yours) in bins of **rigidity**, 5–50 GV, one value per **27-day (Bartels-rotation-length) time bin**, then searched for a **one-year periodic component**.

**[Proposal, flag before proceeding]** AMS's own published lepton time structure work splits along exactly this axis: the *daily* electron/positron papers bin in **rigidity** (1.00–41.9 GV), while the *Bartels-rotation* lepton paper bins in **energy** (1–50 GeV). Combining "27-day/Bartels-rotation cadence" with a "rigidity" variable, as you've specified, is not documented AMS practice — it's a valid but new combination you're choosing; note it explicitly rather than presenting it as how AMS did it.

## 2. Subsystems and selection (per time bin)

Charge sign and rigidity from the **Tracker/magnet only** (the sole charge-sign source); lepton/hadron separation from **TRD** (transition-radiation, γ-dependent) and **ECAL** (shower shape, `E/p`); TOF/RICH/ACC for direction, veto, `|Z|=1`. Use the electron/positron blueprint's Branch A (cut-and-count) or Branch B (template fit in a TRD/ECAL-type discriminant plane) **independently in every 27-day bin** — the selection, calibrations, and templates must be validated to be stable across bins or explicitly allowed to vary (see §5).

## 3. Backgrounds (per bin, per charge sign)

- Positive-rigidity sample: signal `e+` plus the dominant proton background plus charge-confused `e-`.
- Negative-rigidity sample: signal `e-` plus charge-confused `e+`.
- Charge confusion must be estimated from the **Tracker tail**, not the Gaussian core (non-negotiable invariant), as a migration matrix `M(e+←e-)`, `M(e-←e+)` vs rigidity — and this matrix, or its validation, needs to be checked for time-dependence too, since Tracker alignment/gain can drift.

## 4. Time binning

- 27 days = one Bartels rotation — a documented AMS choice of granularity (used for proton/helium/nuclei/lepton Bartels-rotation products), though not previously paired with this rigidity range.
- For a 1-year periodicity search this gives ~13–14 bins per cycle — comfortably above the "at least two bins per cycle" floor.
- Bin edges must not straddle hardware-era or reconstruction-version boundaries.
- At the low end (5 GV) and especially the high end (50 GV) of the range, check the per-bin event count is enough for the inference method chosen; if 27-day bins are too sparse at 50 GV, lengthen time bins there or merge rigidity bins rather than silently switching methods mid-range.

## 5. Exposure, efficiency, response — per bin, per charge sign

- `Φ_k(R) = (N_k - b_k) / (E_k ΔR)` with its own exposure `E_k` (acceptance × efficiency × livetime × geomagnetic transmission) computed **separately for `e+` and `e-` in every bin** — do not average fluxes of bins with different exposure, and do not assume the two charge signs share exposure just because they share the time interval (transmission differs by sign at low rigidity, which matters here since 5 GV is close to typical cutoffs).
- Geomagnetic cutoff transmission computed **per bin, per sign**, not once for the whole span; vary the safety factor as a systematic. This is the most sensitive part of the design at the 5 GV edge.
- State whether the response/resolution function is assumed stable across all 27-day bins or re-derived per bin/era; an unstated assumption here is a common failure mode.

## 6. Ratio construction and covariance

Numerator (`e+`) and denominator (`e-`) share some systematics (acceptance if same fiducial region, parts of trigger efficiency) but **not** all (species-specific charge-confusion, selection efficiency). Classify each nuisance as fully/partially/independently correlated per the ratio blueprint, and carry the numerator–denominator covariance explicitly:
`Var(f_k) ≈ f_k² [ (σ_{N,k}/N_k)² + (σ_{D,k}/D_k)² − 2ρσ_Nσ_D/(N_k D_k) ]` (delta method; use toys if uncertainties aren't small/Gaussian). Build one joint covariance over (time, rigidity), classifying each systematic as persistent (calibration, template shape — correlated across all 27-day bins), slowly varying, or independent per bin.

## 7. Periodicity search

- **Define in advance**: the period/frequency band around 1 year (e.g., a stated window, not "search everywhere"), the test statistic, and the significance threshold, before looking at the series.
- Because the series is unevenly exposed/sampled (bin-to-bin exposure varies), use a method that weights by per-bin uncertainty and handles that sampling (e.g., Lomb–Scargle-type or the wavelet approach AMS used for the daily proton flux, which reports normalized power with a 95%-CL threshold from red-noise Monte Carlo surrogates matched to the series' variance and lag-1 autocorrelation) — **[Unknown/needs input]**: AMS's wavelet method is documented only for the daily-proton analysis; no periodicity method or trial-factor treatment is documented for leptons or for a ratio, so this step is a proposal, not a restatement of AMS practice.
- **Look-elsewhere effect**: report local *and* global significance (Gross–Vitells trial factors) calibrated with toys reproducing the real sampling/exposure/correlated-systematics pattern; do not use Wilks or a single-frequency p-value.
- **Instrumental-artifact check**: run the identical search on the exposure time series, an efficiency series, and an abundant control species/ratio. A coincident ~1-year signal there is evidence of an instrumental or seasonal cycle, not a physics discovery.
- Note the related but distinct documented result: the Phys. Rept. review fits a **step-like logistic transition** of the positron-to-electron ratio across the 2013 solar-polarity reversal (Δt = 830 ± 30 days, energy-independent, reversal date fixed at 1 July 2013) — this is a one-time transition fit, not a periodicity claim, and should not be conflated with an annual-oscillation search.

## 8. Charge-sign modulation caveat

Solar modulation is charge-sign dependent (heliospheric field polarity), so `e+` and `e-` need not follow the same time variation; comparing them requires identical time bins, rigidity variable, exposure treatment, and sign-specific cutoff/charge-confusion handling (already folded into §5–6). Also relevant at your low edge: AMS's daily lepton papers report a positron/proton-vs-electron **hysteresis** below ~8.5 GV and stronger positron modulation than proton below ~7 GV — both are in your 5–50 GV window, so expect (and look for) non-trivial structure there rather than treating the low-rigidity bins as a clean baseline.

## 9. Closure and reporting

Test the joint (time, rigidity) model against separate per-bin fits with toys before trusting a combined periodicity result. Report per-period ratio values with their full covariance and exposures (not just a single "detected/not detected" verdict), so the result is refittable.
