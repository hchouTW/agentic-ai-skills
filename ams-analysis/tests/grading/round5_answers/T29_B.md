# Measurement Brief: ⁴He Absolute Differential Flux, 2 GV – 3 TV (Public AMS-02 Data)

This is a **brief** (estimand + scope + open inputs), not a full specification or a claim that AMS numbers already exist for this exact binning. I drafted it as a structured spec and ran it through `scripts/audit_analysis_spec.py`; as expected for a brief with no numeric inputs supplied, it comes back `blocked` on three structural placeholders (bin edges not numeric, `data_or_mc` not declared, and an `exposure`/`acceptance` overlap in `estimator.applies`) — those are exactly the kind of "fill this in before running" gaps a brief is supposed to surface, not evidence that the design is wrong.

## Estimand
Differential flux `Φ_⁴He(R)` (m⁻² sr⁻¹ s⁻¹ GV⁻¹) of galactic ⁴He nuclei **at the top of AMS** (not corrected to local interstellar spectrum), from `R = 2 GV` to `R = 3 TV`, over a stated time interval, for a stated Tracker configuration. [Proposal — this exact range/species combination is not one AMS has published; see below.]

## Target and level
- Target: `flux`. Level: `flux` (top-of-instrument, not object- or detector-level).
- Variable: rigidity `R`, unit GV. `⁴He` flux vs. kinetic-energy-per-nucleon is a separate, later conversion (needs the mass-per-nucleon Jacobian; [General method], run `scripts/ams_kinematics.py`).

## Species
`name=helium-4, |Z|=2, A=4, charge_sign=+1, mass=3.7274 GeV` [General method mass, PDG-type value]. **Open input:** ⁴He is not separated from ³He by rigidity and charge alone — the brief must state whether ⁴He is isolated by explicit mass (β-based) separation or by an assumed ³He/⁴He composition with propagated uncertainty (see `nuclei-and-isotopes`).

## Data/MC scope and period
Public data (`sample: public`). Data-taking period, hardware/tracker era, and MC generation record (spectrum, transport engine/physics list, geometry/material model, statistics) are **unresolved inputs** — not invented here.

## Subsystem roles
Tracker (rigidity, charge sign, `|Z|`, the only charge-sign source); TOF (direction, trigger, `|Z|`, `β`); RICH (`β`, `|Z|`, relevant if isotope separation is wanted); ACC (side-entry veto); TRD (`|Z|` support only for this species, not e/p separation). ECAL is not a standard nucleus charge estimator.

## What's public vs. user-supplied
**[Documented]** AMS published a helium flux from 1.9 GV to 3 TV using its first 30 months of data (S07/C59-C61), and a He/C/O flux to 3 TV over five years (S11/C62); both give worked examples of the selection (downward-going single track, L1+L9 charge-compatible with `|Z|=2`, cutoff safety factor 1.2, varied 1.0–1.4), systematic ledger (trigger, cutoff, acceptance, He inelastic-interaction/survival, ³He/⁴He composition, resolution, unfolding, rigidity scale), and rigidity-resolution behavior (Gaussian core 9%–64% from 10 GV–2 TV, non-Gaussian tails 3%–11%, MDR 3.2 TV). **None of those cut values, bin edges, or systematic sizes transfer automatically to a 2 GV–3 TV analysis** — they are specific to their own selections/periods and are cited only as precedent, not as this brief's numbers.

## Open inputs (must be resolved before this becomes a runnable spec)
1. Exact data period and hardware/track-layer configuration (drives rigidity resolution and MDR).
2. Geomagnetic cutoff model, version, and safety factor.
3. Full bin edges (width ≥ resolution-driven minimum at each edge; not the arithmetic center by default).
4. MC generation record and its data/MC validation.
5. ⁴He isolation method (mass separation vs. assumed composition) and its uncertainty.
6. Response matrix, built and checked with `scripts/validate_response.py`, and the unfolding-vs-forward-folding choice.
7. Numeric systematic sizes (structural rows only exist above; none is a number until sourced or measured for this selection).
8. Whether the ~13+ year span implied by reaching 3 TV statistics needs a time-averaging or solar-modulation treatment (`time-dependent-analysis`).

**Non-negotiables to carry through:** rigidity ≠ momentum (`p = |Z|R`); charge sign only from the Tracker; do not double-apply acceptance/efficiency/livetime; validate the charge-migration tail, not just the Gaussian core; every AMS-specific number quoted in the eventual write-up needs its own claim ID.
