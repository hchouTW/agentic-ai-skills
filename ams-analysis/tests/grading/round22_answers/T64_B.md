The paper is AMS, "Properties of a New Group of Cosmic Nuclei: Results from the Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer on Sodium, Aluminum, and Nitrogen", PRL 127, 021101 (2021) [Documented, S59]. My evidence is the main article only. The Supplemental Material (per-cut definitions, figures S1-S8, Tables SI-SVI) was not read, and the index values and flux shapes appear only in figures, which I did not read.

**1. Data sample and rigidity range** [Documented, C162]
- The sample is 0.46 million Na and 0.51 million Al nuclei.
- They were collected in the first 8.5 years on the ISS, from May 19, 2011 to October 30, 2019. That period contained 1.50 x 10^11 cosmic-ray events in total.
- The rigidity range is 2.15 GV to 3.0 TV, measured in 49 bins.
- Total flux errors at 100 GV are 5.0% for Na and 4.8% for Al.

**2. Background from interactions between L1 and L2** [Documented, C163]
- This background is evaluated by fitting the L1 charge distribution with templates of Ne, Na, Mg, Al and Si, and then cutting on the L1 charge. The relevant material is the TRD and upper TOF.
- It varies smoothly from 8% below 10 GV to 25% at 3 TV for Na.
- For Al it varies from 9% below 10 GV to 16% at 3 TV.
- This is separate from the main background, which is heavier nuclei (Mg, Si) interacting above tracker L2. The paper estimates the interactions above L1 (carbon fiber and aluminum honeycomb support structures) from simulation validated with AMS charge-changing cross sections.
- The overall background-subtraction uncertainty is:
  - Na: 1.5% at 2 GV, 1.5% at 100 GV and 6% at 3.0 TV.
  - Al: 1% at 2 GV, 1.5% at 100 GV and 5% at 3.0 TV.

**3. Unfolding corrections for Na** [Documented, C164]
- Bin-to-bin migration is corrected by unfolding. The paper cites its Ref. [25] for the procedure, and I did not check that reference.
- The correction is (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i. Here N_i is the number of events corrected for migration, as used in the flux, and aleph_i is the number of observed events in bin i.
- For Na it is +20% at 3 GV, falling smoothly to +6% at 10 GV, -1% at 100 GV, -10% at 300 GV and -20% at 3.0 TV.
- The paper says the Al corrections are "similar". I give no Al numbers.

**4. Which flux the Na and Al spectral indices track** [Documented, C166]
- The spectral index is gamma = d[log(Phi)]/d[log(R)], obtained model-independently over rigidity intervals with boundaries 7.09, 12.0, 16.6, 28.8, 45.1, 80.5, 211.0 and 3000.0 GV.
- Below about 100 GV the Na flux and spectral index follow the nitrogen (N) flux and spectral index.
- Above about 100 GV the Al flux and spectral index follow the N flux and spectral index.
- The comparison N flux is the AMS N flux from the Phys. Rep. 894 review (the paper's Ref. [3]). The paper re-analysed that flux over the same collection time as Na and Al. It is not a new N measurement in this Letter.
- This is an empirical statement about the shapes. Reading it as a primary or secondary composition is a separate model step. The paper's own component fits are in C167, which I did not summarize here.
