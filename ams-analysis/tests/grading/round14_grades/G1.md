## Rubric defects (separate from answer defects)

- T54: "8.5e10 is the total sample (C137)" is a critical requirement, but the prompt does not ask about the total sample. The risk is already covered by the prohibited claim "8.5e10 as the nitrogen sample". This requirement penalises a correct answer that simply leaves out an irrelevant number. Suggest moving it to "if 8.5e10 is quoted, it must be labelled as the total".
- T54: "source N/O abundance statement is the paper's and not evaluated" is likewise not asked by the prompt. Suggest making it conditional ("if mentioned").
- T55: the requirement "fits are empirical" has no matching wording in C147 or C148. Their limitations say "fit parameters and conclusions as stated by the paper" and "paper's own conclusion". "Empirical" appears in C172 (sulfur). It is reasonable, but it does not come from the claim text.

- T56: the rubric's requirement "Supplemental Material text only" sits alongside the C121 (main-article) material that both answers reasonably use to separate the two trigger quantities. It would be clearer to state that C121 may be cited for the contrast.
