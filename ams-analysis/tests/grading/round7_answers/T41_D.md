**Short answer.** About 830 +- 30 days (roughly 2.3 years). This is the 10%-90% transition time of the positron-to-electron flux ratio R_e = Phi(e+)/Phi(e-) across the 2013 solar polarity reversal. It does not depend on energy within the 1-21 GeV range fitted. [Documented, C90, S01]

**How AMS got it.** [Documented, C90, S01, Phys. Rept. 894, Section 16, Eqs. 17-18; scope is the review's analysis of the first seven years, with data from the lepton time-structure paper S42, C89]

1. Data: 3871 independent R_e measurements in energy and time. They come from the electron and positron fluxes in 1-50 GeV, measured per Bartels rotation (27 days) over 79 rotations, May 2011 to May 2017, with 49 energy bins (C89). Taking the ratio of the two fluxes cancels most of the shared short-term structure. The review states that only particle drift produces a charge-sign-dependent solar modulation, so the ratio isolates that effect (C89).
2. Model: a descriptive four-parameter logistic function in time at each energy, R_e(t,E) = R0(E) [1 + C(E) / (exp(-(t - t_1/2(E)) / (Delta t / Delta_80)) + 1)]. The parameters are R0 (baseline), C (transition amplitude), t_1/2 (midpoint) and Delta t. Delta_80 = 4.39 is a normalization that makes Delta t the 10%-90% transition time.
3. Fit: the fits over 1-21 GeV have chi2/d.o.f. about 1. Delta t comes out independent of energy at 830 +- 30 days. Above 6 GeV the review fixes Delta t to 830 days and uses the parametrized midpoint of Eq. 18, because Delta t and t_1/2 are only determined at low energy where C is large.
4. Related results from the same fit (C90): the midpoint delay follows t_1/2(E) - t_rev = tau (E/GeV)^rho, with rho = -0.33 +- 0.04 (stat) (+0.08, -0.15) (syst) and tau = 580 +- 19 (stat) +- 136 (syst) days. t_1/2 shifts by 260 +- 30 days from 1 to 6 GeV. C is close to 1 at 1 GeV, falls smoothly with energy, and is consistent with zero above 20 GeV. This is in qualitative agreement with drift-including solar modulation models.

**Caveats.**
- The logistic form is the collaboration's choice. It is a descriptive fit, not a physical model. The 830-day value is a parameter of that form. A different functional form would give a somewhat different transition time.
- Delta t does not depend on t_rev. The delays t_1/2 - t_rev do depend on it, and t_rev = 1 July 2013 is a stated choice (the centre of the period without a well-defined solar polarity). The solar-physics source for it was not read here.
- Quote both statistical and systematic errors on rho and tau. The systematic is large.
