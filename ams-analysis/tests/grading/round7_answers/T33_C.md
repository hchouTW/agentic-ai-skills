This question has two parts. I can't support either as written.

## 1. The 2013 paper is not the latest positron-fraction measurement

The 2013 paper is M. Aguilar et al. (AMS), "First Result from AMS on the ISS: Precision Measurement of the Positron Fraction in Primary Cosmic Rays of 0.5-350 GeV", PRL 110, 141102 (2013). It is the first AMS positron-fraction result. [Documented, C03; S02 was checked at abstract+metadata level only] It reports the fraction over 0.5-350 GeV from 6.8x10^6 positron and electron events.

You can cite it as the first AMS measurement. Calling it the "latest" would be wrong, for these reasons:

- **Same quantity, newer paper (2014).** The index records PRL 113, 121101 (2014), a high-statistics positron fraction over 0.5-500 GeV. It sits at metadata-only level, so I quote no numbers from it. [Documented, S03]
- **Later positron work (2019).** AMS published "Towards Understanding the Origin of Cosmic-Ray Positrons", PRL 122, 041102 (2019). [Documented, C04; S04, abstract-level statement, main article also read]
  - It reports a positron flux from 1.9 million positrons up to 1 TeV.
  - The flux rises from about 25 GeV, falls off above about 284 GeV, and shows a source-term energy cutoff at more than 4 sigma.
  - That paper presents the positron flux, not the fraction. A model constraint should say which one it uses. The fraction moves if the electron spectrum moves, and the two share events and systematics.
- **Publication date versus data period.** Publication year and data-taking period differ for each result, so any "latest" statement should give both.
  - 2013: the first-result paper, published in 2013.
  - 2014: published in 2014 (S03).
  - 2019: published in 2019 (S04). The positron flux in the 2019 paper uses a longer data set than the 2013 first result.
- **Currency check not done.** I could not browse INSPIRE-HEP, the journal or ams02.space. The skill's index was last verified on 2026-09-20. I can't claim anything newer than that, and I haven't checked for superseding papers, supplements or errata after that date.

A defensible sentence: "The positron fraction was first measured by AMS in PRL 110, 141102 (2013), and was extended in 2014 (PRL 113, 121101) and in the 2019 positron-flux paper (PRL 122, 041102). As of the last index verification (2026-09-20), I have not confirmed anything newer."
