Not as a bare, model-free measurement. You can quote it as what the AMS review reports, but only with its conditions attached.

**What AMS actually measured [Documented, C91].** The 0.092 is not a directly measured source abundance. The AMS review (Phys. Rept. 894, 2021, Section 12) fits the nitrogen flux over 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV as a weighted sum of a primary reference and a secondary reference:

- Phi_N = Phi_NP + Phi_NS
- Phi_NP = (0.092 +- 0.002) x Phi_O
- Phi_NS = (0.61 +- 0.02) x Phi_B
- chi2/d.o.f. = 59/64

The review describes the fitted 0.092 +- 0.002 as the N/O abundance ratio at the source. It compares this with the Solar-System value of 0.135 (+0.051, -0.047) and calls the two consistent.

**Why you cannot quote it as a plain source abundance.**

1. **The decomposition is conditional on its inputs.** The fit assumes oxygen is a purely primary shape and boron a purely secondary shape. The review itself notes that O has the lowest secondary content among He, C and O. It also notes that B is mostly made from primary C and O. So 0.092 and 0.61 are conditional on that choice. The label "source abundance" rests on the same assumption, so it is not a propagation-free measurement.
2. **The fit quality does not test the assumption.** chi2/d.o.f. = 59/64 shows the two-component form describes the data. It does not show that O is purely primary or B purely secondary.
3. **The ratio is a flux-normalization ratio.** It multiplies the O flux in rigidity. Calling it an abundance ratio at the source also requires comparable acceleration and injection at the same rigidity or energy per nucleon. Within the review it is an interpretation step, not a direct observable.
4. **The uncertainty is a fit error.** The +- 0.002 is the error on a fit parameter within that model. It excludes the model-choice systematic above, and it is much smaller than the +0.051/-0.047 on the Solar-System comparison value.
5. **The source is a review.** The numbers come from the Phys. Rept. review, which overlaps a separate AMS nitrogen paper that is not indexed here. For a citation, check the primary PRL (S20 lists N: PRL 121, 051103, 2018) before quoting, and do not copy review wording as if it came from the paper.

**Suggested wording.** "In a fit of the AMS nitrogen flux (2.2 GV-3.3 TV) as Phi_N = Phi_NP + Phi_NS, with oxygen as the primary reference and boron as the secondary reference, the primary component is (0.092 +- 0.002) x Phi_O. The AMS review interprets this as the N/O ratio at the source, consistent with the Solar-System value 0.135 (+0.051, -0.047). This is conditional on treating O as purely primary and B as purely secondary."

Also state that this is a fit-model result (a [General method] decomposition applied to published data), not a direct detector measurement. Do not claim a model-independent source abundance. If you need that, you would have to vary the choice of primary and secondary reference species and propagate the difference as a systematic.
