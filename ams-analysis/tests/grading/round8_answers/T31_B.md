## Defects, in priority order

**1. Efficiency applied twice (response matrix plus a separate efficiency).** [General method]
- If the response matrix R_ij already includes inefficiency, its columns sum to the efficiency (at most one), and A and ε are already inside it. You must not divide by a separate efficiency again.
- Declare the matrix convention: `includes_efficiency` (inefficiency lives in R) or `conditional_on_selected` (columns sum to one, inefficiency lives in A_j or ε).
- The trigger efficiency is a conditional efficiency. If the matrix was built from MC with the trigger and selection simulated, it may already be in there. If not, it must be applied once, and only if it was measured separately with a named denominator.
- In the response model `μ_i = Σ_j R_ij A_j T_j Φ_j Δx_j + Σ_k b_ik`, list where each factor is applied: acceptance, response, exposure, calibration. Each must appear exactly once.
- Do not multiply marginal efficiencies as if independent. The trigger efficiency and the selection efficiencies share rigidity, charge and geometry dependence. Measure the joint efficiency, or use conditional factors with a stated denominator, and show ordering and combined-efficiency closure. Two 99% factors only bound the joint efficiency to 98–99%.
- Also check that the trigger efficiency is not already folded into the livetime or exposure (prescale and dead-time handling).

**2. A TRD gain drift handled as both a veto and an efficiency correction.** [General method]
- This is double counting of one physical effect. A single effect must sit in exactly one category: veto, calibration, efficiency, or response.
- If a run veto removes the drifting periods, reduce livetime and exposure for those periods. Then do not also include that period or effect in the TRD efficiency.
- Alternatively, correct the drift with a time-dependent calibration or template, which leaves only a residual systematic.
- Record the category in the systematic ledger. Do not also carry the same drift as a separate systematic (the "veto and systematic" case).
- If the TRD cut efficiency is measured on veto-surviving runs only, it is already conditional on that veto. Do not apply a second correction for the same period.
- Check that the veto itself does not bias the sample, and that the efficiency is time-stable after it (plot an abundant control species versus time).

**3. The estimator itself.** `Φ = (N − b) / (T_live · exposure · Δx)` has several problems.
- Livetime is multiplied by exposure. Exposure already contains acceptance, livetime and geomagnetic transmission, `Σ_t A_eff(t) T_live(t) × transmission`. Multiplying by livetime again is a double count. Use either `T·A·ε` with separate factors or `E·Δx`, not both. The same applies to the term "exposure" if it is the effective acceptance in m² sr s.
- Efficiency and acceptance are distinct from exposure and livetime. Check which of them your "exposure" already contains.
- The bin width must be the true-bin width Δx_j, in the variable of the flux (rigidity or energy).
- Differential flux needs a bin-centering prescription, not the arithmetic center by default.

**4. The N − b diagonal estimator and migration.**
- `N_i / (Δx_i T_i A_i ε_i)` is valid only if migration is negligible. For a steep spectrum, feed-in from abundant low-R events to higher bins dominates even when the resolution core looks small. Test against the response matrix.
- If you have a response matrix, you are presumably unfolding or forward-folding. Then N − b is not divided by an efficiency at all. Unfold the background-subtracted counts, or fit forward.
- Background subtraction needs an estimator, a control region with its signal contamination, a transfer model, a closure test and nuisances. The background must be subtracted in the same reconstructed variable and bins as N.
- Do not subtract a scalar b if b has a response of its own.

**5. Uncertainties.**
- Carry covariance, not per-bin percentages. Include statistical, template/MC, systematic, unfolding-induced and cross-time covariance.
- At low counts, N − b can be negative or Gaussian errors fail. Use Poisson or toy-validated treatment.

## Corrected structure [Proposal]

1. Estimand and variables (true versus reconstructed; |R| versus signed R).
2. Observed N_i (after the run veto) and background b_ik, with their ledger.
3. Response R_ij declared as either `includes_efficiency` or `conditional_on_selected`.
4. Exposure E_j built once, from time-resolved A_eff, livetime (after the veto) and geomagnetic transmission.
5. The trigger efficiency applied once, as a measured conditional efficiency with a named denominator, only if not already in R.
6. The TRD drift in exactly one category.
7. Solve with unfolding or forward folding: `μ_i = Σ_j R_ij E_j Φ_j Δx_j + Σ_k b_ik`.
8. Validate with MC closure, and with stability of the flux across the veto period.
