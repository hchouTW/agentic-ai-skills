**What AMS reports.** [Documented, C84; source S01, the AMS Phys. Rept. 894 review of the first seven years, full text, review-level scope] AMS divided the Li, Be and B fluxes (secondary) by the C and O fluxes (primary), giving Li/C, Be/C, B/C, Li/O, Be/O and B/O. It fitted a single power law Phi_S/Phi_P proportional to R^Delta in two non-overlapping rigidity intervals, 60.3-192 GV and 192-3300 GV. Above about 200 GV the secondary-to-primary index hardens on average by

Delta[192-3300] - Delta[60.3-192] = 0.140 +- 0.025,

and the review states a significance exceeding 5 sigma. This means the secondary-to-primary ratios fall less steeply with rigidity above about 200 GV than below. The review says the Li, Be and B spectral indices are nearly identical to each other and clearly different from those of He, C and O. The review's high-rigidity index of -0.266 +- 0.022 above 192 GV is recorded under C82 for the secondary/primary ratios (C84 is the claim for the 0.140 hardening). [Documented, C81] The primary He, C and O fluxes harden from about 200 GV as well, and above 60 GV have identical rigidity dependence.

**How significant it is, and the caveats.**
- The ">5 sigma" is the review's own statement for the average hardening across the ratios. [Unknown/needs input] I could not verify how the significance was computed: how systematic correlations were handled, and whether any interval-choice or trial correction was applied. Treat it as a quoted statement, not something I re-derived. As a rough scale, 0.140 / 0.025 is about 5.6 [General method], which is consistent with the quoted figure but is not the review's calculation.
- The two interval boundaries (60.3, 192 and 3300 GV) are the review's choice, not a prescription. A different split could change the number.
- The statement is about a change in power-law index (a spectral shape), not a detection of a particular source or mechanism.
- Interpretation is a separate, model-dependent step. The review says the hardening is consistent with expectations if it comes from propagation in the Galaxy. If it came from hardening of the source injection, secondaries and primaries would harden similarly. The review also states that no model predicted the observed behaviour of the primary He, C, O or the secondary Li, Be, B. That is the review's reading. I did not read the third-party model papers.
- Numbers are from the review's data set. Do not attach them to the earlier separate papers, such as the 2016 B/C paper (S10, C08), which covers 1.9 GV-2.6 TV with its own power-law result above 65 GV.

I did not check for superseding papers or newer results, and no web access was available. This index was last verified 2026-09-20, so I make no claim about currency.
