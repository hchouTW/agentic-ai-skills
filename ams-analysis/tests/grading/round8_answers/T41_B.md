The transition takes about 830 +- 30 days (roughly 2.3 years), and the number comes from a fit to the positron-to-electron flux ratio, not a direct count of days.

**What was measured** [Documented, C39, S42; C89, C90, S01]
- The ratio is R_e = Phi(e+)/Phi(e-). It was built from electron and positron fluxes in 79 Bartels rotations (27 days each), May 2011 to May 2017, in energy from 1 to 50 GeV. The flux paper bins in energy, not rigidity.
- The review uses 3871 independent R_e measurements in energy and time. The S42 abstract (C39) reports the smooth transition over 830 +- 30 days with an energy-dependent midpoint delay.
- Months-scale structures appear in both e+ and e- but are not visible in the ratio, because they largely cancel.

**How the 830 days was obtained** [Documented, C90, S01]
- The Phys. Rept. review (C90) fits a four-parameter logistic function at each energy: R_e(t,E) = R0(E) [1 + C(E) / (exp(-(t - t_1/2(E)) / (Delta t / Delta_80)) + 1)].
- The free parameters are R0, C, t_1/2 and Delta t. The constant Delta_80 = 4.39 is chosen so that Delta t is the 10%-90% transition time.
- The fits give chi2/d.o.f. of about 1 over 1-21 GeV. Delta t comes out independent of energy at 830 +- 30 days.
- The midpoint delay follows t_1/2(E) - t_rev = tau (E/GeV)^rho. Here t_rev = 1 July 2013, which the review takes as the centre of the period without a well-defined solar polarity.
- The fitted values are rho = -0.33 +- 0.04 (stat) (+0.08, -0.15) (syst) and tau = 580 +- 19 (stat) +- 136 (syst) days. The midpoint t_1/2 shifts by 260 +- 30 days between 1 and 6 GeV.
- The amplitude C is close to 1 at 1 GeV and consistent with zero above 20 GeV.

**Caveats**
- The logistic fit is the review's own (C90). The S42 abstract (C39) states the 830-day result and the energy-dependent delay.
- The fit is descriptive. The collaboration chose the four-parameter form, and it is not a physical model.
- t_rev is a stated choice, so the midpoint delays depend on it. The 830-day duration itself does not depend on t_rev.
- Delta t and t_1/2 are only constrained at low energy, where C is large. Above 6 GeV the review fixes Delta t to 830 days.
- The text read describes no trial factor and no correlated-error model across energy bins. Treat those as [Unknown/needs input].
- The review says the result agrees qualitatively with solar-modulation models that include drifts. That is the collaboration's interpretation, not a separate measurement.
