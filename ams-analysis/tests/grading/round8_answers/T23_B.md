The ACC (Anti-Coincidence Counters) is AMS-02's side-entry veto. Its job is to reject particles that enter or leave the Tracker volume transversely, through the magnet sides, outside the nominal acceptance. It also flags events with extra activity.

**Construction [Documented, C69].** In the Phys. Rept. 894 review, the ACC is sixteen curved scintillator panels, each 800 mm long. They sit inside the magnet bore around the Tracker. Wavelength-shifting fibres carry the light to PMTs, with each PMT reading two adjacent panels. The vertical edges are tongue-and-groove, so a particle crossing a seam is seen in two panels.

**Trigger role [Documented, C69, C73].** For |Z| = 1 the level-1 trigger requires four TOF signals and no ACC signal. The ACC therefore acts as a veto in the trigger and again at event selection.

**Performance [Documented, C21, C69].** The quoted side-entry rejection efficiency is 0.99999. This is a pre-flight long-duration test value, and the analysis papers quote the same number. It applies only in the context of those sources (|Z| = 1, the stated analysis and configuration), and it is not a universal performance figure. The review does not give the inefficiency definition or its per-condition validity.

**What it does not do [Documented, C13].** The ACC measures none of the following:
- charge sign, which comes only from the Tracker and magnet;
- |Z|;
- β;
- direction;
- lepton/hadron identification.

Its only roles are the veto and, in the cross-subsystem matrix, supporting side-entry geometry. Do not present it as an independent identification detector.

**Analysis implications [General method / Proposal].**
- The veto is an event-level thresholded decision. Its loss of good events is an efficiency, to be measured as a conditional efficiency versus rigidity or energy, time and rate.
- Do not also fold its effect into the geometric acceptance (double counting).
- Failure modes:
  - Backsplash from ECAL showers at high energy can veto real signal, giving an energy-dependent inefficiency.
  - Accidental activity depends on orbit and rate.
  - Hardware inefficiency lets false side-entering events through.
- The ACC thresholds and inefficiency are treated as a slowly varying calibration item, to be checked per period against flight data.
- Internal ACC thresholds, trigger bits and cuts are not public here. Treat them as [Unknown/needs input].
