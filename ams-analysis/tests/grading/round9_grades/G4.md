## Notes on the test specification (T44, T45 are new)

1. **T44, "thresholds like pull width above 1 are Proposal not AMS values"**: this is unconditional as written, but it can only be checked when the answer quotes a threshold. T44_B quotes none and should not lose credit for it. Suggest: "if a threshold (pull width, mean pull, bias fraction) is quoted, it is labelled Proposal, not an AMS value".
2. **T44, "the L-curve corner is often not safe"**: the reference's support is the toy behaviour of `choose-regularization` ("the L-curve corner often is" well above the best fixed setting). That is a general claim from the maintainer's toys, not a literature result. The requirement is better phrased as "the criterion must be validated on toys against alternatives (GCV, LOO, best fixed) before being trusted", which is what both answers do. As written it rewards repeating the reference's wording.
3. **T44, "scripts named"**: does not say which scripts. Given the reference rules (line 69-71), the natural minimum is `choose-regularization` plus `fold-compare` (or `closure`). T44_A names only `fold-compare` and still meets the requirement as written.
4. **T45, "run or propose ratio-toys or ratio-measured"**: `ratio-toys` is a single-bin two-count tool and does not do the constant fit across time bins that the prompt asks about. The tools that test the prompt's actual claim are `ratio-measured` (measured covariance) and `ratio-cov` (declared structures, fallback). Suggest "ratio-toys for the per-effect cancellation and ratio-measured (or ratio-cov when no measured covariance) for the constant fit across bins".
5. **T45, missing physics requirement**: the prompt asks to fit a *constant* to e+/e- over 27-day bins. AMS has published charge-sign-dependent time structure of e+/e- (C39, C41, C89, C90). Neither the critical requirements nor the prohibited claims cover "a constant is a physics hypothesis to test against a time-dependent model", although both answers raise it. Consider adding it (and, as prohibited, "presenting a good diagonal chi2 as evidence that e+/e- is constant").
6. **T45, "chi2 with the full covariance and goodness of fit with a trigger for adding an uncertainty"**: for a constancy test, a poor chi2 can indicate real time dependence, not only a missing uncertainty. The requirement should allow "trigger for adding an uncertainty or for rejecting the constant model", as T45_B argues explicitly.
