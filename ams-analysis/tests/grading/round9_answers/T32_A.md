Design for a 27-day-bin e+/e- ratio, 5 to 50 GV, searched for a one-year periodicity. Everything below is [General method] or [Proposal] unless a claim ID is attached.

## 1. Estimand and variable

- **Estimand.** Per 27-day bin k and rigidity bin i, R_ki = Phi(e+)/Phi(e-). I recommend the flux ratio over the positron fraction; the fraction is a monotonic transform, so either is fine if fixed in advance.
- **Rigidity versus energy.** You asked for 5 to 50 GV, so the binning is in rigidity (Tracker). Published AMS time-integrated lepton fluxes and the Bartels-rotation lepton paper bin in energy (1-50 GeV for the latter) [Documented, C39, C48, C24, C25]. The daily e+- papers use rigidity, 1.00-41.9 GV [Documented, C40, C41, abstract level]. State which variable defines your bins; if energy, you need a rigidity-to-energy response and bremsstrahlung treatment.
- **Range caveat.** The published daily-lepton rigidity range stops at 41.9 GV; 5-50 GV goes beyond it, so top-bin statistics are the limiting issue (section 3).
- **Binning.** A 27-day bin is one Bartels rotation. Align to rotations, fix edges in advance, and do not let a bin straddle a hardware-era or reconstruction-version boundary.
- **Sampling.** One year is about 13.5 bins; the Nyquist period is 54 days, so a 365-day period is well sampled. Periods below 54 days cannot be resolved: the 27-day, 13.5-day and 9-day variations AMS saw in daily proton and helium fluxes [Documented, C36, C37, abstract level] are washed out or aliased.
- **Rigidity bins.** Use few bins across 5-50 GV (for example 5-10, 10-20, 20-50), fixed before looking at the time dependence, with widths at least about twice the resolution [Documented, C25, energy-binned analogue]. [Proposal]

## 2. Per-bin measurement chain (positron/electron blueprint)

1. **Charge sign** from the Tracker/magnet only; TRD and ECAL give no charge sign. Select |Z| = 1.
2. **Lepton selection.** TRD estimator and ECAL estimator (shower shape, E/p compatibility). Public AMS practice is a template fit in the (Lambda_TRD, Lambda_CC) plane with correct-sign lepton, correct-sign proton and charge-confusion templates [Documented, C24 (positrons), C26 (electrons)]. Run the fit per time and rigidity bin, or simultaneously across the two charge-sign samples sharing charge-confusion parameters. [Proposal]
3. **Backgrounds.** Dominant: protons misidentified as positrons (orders of magnitude more abundant). Also charge confusion e- to e+ (a Tracker tail; validate in the tail, not from the Gaussian core), secondary/albedo leptons, bremsstrahlung and conversion mismatch.
4. **Per-bin response and exposure.** Unfold migration independently in each time bin, as AMS did for its time-resolved flux products [Documented, C43, C46, C49]. Use Phi_ki = (N_ki - b_ki)/(E_ki dR_i) with E_ki = acceptance x efficiency x livetime x cutoff transmission accumulated in the bin. Never average fluxes of bins with unequal exposure; keep acceptance, efficiency, exposure and livetime distinct.
5. **Geomagnetic cutoff per bin.** Transmission is bin-dependent and matters most at 5 GV and can differ by charge sign, so handle cutoff and charge confusion per sign. AMS exposure used livetime-weighted seconds above cutoff, in normal conditions, within a zenith cone, outside the South Atlantic Anomaly [Documented, C48, C51], with cutoff safety factors treated as a systematic [Documented, C31, C27]; these are choices of those analyses, not rules.
6. **Detector-state drift.** Correct each drift in one category only (veto, calibration, efficiency or response). Show stability with a metric and trigger, for example a control-species rate flat versus time after livetime and cutoff correction. Per-period efficiency and calibration values are [Unknown/needs input]; none are supplied here.

## 3. Statistics and uncertainties

- **Counts.** Positrons limit the statistics, especially in the top rigidity bin of a 27-day slice. Use Poisson-exact or toy-validated intervals, not Gaussian errors, at low counts. If sparse, merge rigidity bins or restrict the periodicity search to the range where time resolution is supported, and state that range.
- **Ratio cancellations.** Acceptance and efficiency do not cancel automatically; classify each nuisance as correlated, partial or independent between numerator and denominator and verify. Sign-dependent items (charge confusion, cutoff) do not cancel.
- **Systematics over (time, rigidity).** Split into time-independent and time-dependent parts, the latter added in quadrature as in the AMS daily proton flux [Documented, C44]. Classify each source as persistent, slowly varying (neighbouring bins correlated) or independent per period. Build one covariance with a documented ordering; a time-by-rigidity Kronecker product is valid only if the correlation factorizes, which must be tested. Check with scripts/validate_covariance.py.
- **AMS correlation model.** How AMS correlated systematics across days or rotations is [Unknown/needs input]; it is not in the public text read.
- **Reference-period normalization.** It cancels only the persistent systematics and correlates the statistical errors with the reference; carry that covariance.

## 4. Pre-registered one-year periodicity search

Define before examining the time dependence [Proposal]:

- **Series.** R_k per rigidity bin with its covariance, plus a joint series across rigidity bins; declare which is primary.
- **Frequency band.** A single targeted period of 365.25 days has no look-elsewhere penalty. A scan over a band (for example 300-430 days) does.
- **Detrend the solar cycle.** The solar cycle and the 2013 polarity reversal dominate on multi-year timescales. AMS described the e+/e- transition across the reversal with a logistic fit, 830 +- 30 days [Documented, C90, C39, C89]; this is a descriptive fit of the collaboration, not a rule, and the energy dependence of the midpoint is S42's. Remove a smooth trend (low-order polynomial or spline plus a logistic term across 2013, or a long-window running median) with a window much longer than one year. Test with injected annual signals that the detrending does not absorb them. Prefer a joint trend-plus-sinusoid fit over sequential detrending, which biases the amplitude.
- **Test statistic.** Use a method that handles unequal exposure and bin uncertainties: weighted least squares of a + b cos(2 pi t/T) + c sin(2 pi t/T) with the full covariance, a Lomb-Scargle periodogram of the detrended series, or a wavelet analysis. Amplitude is sqrt(b^2 + c^2), with phase. AMS's daily proton analysis used wavelet normalized power with 95% levels from red-noise (lag-1 autoregressive) Monte Carlo surrogates [Documented, C45, C52; daily proton flux only].
- **Look-elsewhere and null calibration.** If scanning, report local and global significance with trial factors (Gross and Vitells) and calibrate with toys that reproduce the sampling, exposure pattern, covariance and red noise. No Wilks and no single-frequency p-value. Whether AMS applied a trial-factor correction to its periodicity or hysteresis significances is not described in the text read [Unknown/needs input].
- **Per-sign and per-rigidity comparison.** Also fit e+ and e- separately. Positron variations resemble proton and differ from electron, with electron-proton and electron-positron hysteresis below about 8.5 GV [Documented, C40, C41, abstract level]; positrons are modulated more than protons below about 7 GV [Documented, C41]. Do not merge the two thresholds. 5-50 GV straddles them, so an annual signal may differ between the 5-10 GV bin and higher bins; report per rigidity bin as well as combined.

## 5. Controls: how an annual line becomes fake

A one-year line most likely comes from exposure, livetime, ISS orbit or beta-angle seasonality, seasonal cutoff transmission or South Atlantic Anomaly fraction, detector temperature or state cycles, or seasonal variation of the proton contamination or charge-confusion tail (the positron sample is a small residual of a proton-dominated sample).

Run the same search on the exposure series, efficiency series (TRD and ECAL), and a control species or ratio with no expected annual modulation (for example He/p or a high-rigidity ratio); treat a coincident annual signal as a systematic, not a discovery. Add a block-shuffled null and signal injection at several amplitudes to measure recovered amplitude, bias and detection threshold.

## 6. Pitfalls

- Averaging fluxes of bins with different exposure; one cutoff transmission for all bins.
- A calibration drift treated as both a veto and an efficiency correction.
- Independent errors assumed across bins while persistent systematics are shared.
- A periodicity claimed from a single-frequency p-value or without exposure and control checks.
- Detrending that removes the annual signal, or quoting an amplitude after sequential detrending as unbiased.
- Reading e+/e- as pure heliospheric physics without sign-specific handling; a modulation or drift interpretation is a separate model step.
- Quoting the 108-day periods of the deuteron and Li-isotope analyses [Documented, C27, C29] or the proton quality-time conditions [Documented, C31] as rules.
- Inventing AMS livetimes, calibration constants, cuts or per-period counts.

## 7. Inputs that would change the design

- Public versus collaboration-internal event-level data.
- Per-bin livetime, exposure and calibration records, and the hardware eras and reconstruction versions in the span.
- Rigidity (assumed) versus energy binning.
- Whether the 365.25-day period is pre-registered or a band is scanned.
- Per-bin positron counts, which set how far up in rigidity 27-day bins are supported.
