## T45_A: 9/10 (2/2/1/2/2)

Claim verification:
- **"AMS reported that e+ and e- time structures differ at the daily level [Documented, C40, C41, abstract level]"**: C41 (S46, positron/electron/proton, 1.00-41.9 GV, 11 years, abstract+metadata, numeric allowed) says that positron variations "differ distinctly from electron variations", so the statement is covered. C40 (S45) compares electrons with **protons** ("electron and proton variations differ") and does not compare e+ with e-. As a co-citation it is harmless because C41 carries the statement, but C40 alone would not support it. The abstract-level flag is correct.
- **"smooth e+/e- transition across the solar polarity reversal over about 830 days [Documented, C39, C89, C90]"**: C39 (S42, abstract, 1-50 GeV in energy, May 2011-May 2017) and C90 (S01 review, full-text) both contain 830 +- 30 days. C89 (S01) does not contain the 830-day figure. It supports only "the long-term evolution differs between electrons and positrons" in R_e. G4 flagged the same over-citation of C89 in round 9 T45_A. Harmless, because the other two IDs cover it.
- **"AMS evaluated efficiencies per day and added the time-dependent systematics in quadrature, separate from the time-independent ones [Documented, C44, S43, daily proton flux only]"**: C44 (S43, proton, 1.00-100 GV, 2011-05-20 to 2019-10-29, full-text) matches. The scoping "daily proton flux only" and "one analysis's choice, not a rule" are correct. "No covariance model appears in the text the ledger read" matches C44's `limitations` exactly.
- No other AMS numbers. The answer says honestly that the IDs were taken from `time-dependent-analysis.md` and were not looked up in `source-index`.

- Joint covariance; a diagonal-only fit is wrong: present (item 2: persistent and slowly varying systematics, the full time x energy covariance with the numerator-denominator cross-covariance, and the reference-period normalization caveat).

## T45_B: 9/10 (2/2/2/1/2)

But C24 also says that the positron template comes **from electron data below 100 GeV**, and C25 (S04) says that the data/MC efficiency correction delta comes **from the high-statistics electron sample**. Both document shared, electron-derived inputs, which makes them correlated nuisances, not separate or non-identical ones. This is the round 9 T45_B slip again, softened from "species-specific" to "not identical" and "separate provenance".
