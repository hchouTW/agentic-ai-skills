**Source scope.** All statements below are from the Supplemental Material text of PRL 134, 051002 (S09), as recorded in the skill's source ledger (read 2026-10-02). I had no web access, so I did not re-check the paper and make no claim about whether a newer version exists.

**1. Antiproton trigger efficiency [Documented, C175].** It is 80% above 10 GV and rises to 83% at 1 GV. This is the efficiency used in the flux formula as eps. It is not the trigger-efficiency systematic, which is below 1% (C121).

**2. Signal template definition and rationale [Documented, C177].**
- The signal template is always taken from the high-statistics proton data sample. Only the negative-rigidity sample is used for the antiproton analysis.
- The reason is that the template variables have the same distribution for antiprotons and protons when both are reconstructed with the correct charge sign. This was verified with Monte Carlo and with antiproton and proton data.
- The fit floats the signal and background normalizations per rigidity bin.
- The negative sample contains antiprotons, electrons, pi- (and a negligible amount of K-), and charge-confusion protons.
  - Mesons are present only below 10 GV.
  - Electrons are present at all rigidities.
  - Charge confusion matters only at higher rigidity. A charge-confusion estimator reduces it to below 0.1% in all bins.

**3. Templates below and above 2.97 GV [Documented, C177].** Two rigidity regions use different templates.
- **1.00-2.97 GV:**
  - The fit variable is the mass distribution computed from the inner-tracker rigidity and the TOF velocity.
  - A TRD-estimator cut is applied.
  - Electron and pion templates are defined per rigidity bin from data selected with TRD, RICH and ECAL.
- **2.97-41.9 GV:**
  - The variables are the TRD estimator and the RICH velocity.
  - The pi- background is removed by a rigidity-dependent RICH velocity cut.
  - The electron template is defined per bin from ECAL-selected data. Monte Carlo verifies that the electron template shapes inside and outside the ECAL acceptance are identical.
- In total, 1.1e6 antiprotons are identified from 1.00 to 41.9 GV.
