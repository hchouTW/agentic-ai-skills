**1. Antiproton trigger efficiency** [Documented, C175]
- It is 80% above 10 GV and rises to 83% at 1 GV.
- This is the efficiency eps in the flux formula. It is a different quantity from the trigger-efficiency systematic, which is below 1% over the whole rigidity range and in every rotation [Documented, C121].

**2. Signal template: definition and reason** [Documented, C177]
- Only the negative-rigidity sample is used. It contains antiprotons, electrons, light negative mesons (pi-, with a negligible amount of K-) and charge-confusion protons.
- The signal and background yields are found per rigidity bin by fitting signal and background templates to data, with floating normalizations.
- The signal template is always defined from the high-statistics proton data sample.
- The reason given is that the template variables have the same distribution for antiprotons and protons when both are reconstructed with the correct charge sign. This was verified with Monte Carlo and with antiproton and proton data.
- A charge-confusion estimator, using the technique of an earlier AMS paper, reduces charge-confusion protons to below 0.1% in all rigidity bins. This is a bound after the estimator cut, not a rate before it.

**3. Templates below and above 2.97 GV** [Documented, C177]

| Item | 1.00-2.97 GV | 2.97-41.9 GV |
|---|---|---|
| Fit variables | Mass distribution computed from the inner-tracker rigidity and the TOF velocity, with a TRD estimator cut | TRD estimator and RICH velocity |
| Signal | Proton-data template | Proton-data template |
| Background templates | Electron and pion templates defined per rigidity bin from data selected with the TRD, RICH and ECAL | Electron template defined per bin from data selected with the ECAL |
| Pion handling | pi- is an explicit template | pi- is removed by a rigidity-dependent RICH velocity cut |
| Electron template check | Not stated in the ledger | Monte Carlo verifies that the electron template shapes inside and outside the ECAL acceptance are identical |

- Mesons are present only below 10 GV.
- Charge confusion becomes noticeable only at higher rigidity.
- Electrons are present at all rigidities.
- The fit yields 1.1x10^6 antiprotons in total over 1.00-41.9 GV.
