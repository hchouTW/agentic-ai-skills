**Nitrogen paper:** AMS, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), "Precision Measurement of Cosmic-Ray Nitrogen and its Primary and Secondary Components..." [Documented, S55; main article only, the Supplemental Material was not read].

**Sample, range, period** [Documented, C137]
- The paper quotes 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events. These are nitrogen nuclei after background subtraction.
- The rigidity range is 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, measured in 66 bins.
- The data are from the first five years on the ISS, May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016.
- The total flux error is 4% at 100 GV.
- A different count appears in the later Phys. Rept. review (S01): 3.9 million nitrogen nuclei [Documented, C91]. That is a review-level number and does not match the Letter's 2.2 x 10^6. For the nitrogen paper, use 2.2 x 10^6.

**Two-component fit** [Documented, C142]
- Over the whole rigidity range the flux is fitted as a weighted sum of a primary reference (oxygen) and a secondary reference (boron):
  Phi_N = Phi_PN + Phi_SN = (0.090 +- 0.002) x Phi_O + (0.62 +- 0.02) x Phi_B, with chi2/d.o.f. = 51/64.
- The same fit above 60 GV gives (0.083 +- 0.005) x Phi_O + (0.66 +- 0.04) x Phi_B, with chi2/d.o.f. = 18/25.
- The secondary share falls from about 70% at a few GV to below 30% above 1 TV.
- The paper presents the O coefficient as the N/O abundance ratio at the source.
- This is a descriptive fit. It assumes O has a purely primary shape and B a purely secondary shape. The fit quality does not test that assumption, and it does not prove a mechanism.
- The review quotes slightly different numbers: 0.092 +- 0.002 and 0.61 +- 0.02, with chi2/d.o.f. = 59/64 [Documented, C91]. Do not mix them with the Letter's numbers.

**What comes from the nitrogen paper itself**
- The measured nitrogen flux and the event sample.
- The two fit coefficients (0.090 and 0.62), their errors, and the chi2/d.o.f.
- The fit above 60 GV.
- The N spectral index above 8.48 GV, which hardens above about 100 GV and equals the He, C and O indices above about 700 GV.
- The secondary-fraction trend.

**What does not come from it** [Documented, C142]
- The oxygen flux Phi_O and the boron flux Phi_B are published AMS fluxes from earlier papers (Refs. [8,9]), used as inputs.
- The He, C, O and Li, Be, B spectral indices used for comparison also come from those earlier papers.
- The review's 0.092/0.61 values are review-level and not from the Letter.
