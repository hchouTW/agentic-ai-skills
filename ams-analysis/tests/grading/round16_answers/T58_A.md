**Scope.** I take "the AMS nitrogen flux paper" to be the dedicated Letter, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), source S55 in the skill's source index. It covers 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, with 2.2x10^6 N events from May 2011 to May 2016. I had no web access, and the ledger holds only the main article. The Supplemental Material was not read, so the per-bin systematic breakdown is [Unknown/needs input]. Every number below comes from the main text, not from the Supplemental Material, and is given at that level. Numbers from the Li/Be/B paper (S54) and from the Phys. Rept. review (S01) are different datasets and are not substituted here.

**Rigidity resolution** [Documented, C140]
- The rigidity is measured by the Tracker in the permanent-magnet field. Selection requires a downward-going inner-tracker track through L1 with track-fit chi2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate [Documented, C138].
- The tracker bending-coordinate resolution is 5.5 micrometre, stated to agree with simulation. Its derivation is in the Supplemental Material of another AMS paper (Ref. [16]), which was not read.
- The maximum detectable rigidity (MDR) is 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty. The amplitudes of the non-Gaussian tails carry a 10% uncertainty.
- The rigidity resolution is therefore not a Gaussian core only. The tails are modeled and given their own uncertainty, which matters for any charge-sign or confusion estimate.

**Bin-to-bin migration** [Documented, C140]
- Migration is corrected by unfolding. The paper cites a reference (Ref. [20]) for the procedure, which I did not read.
- The size of the correction, (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i, is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, -5% at 200 GV and -6% at 3.3 TV.
- The flux is Phi_i = N_i/(A_i eps_i T_i dR_i), with N_i the unfolded event count in each bin. The measurement is in 66 bins.
- Backgrounds are handled separately. Interactions of O, F and Ne between L1 and L2 are fitted with charge templates and are < 5%. Interactions above L1 are taken from simulation and are < 3% below 200 GV, rising to 6% at 3.3 TV. The total background-subtraction uncertainty is < 1.5% [Documented, C139]. I did not fold this into the migration correction, to avoid double counting.

**Systematic sizes quoted in the main text**
- Rigidity resolution function (this is the resolution and migration systematic): < 1% below 150 GV and 3% at 3.3 TV [Documented, C140].
- Absolute rigidity scale (tracker misalignment, magnetic-field map, temperature): < 0.6% up to 100 GV and 5% in the last bin, 1.3-3.3 TV [Documented, C141].
- Inelastic cross sections (N+C and N+O): < 3% up to 100 GV and 4% at 3 TV [Documented, C141].
- Other terms [Documented, C141]:
  - Trigger efficiency: < 0.7%.
  - Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: < 0.1% below 30 GV.
  - Effective-acceptance data/MC corrections: < 3% up to 500 GV and < 5% at 3.3 TV.
  - Reconstruction and selection: < 1%.
- Total flux error is 4% at 100 GV [Documented, C137].

**Caveats**
- These are per-source upper bounds from the text, not a quadrature total, and not per-bin values.
- The rigidity-scale and resolution terms are separate systematics for the same tracker, so they should not be treated as independent of each other or as a single combined number without the paper's tables.
- The scale systematic is largest at the highest rigidity, where the statistics are also smallest.
