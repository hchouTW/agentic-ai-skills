Source: AMS nitrogen flux letter, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), S55; 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, 66 bins, first five years of ISS data. Main article only; the Supplemental Material was not read.

**Bin-to-bin migration** [Documented, C140]
- Migration of events between rigidity bins was corrected by unfolding. The procedure is described in the paper by reference to Ref. [20]; I did not read that reference.
- The unfolding correction (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, -5% at 200 GV and -6% at 3.3 TV.
- Here N_i is the event count in bin i (aleph_i is the paper's symbol for the unfolded quantity). Which is which was not checked against the paper's definitions. I am not quoting any other bin values.

**Rigidity resolution** [Documented, C140]
- Tracker bending-coordinate resolution: 5.5 um, in agreement with simulation. The Letter states this value, and its derivation is in the Supplemental Material of Ref. [16], which I did not read.
- Maximum detectable rigidity (MDR): 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty.
- Amplitudes of the non-Gaussian tails: 10% uncertainty.
- Resulting flux systematic from the rigidity resolution function: less than 1% below 150 GV and 3% at 3.3 TV.
- The tail uncertainty matters because migration and charge confusion are tail effects. The Gaussian core alone does not describe them.

**Systematics quoted in the text** [Documented, C141]
- Absolute rigidity scale (tracker misalignment, magnetic field map and temperature): less than 0.6% up to 100 GV, rising to 5% in the last bin (1.3-3.3 TV).
- Inelastic cross sections (N+C and N+O): less than 3% up to 100 GV and 4% at 3 TV.
- For comparison, the other terms in the same claim are:
  - Trigger efficiency: less than 0.7%.
  - Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: below 0.1% below 30 GV.
  - Data-MC acceptance corrections: below 3% up to 500 GV and below 5% at 3.3 TV.
  - Reconstruction and selection: below 1%.
- The total flux error is 4% at 100 GV [Documented, C137]. The full error breakdown is in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.

**Context and caveats**
- The Letter does not state how the rigidity-scale error was split between its three sources, or its correlation across bins.
- The resolution, rigidity-scale and cross-section terms are different systematics. Do not add them in quadrature across bins as if independent without stating a correlation assumption.
- The review (Phys. Rept. 894, C91) quotes 3.9 million nitrogen nuclei and a 3.8% flux error at 100 GV. It is a separate review-level summary, not this Letter's 2.2 x 10^6 events and 4%. Do not mix the two.
- Browsing was not available. The source index was last verified 2026-09-20, so I make no claim about newer nitrogen results, and I did not check for supplements or superseding papers.
