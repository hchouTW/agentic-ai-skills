**Short answer.** The AMS collaboration reports that the positron-to-electron ratio R_e = Φ(e⁺)/Φ(e⁻) takes **Δt = 830 ± 30 days** (about 2.3 years) to go from 10% to 90% of its transition across the 2013 solar polarity reversal. Δt is energy independent over 1-21 GeV. [Documented, C90, S01; review-level summary]

**How AMS got the number.** It is a fit parameter of a descriptive function, not a direct measurement of an elapsed time.

1. **Data.** Electron and positron fluxes were measured per Bartels rotation (27 days). The Phys. Rept. review (S01) covers 79 rotations from May 2011 to May 2017, 23.5 million events, 1-50 GeV, 49 energy bins. Its R_e analysis uses 3871 independent measurements of R_e in energy and time. In R_e the short-term structures largely cancel, which exposes the charge-sign-dependent long-term evolution. [Documented, C89, C90, S01]
2. **Fit function.** A four-parameter logistic form per energy bin: R_e(t, E) = R0(E) [1 + C(E) / (exp(-(t - t_1/2(E)) / (Δt/Δ80)) + 1)], with Δ80 = 4.39. The constant Δ80 makes Δt the 10%-90% transition time. The four parameters are R0 (baseline), C (transition amplitude), t_1/2 (midpoint time) and Δt. The fits give χ²/d.o.f. of about 1 over 1-21 GeV. [Documented, C90, S01]
3. **Result.** Fitting each energy bin gives Δt independent of energy, 830 ± 30 days. [Documented, C90]

**Caveats.**
- The logistic form is the collaboration's descriptive choice, not a physical model. Δt is a parameter of that shape. Its meaning depends on the functional form.
- Δt and t_1/2 are only determined at low energy, where C is large. C is close to 1 at 1 GeV and consistent with zero above 20 GeV. Above 6 GeV the review fixes Δt to 830 days and uses an energy-dependent form for t_1/2. [Documented, C90]
- The midpoint delay follows t_1/2(E) - t_rev = τ (E/GeV)^ρ. Here t_rev = 1 July 2013 is a stated choice (the centre of the period without a well-defined solar polarity), so the delays t_1/2 - t_rev depend on it. Δt itself does not depend on t_rev. The fitted values are ρ = -0.33 ± 0.04 (stat) (+0.08, -0.15) (syst) and τ = 580 ± 19 (stat) ± 136 (syst) days. Quote both errors, because the systematic is large. [Documented, C90]
- The source ledger does not show how errors are correlated across bins, or any trial-factor treatment. Whether the quoted 830 ± 30 includes correlated systematics is [Unknown/needs input]. The ledger describes this as a review-level summary of fits, not a main-article claim. The ledger's check of this result is against the review's rendered pages, not against a dedicated paper.
- The physical reading is that charge-sign-dependent modulation arises from particle drift in the heliospheric magnetic field. That is the collaboration's qualitative interpretation and a separate model step from the fit. [Documented, C89, C90]
