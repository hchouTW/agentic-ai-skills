# Measurement Brief: Helium-4 Flux, 2 GV to 3 TV, from Public AMS Data

Labels: [Documented, Cxx] = claim ID in source-index; [General method]; [Proposal]; [Unknown/needs input]. No AMS selection cut, efficiency, acceptance or systematic size is invented here; quantities are symbolic unless a claim supports them.

## 0. Two issues to settle before anything else

1. **"Public AMS data" fixes the level of the measurement.** [Unknown/needs input] AMS event-level data, production passes, conditions databases, good-run lists and the Supplemental Material cut details are not available in this setting. Two different jobs are therefore possible:
   - **(A) Flux-level work** (what a public-data analysis can honestly be): take published He flux values with their uncertainties and covariance, and rebin, convert variable, fit or compare. Level = `flux`. The target is a re-use of the AMS result, not a new measurement.
   - **(B) Event-level measurement** (selection, efficiency, acceptance, unfolding from counts). This is an internal-collaboration analysis. Without event-level access it can only be written as a specification with symbolic inputs.
   This brief is written as a **specification for (B) with a clearly marked fallback (A)**. Say which you are doing; do not present (A) as an independent measurement.
2. **Species vs isotope.** AMS He flux is elemental (|Z| = 2). Calling it "helium-4" is an assumption: ³He is a non-zero component. The elemental flux is the estimand unless ³He/⁴He is separated (a different analysis, with a 1.9-21 GV isotope range for ⁴He [Documented, C09]). Quote "He (|Z| = 2)" and treat the ³He fraction as a systematic (see section 8).

## 1. Estimand and target

- **Target / level:** differential flux, `flux` level (top-of-instrument, not corrected to local interstellar spectrum; no solar-modulation model applied).
- **Estimand:** `Φ_He(R)` in m⁻² sr⁻¹ s⁻¹ GV⁻¹ for primary He nuclei (|Z| = 2, A treated as 4 only for conversions), as a function of absolute rigidity `R`, for the stated data period, averaged over that period (time-averaged unless time-resolved bins are chosen; see [time-dependent-analysis] if so).
- **Variable / unit / range:** rigidity, GV, **2 GV to 3 TV**. [General method], via `scripts/ams_kinematics.py`, with |Z| = 2, A = 4, m = 3.7274 GeV:
  - R = 2 GV: p = 4.0 GeV/c; T/A = 0.435 GeV/n.
  - R = 3000 GV: p = 6000 GeV/c; T/A = 1499.07 GeV/n.
  Rigidity is not momentum (`p = |Z|R`), and not T/A; a flux in T/A needs the Jacobian and an assumed A (script output: dR/d(T/A) factor 2.73 at 2 GV and 2.00 at 3 TV), not a relabeling of bin edges.
- **Bins:** [Proposal] choose edges on a fixed rigidity grid (log-spaced) with widths set relative to the resolution (see section 5); [Unknown/needs input] the AMS bin edges for He are not reproduced here. Published AMS He analyses used 68 bins over 1.9 GV-3 TV (30-month paper) [Documented, C59] and 67 bins over 2.2 GV-3.0 TV (five-year He, C, O paper) [Documented, C62]; those are choices of those analyses.
- **Range caveat:** [Documented, C59, C62] the public He results cover 1.9 GV-3 TV (first 30 months) and 2.2 GV-3 TV (first five years). A **2 GV lower edge** is inside the first paper's range but below the five-year paper's first bin; it must be matched to the bins of whichever paper is the source. The 3 TV upper edge coincides with the published upper edge, and is at the edge of sensitivity: the maximum detectable rigidity for He is 3.2 TV [Documented, C61, C62, C63], so the top bins are resolution-dominated (section 5).
- **Data scope:** data (not MC) for the measurement, MC for acceptance/response; **period** [Unknown/needs input]: 30 months (19 May 2011 to 26 Nov 2013 is the window of the proton paper, C31; the helium paper's own period should be read from S07), five years (S11), or later. State one period and never mix; if time-resolved, use Bartels-rotation or daily blocks (the daily He flux used a different, L1-only selection [Documented, C54]).

## 4. Signal, backgrounds, controls

| Background | Entry mechanism | Estimator / control | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proton (|Z| = 1) contamination | Charge-estimator tails; rigidity-dependent | Charge-distribution template fit with Z = 1 and Z = 2 shapes in control; subtract with uncertainty | MC closure; template-alternative test |
| Heavier-nucleus fragmentation to He above the detector (Li and up, mainly C, O) | Interactions in upper material | Template fit of L1 charge distributions or charge-changing survival-probability method | Injected-fraction closure |
| Interactions inside the detector (He survival) | Charge-changing between layers | Charge consistency among L1, TOF, L9, plus survival-probability correction | Horizontal-pointing sample |
| Charge confusion / rigidity tail | Finite resolution, scattering | Tail from data-driven or validated-MC; **not** the Gaussian core | Tail validation across rigidity |
| Albedo/secondaries at low R | Near and below cutoff | Cutoff safety factor scan | Rate vs R/R_c |

[Documented, C59] In the 30-month He analysis, proton contamination was below 10⁻⁴ and He from heavier-nucleus interactions at the top of AMS below 10⁻³, both subtracted with their uncertainties; [Documented, C62] He purity above 99.9% in the five-year paper. These are properties of those selections and do not transfer to another selection or period.

Template fits (if used): provenance of every template without circularity; finite-template statistics (Barlow-Beeston or Conway); per-rigidity-bin templates; signal contamination of control samples; goodness of fit; alternate templates. [General method]

## 5. Efficiency, acceptance, livetime/exposure and response (kept distinct)

- **Efficiency** `ε_j`: conditional factors above (trigger, reconstruction, charge, quality), each with named denominator; data/MC scale factors with their own uncertainties. Trigger efficiency is rigidity-dependent; prescaled samples limit it at the top [Documented, C60 for the 30-month paper (below 0.2% under 100 GV, 1% at 3 TV)]; the published-paper values are not to be reused for another selection.
- **Acceptance** `A_j` (m² sr): from the MC generation record (surface, solid angle, generated spectrum and reweighting, geometry/material version). Interaction/survival correction kept separate, with a cross-section model variation (He inelastic cross section measured in flight; Glauber-Gribov scaled [Documented, C60, C80]).
- **Livetime and geomagnetic transmission** `T_j`: livetime-weighted seconds above cutoff in each bin, using the stated cutoff model and safety factor. Above ~30 GV the collection time is essentially constant [Documented, C59, C62: 6.29×10⁷ s for 30 months; 1.23×10⁸ s for five years]; below it, it depends strongly on rigidity (the 2 GV edge is the cutoff-limited end). Exposure `Σ_t A_eff(t) T_live(t) × transmission` already contains acceptance and livetime; do not multiply them again.
- **Response** `R_ij = P(R_meas in i | R_true in j, selected)`: conditional on selected events with inefficiency in `A`/`ε` (state which, and run `scripts/validate_response.py`; includes under/overflow, tails and charge migration). Orientation and normalization declared.
- **Resolution and migration are not small at the ends of this range.** [Documented, C61, C59] In the 30-month He paper the Gaussian core was 9% at 10 GV, 14% at 100 GV, 20% at 400 GV, 64% at 2 TV, with non-Gaussian tails (3% to 11%), MDR 3.2 ± 0.16 TV; the migration correction was +15% at 2 GV and -19% at 3 TV. Hence the diagonal estimator `(n_i - b_i)/(ΔR_i A ε T)` is **not valid** here; unfolding or forward folding with the full response is required. [Documented, C31/C59 style: iterative unfolding; the antiproton paper stops at 0.1% agreement between steps, C23, not stated for He]
- **Bin centers:** use the bin-average convention for a steeply falling spectrum, not arithmetic centers. [General method]

## 6. Inference

- **Estimand-first choice:** a model-independent spectrum → regularized/iterative unfolding with full covariance; a spectral parameter (e.g. a hardening) → forward-folded likelihood. State which. [General method] The AMS He flux has been published with unfolding. [Documented, C59, C62]
- **Likelihood:** `μ_i = Σ_j R_ij A_j ε_j T_j Φ_j ΔR_j + Σ_k b_ik`, binned Poisson with auxiliary constraints. [General method]
- **Counts:** bulk bins have large counts (millions of He nuclei in total [Documented, C59, C62: 5.0×10⁷ and 9×10⁷ events]); the **high-rigidity bins near 3 TV have few events**, so use Poisson/exact or toy-validated intervals there, not Gaussian errors or `S/√B`. [General method]
- **Unfolding validation:** nominal closure, reweighted-spectrum closure, distorted-spectrum stress tests, response perturbation (core and tail), toys through the full unfolding, prior-dependence spread, stability vs binning; choose the regularization/iteration criterion before looking at the final spectrum.
- **Output:** flux with full statistical plus systematic covariance (not per-bin percentages), response, and exposure, so others can forward-fold.

## 8. Systematics ledger (one row per effect, with how it is applied)

| Effect | Representation / applied via | Correlation across bins | Validation / double-counting check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger efficiency | Nuisance on normalization; shape at high R | Smooth, partial | Prescaled/unbiased sample; not also in acceptance |
| Acceptance (MC, data/MC correction) | Nuisance (normalization) | Largely correlated | Scale-factor vs MC-model variation not both counting the same discrepancy |
| Inelastic cross sections / survival | Alternate sample/nuisance | Partial | Horizontal-pointing survival method |
| Geomagnetic cutoff factor and model | Alternate selection (1.0-1.4 scan documented) | Concentrated at low R | Compare with data-derived cutoff |
| Rigidity resolution core and tail | **Migration** (response variation: core, tail amplitude) | Partial, grows with R | Tail validated vs data (layer-subset residuals, L1-L8 vs L2-L9) |
| Absolute rigidity scale / alignment | **Migration** (shifted R, re-unfold), never a final-value rescaling | Strongly correlated, dominant at the top | Tracker/ECAL E/p check of e± [Documented, C64]; the scale is the largest high-R systematic [Documented, C64] |
| Background subtraction (p, heavier nuclei) | Nuisance in template fit | Partial | Alternate templates |
| ³He/⁴He composition | Alternate sample (ratio 0 to a stated maximum) | Smooth | Convert only with stated A |
| Unfolding (method, prior, regularization) | Alternate method/prior; covariance | Bin-to-bin correlated | Closure suite |
| Bin width/binning | Alternate bins | n/a | Stability |
| Time/period dependence | Period splits | Correlated | Time-stability of an abundant control rate |

[Documented, C60] The 30-month He paper's ledger covers: trigger, cutoff factor, acceptance, interaction cross section, ³He/⁴He composition, resolution function, unfolding procedure, rigidity scale, and binning. Its sizes (e.g. rigidity scale below 0.6% up to 100 GV and 6% at 3 TV) are for that analysis only. Combine covariances, not variances; no quadrature sum for unknown correlations.

## 9. Validation (metric, threshold, action)

[Proposal] thresholds to be fixed in advance:
- MC closure of the full chain (truth recovery per bin): deviation beyond a pre-set fraction of the statistical error, or beyond the assigned systematic → add an uncertainty or change method.
- Time stability of rate/efficiency after livetime and cutoff correction: drift beyond the pre-set band → split the period or add a time-dependent systematic.
- Selection-variation scan (loose/tight charge and quality, track configuration, detector region, upward/downward, geometry): spread vs statistical error; failure → add systematic.
- Cutoff-factor scan 1.0-1.4: flux change beyond pre-set tolerance at the low-R edge → raise safety factor or restrict range.
- Rigidity-scale check: shift in 1/R of a stated size → propagate via migration.
- Plausibility comparison with published AMS He: agreement is a sanity check, not validation.
