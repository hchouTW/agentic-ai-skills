# Measurement brief: helium-4 flux, 2 GV to 3 TV, public AMS data

Labels: [Documented, Cxx] = public AMS source via the skill's source-index (verification level noted), [General method], [Proposal], [Unknown/needs input]. No AMS selection cut values, efficiencies, acceptances or livetimes are invented here; where they are needed they are symbols.

## 0. Blocking caveat: what "public AMS data" can support

[Unknown/needs input] Event-level AMS data, calibration constants, production software and good-run lists are not available to this skill and are not assumed public. Public material is the published papers (and whatever tabulated fluxes they release). Two consequences:

- With published results only, the achievable deliverable is a comparison, reinterpretation or forward-folding fit of the published He flux and its published uncertainties. It is not an independent re-measurement of the flux from counts.
- The measurement design below is the specification an analyst with event-level access would follow. Confirm which one you mean before spending effort. Under "public only", the estimand and range below stay the same, but sections 5 to 8 become "use published values and their stated systematics", not "derive".

## 1. Estimand and level

Differential flux of cosmic-ray helium nuclei at the top of the instrument (not corrected to the local interstellar spectrum; no solar-modulation model applied), Phi(R) in m^-2 sr^-1 s^-1 GV^-1, integrated over the stated data period, as a function of absolute rigidity R = pc/(|Z|e) in GV, from 2 GV to 3 TV. Level: flux. Target: flux (not a ratio).

- Species: |Z| = 2, charge sign +1. Elemental He (3He + 4He), not isotope-resolved. Your brief says "helium-4": the AMS He measurement is elemental. Treating it as 4He needs an assumed 3He/4He composition and an uncertainty (in the published helium analysis the 3He/4He ratio was varied from 0 to 0.2 as a systematic, 0.3% effect; [Documented, C60], first 30 months, S07, main article only). If you want a true 4He-only flux, that is an isotope analysis limited to low rigidity (S12 covers 4He at 2.1-21 GV, [Documented, C09], abstract-level) and cannot reach 3 TV. State which is wanted.
- Variable conversion [General method, scripts/ams_kinematics.py, Z = 2, A = 4, m = 3.7274 GeV]: R = 2 GV is p = 4 GeV/c, T/A = 0.4350 GeV/n; R = 3000 GV is p = 6000 GeV/c, T/A = 1499.07 GeV/n. Flux Jacobians dR/d(T/A) are 2.734 and 2.000. Reporting in T/A requires the A assumption above and a Jacobian applied to bins, not relabeling.
- Range check: AMS published He over 1.9 GV to 3 TV ([Documented, C59/C60, S07, first 30 months]) and 2.2 GV to 3.0 TV for the five-year He/C/O paper ([Documented, C62, S11]). 2 GV is therefore not a published bin edge; your lower edge sits inside the first published bin of S07 and below S11's first edge. Choose edges that coincide with published edges if you want to compare bin by bin. [Unknown/needs input]: the published bin edges themselves are not in this skill.
- The 3 TV top end is near the He maximum detectable rigidity, 3.2 +- 0.16 TV ([Documented, C61], S07; C63 for the review). Edge bins are dominated by resolution tails and migration (see section 6).

## 2. Data scope

- Data or MC: data for the flux, MC for acceptance and response.
- Period: [Unknown/needs input]. The published He periods are the first 30 months (S07, C59) and the first five years (S11, C62). A time-resolved or longer-period result is a different, time-dependent analysis (use the time-dependent reference).
- Public vs internal: public (see section 0). Hardware eras and reconstruction/production versions: [Unknown/needs input]; MC and data versions must match.

## 3. Observables and subsystem roles

[Documented, C13; detector reference] Tracker (with magnet): rigidity and charge sign, plus |Z| from dE/dx. TOF: direction, beta, |Z|, trigger. RICH: beta and |Z| (not needed for rigidity but a charge cross-check). ACC: side-entry veto. TRD: |Z| support. ECAL: not a nucleus charge estimator, but electrons and positrons in ECAL are used for the tracker rigidity scale check ([Documented, C64], review). Charge sign comes only from the Tracker; for He the relevant concern is charge confusion in the tail (section 5).

## 4. Selection (symbolic; conditional denominators named)

[Documented practice, S07 first 30 months, C59, not a universal AMS standard] Collection time counts only seconds in normal operating conditions, instrument pointing within 40 degrees of the local zenith, DAQ livetime above 50%, outside the South Atlantic Anomaly; downward-going events with an inner-tracker track of charge compatible with Z = 2; track through L1 to L9 with chi^2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate; charges from L1, upper TOF, lower TOF and L9 compatible with Z = 2 to remove interacting candidates; rigidity above 1.2 times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff in the field of view (IGRF back-tracing). The five-year paper used the same selection ([Documented, C62]). The daily He analysis used a different, L1-only selection and data-derived cutoff (C54), so there is no single AMS standard.

| Cut | Level | Target failure mode | Conditional denominator | Efficiency source | Validation (metric / trigger / action) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality time and pointing | exposure | bad conditions, low-livetime | calendar time | livetime accounting, not an efficiency | Rate stability vs time; drift beyond statistical scatter, split the period or add systematic |
| Downward-going | event | albedo, upward | triggered events | hybrid | Upward/downward split; mismatch beyond stat, add systematic |
| Tracker track, chi^2 | track | bad fits, tail events | Z = 2 candidates with L1-L9 track | hybrid, MC with data scale factor | Alternate layer configuration; shift beyond stat, add systematic |

Trigger: efficiency from the unbiased (prescaled) sample, which limits statistics at high rigidity ([Documented, C60]: below 0.2% under 100 GV, 1% at 3 TV, limited by the 1% prescaled sample). Do not multiply conditional efficiencies as independent; each is measured with the named denominator above.

## 5. Backgrounds and tails

Background registry (each with estimator, control, closure test):

- Proton contamination (Z = 1 charge-estimator tail and rigidity-sign confusion): [Documented, C59] below 1e-4 (stated in S07, first 30 months, for the selected sample; high-R behavior depends on the charge-confusion tail).
- Helium from fragmentation of heavier nuclei (C, O, and so on) above L1 and in the upper detector: [Documented, C59] below 1e-3 in S07. Estimate by template or data-driven charge-ladder method; closure on MC with injected heavier-nuclei fraction.
- Adjacent-charge leakage (Li, Be, B into Z = 2 and He into Z = 3 loss): template fit of the charge estimator in control sample; closure vs window variations.
- Charge-sign confusion (He- or wrong-sign): a tail phenomenon. Reject any estimate from the Gaussian core of the rigidity resolution; require data-driven or MC-tail-validated estimate with independent check. Its effect at high rigidity is probably small for He flux (positive nucleus, steeply falling spectrum, antihelium negligible by orders of magnitude) but must be shown, not assumed. [Proposal]: validate by comparing rigidity from L1-L8 and L2-L9 subsets and from alternate layer configurations.
- Albedo and secondaries near the cutoff, mostly below about 5 GV: handled by the cutoff factor (section 4).

## 6. Efficiency, acceptance, livetime, response (kept distinct)

- Exposure E_j = A_j * eps_j * T_j with A_j effective acceptance (m^2 sr) from MC generation record, eps_j product of conditional efficiencies with data/MC scale factors, T_j livetime and geomagnetic transmission (the fraction of time each rigidity is above SF times cutoff). Exposure already contains acceptance and livetime; do not apply them again in the estimator.
- Survival and interaction correction: a He inelastic cross-section model is required (material is mostly carbon and aluminum; [Documented, C80 review]). AMS measures He survival probabilities in flight with horizontal-pointing data and scales Glauber-Gribov cross sections by 1.05 to 1.20 ([Documented, C60]; effect 1% under 100 GV, 2% at 3 TV). This is a separate correction from efficiency.
- Response: R_ij = P(R_meas in bin i | R_true in bin j, selected), including non-Gaussian tails, with the declared convention (orientation, normalization conditional on selected, efficiency and acceptance outside the matrix). The AMS He resolution (Gaussian core sigma/(1/R) = 9% at 10 GV, 14% at 100 GV, 20% at 400 GV, 64% at 2 TV; non-Gaussian tails 3% to 11%) comes from simulation checked in four ways with data ([Documented, C61]; first 30 months; S07). These numbers are the paper's, not a recommendation for new selection.
- Migration is not small and must be unfolded; the diagonal estimator is invalid at high rigidity where the resolution is 20% to 64% in 1/R and the spectrum is steep. For comparison, carbon unfolding corrections were +14% at 3 GV, +6% at 5 GV, -4% at 150 GV and -6% at 3 TV ([Documented, C62]); He-specific numbers must be read from S07/S11, [Unknown/needs input].
- Estimator [Proposal]: iterative unfolding as in the published papers, or forward folding of a smooth parametric flux (broken power law) if only public tables are available; the estimand and response are fixed first, then the algorithm. Two unfolding procedures should be compared (AMS did so; [Documented, C60], difference 0.25% at 2 GV, below 0.1% from 2.5 to 200 GV, 0.4% at 3 TV).

## 7. Inference and systematics

Estimator: Phi_j from N_i corrected for migration, divided by A_j eps_j T_j and bin width; statistical covariance from the unfolding (toys), systematics through the response or alternate samples, not as a final-value rescaling (a scale or resolution systematic is applied by migration).

Systematic registry (one row per effect, category stated to avoid double counting; sizes are the published ones with context [Documented, C60, S07, first 30 months, He], and must not be assumed for a new analysis):

| Source | How applied | Published size (S07) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger efficiency | normalization nuisance | below 0.2% under 100 GV, 1% at 3 TV |
| Cutoff factor 1.0-1.4 | alternate selection | below 0.1% |
| Acceptance (data/MC differences) | normalization nuisance | below 1% under 200 GV, 1.4% at 3 TV |
| Inelastic interaction / survival | cross-section variation | 1% under 100 GV, 2% at 3 TV |
| 3He/4He composition | alternate composition 0-0.2 | 0.3% |
| Rigidity resolution (core 5%, tail 10%) | migration variation | below 1% under 400 GV, 3.5% at 3 TV |
| Unfolding procedure difference | alternate method | 0.25% at 2 GV to 0.4% at 3 TV |
| Absolute rigidity scale | scale shift through migration | largest at the highest rigidity; see below |
| Background (charge confusion, fragmentation, proton) | template/closure | [Unknown/needs input] |

For the rigidity scale: [Documented, C64 review] a coherent inner-tracker shift below 0.5 micrometre moves the scale by 10% at 1 TV, checked in flight with E/p of e+- in 72 bins over 2 to 300 GeV (accuracy 0.033 TV^-1, 3% at 1 TV, first seven years). Extrapolation to He at 3 TV (R = 3000 GV, p = 6000 GeV/c, outside the electron calibration range) is the dominant uncertainty at the top and is not an assumption to hide. Correlations across bins: scale, acceptance and cross-section shifts are fully correlated across rigidity (smooth), trigger and statistical terms are bin-local; give a covariance, not only diagonal errors. Low counts at the top bins: use exact Poisson or toy-validated intervals, not Gaussian errors or Wilks.

Quadrature combination of independent sources is the published practice (C23 for antiprotons, C25 for positrons, C33 for isotopes); verify independence per source.

## 8. Validation (metric, threshold, action)

- Closure: MC injected spectrum with realistic and perturbed shapes through the full unfolding; truth recovery within stat uncertainty per bin; failing bin triggers a response or binning change, or an added systematic.
- Stability: flux in time blocks (for example by Bartels rotation or yearly), after livetime and cutoff correction; trigger [Proposal]: chi^2 p-value below 0.01 or a coherent drift beyond statistics, then split periods or add a time systematic.
- Splits: detector region, incident angle, upward/downward, alternate layer sets (L1-L8 versus L2-L9), loose versus tight charge window.
- Data/MC: trigger and charge-efficiency scale factors, tail of the resolution function against L1-L8 vs L2-L9 rigidity comparison ([Documented, C63] describes this check used for protons).
- Comparison with published He fluxes is a plausibility check only; agreement is not validation.

## 9. Outputs

Flux table with bin edges (rigidity), flux, statistical error, systematic error per source, full covariance; exposure and response provided for forward-folding; statement of the variable (R, GV), the species (elemental He), the period, and the cutoff safety factor; top-of-instrument convention.

## 10. Unresolved inputs that change the result

1. Whether the target is the elemental He flux (assumed) or a 4He-only flux.
2. Public-table reanalysis or event-level measurement (section 0).
3. Data period and hardware era; reconstruction and MC versions.
4. Bin edges (published edges starting at 1.9 or 2.2 GV; your 2 GV edge is not one of them).
5. Trigger configuration, prescale handling, and the efficiency and background measurements and their control samples.
6. Response matrix and tail validation at 1 to 3 TV; rigidity-scale correction near 3 TV.
7. Cutoff model and safety factor.
