## 1. The 2013 positron-fraction paper is not the latest

It measured the positron fraction from 0.5 to 350 GeV on 6.8×10⁶ positron and electron events. This is the first AMS result. The source index records it as superseded in range and statistics by the 2014 papers (C03). I read only its abstract and metadata, not the paper body.

Later AMS publications, with their records in the source index:

- **2014 positron fraction.** PRL 113, 121101 is the high-statistics positron fraction, 0.5-500 GeV. The index lists it, together with the 2014 electron and positron flux papers (PRL 113, 121102 and PRL 113, 221102), at metadata-only level (S03). I would not quote numbers from it.
- **2019 positron flux.** PRL 122, 041102 reports 1.9 million positrons up to 1 TeV. It finds a rise starting near 25 GeV, a drop-off above about 284 GeV, and a source-term cutoff at more than 4σ [Documented, C04, S04].

- **2023 time structures.** PRL 131, 151002 covers temporal structures in positron spectra and charge-sign effects (S46).
