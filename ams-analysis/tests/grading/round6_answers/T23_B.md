The ACC (Anti-Coincidence Counters) is AMS-02's side-entry veto. Its job is to reject particles that enter or leave the Tracker volume transversely, through the magnet sides, and to flag events with extra activity outside the nominal acceptance. [Documented, C13, C69]

**Layout and mechanism.** The ACC is a ring of scintillator panels around the Tracker inside the magnet bore. The review describes sixteen curved panels, each 800 mm long. Wavelength-shifting fibres carry the light to PMTs, and each PMT serves two adjacent panels. Vertical edges are tongue-and-groove, so a particle crossing a seam is seen in two panels. A thresholded signal in these panels becomes an event-level veto decision. [Documented, C69]

**Role in the trigger.** For |Z| = 1 particles, the level-1 trigger requires four TOF signals and no ACC signal. [Documented, C69, C73]

**Efficiency.** The papers quote an ACC efficiency of 0.99999 for rejecting side-entering particles. This applies to the stated analyses and was a pre-flight long-duration test result. It is not a universal figure, and the papers' definition of inefficiency is not given in the sources I have. [Documented, C21, C69]

**What it does not do.** The ACC gives no charge sign, no |Z|, no β, and no direction. It is not a lepton/hadron discriminator, and it is a veto only. Charge sign comes only from the Tracker and magnet. Side-entry rejection is also supported by geometry from the Tracker, TRD and RICH, and by shower information from the ECAL, but the ACC is the primary veto. [Documented, C13; [General method] for the matrix framing]

**Analysis caveats.** [General method]
- The veto has a cost. Accidental or backsplash activity can veto good events. Backsplash from ECAL showers at high energy gives an energy-dependent inefficiency, and accidental activity varies with orbit and rate. This loss is an efficiency. It must be measured as a conditional efficiency and checked against rigidity, energy and time.
- Do not count the ACC veto both in the geometric acceptance and in the efficiency. That is double counting.
- Hardware inefficiency lets false side-entering events through, so it needs a data-driven check.
