**Short answer.** In the AMS Phys. Rept. review, the positron-to-electron ratio R_e = Φ(e⁺)/Φ(e⁻) takes **Δt = 830 ± 30 days** to go through the 2013 solar polarity reversal. That is the 10%-90% transition time, and the review finds it independent of energy [Documented, C90, S01; review-level, scope below].

**How AMS got the number.** The value comes from a descriptive fit, not a direct count of days.

1. **Data.** The review reports 3871 independent R_e measurements, binned in energy and time. The underlying lepton time-structure data are Bartels-rotation (27-day) flux measurements over May 2011 to May 2017, 1-50 GeV in 49 energy bins [Documented, C89, S01; the underlying paper is S42]. Taking the ratio of the e⁺ and e⁻ fluxes largely cancels the short-term structures common to both species. Per the review, simultaneous e⁺ and e⁻ measurement isolates the charge-sign-dependent drift effect [Documented, C89].
2. **Fit function.** In each energy bin, the ratio is fitted with a four-parameter logistic (sigmoid) function:
   R_e(t,E) = R0(E) [1 + C(E) / (exp(-(t - t_1/2(E)) / (Δt/Δ80)) + 1)], with Δ80 = 4.39. The parameters are R0 (baseline), C (transition amplitude), t_1/2 (midpoint time) and Δt (width). Δ80 = 4.39 is chosen so that Δt is the 10%-90% transition time [Documented, C90].
3. **Result.** The fits give chi2/d.o.f. of about 1 over 1-21 GeV, and Δt comes out energy independent at 830 ± 30 days [Documented, C90].

**Related results from the same fit** [Documented, C90]:
- The midpoint delay follows t_1/2(E) − t_rev = τ (E/GeV)^ρ, with ρ = −0.33 ± 0.04 (stat) (+0.08, −0.15) (syst) and τ = 580 ± 19 (stat) ± 136 (syst) days. The midpoint shifts by 260 ± 30 days between 1 and 6 GeV.
- The transition amplitude C is close to 1 at 1 GeV, falls with energy, and is consistent with zero above 20 GeV. The review calls this qualitatively consistent with drift-including solar modulation models.

**Caveats.**
- The source is the Phys. Rept. 894 review (S01), checked in the ledger against the rendered PDF pages on 2026-09-21. The ledger lists no separate main-article claim for this specific logistic fit.
- The logistic form is the collaboration's descriptive choice, not a physical model, and the fit does not by itself say the transition is physically logistic.
- t_rev = 1 July 2013 is a stated choice, the centre of the period with no well-defined solar polarity. It affects the midpoint delays (t_1/2 − t_rev), not the width Δt.
- Δt and t_1/2 are well determined only at low energy, where C is large. Above 6 GeV the review fixes Δt to 830 days and uses its Eq. 18 for t_1/2. The "energy independent" statement therefore holds where the transition is measurable, and I would not extrapolate it.
- Quote both statistical and systematic errors for ρ and τ, because the systematic errors are large.
- I did not find a trial factor or a model of correlated errors across bins described in the text read. That is [Unknown/needs input], not proof of absence.
- Why Δt is energy independent while the delay depends on energy is a modulation-model question. The measurement does not settle it.
- The ledger was last verified 2026-09-20. I did not check for newer or superseding AMS papers, and I make no claim about the current status.
