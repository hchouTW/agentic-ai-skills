**Short answer.** AMS searched for strangelets and found none. That is a null result under stated assumptions, not a blanket exclusion of strangelets. The numerical limits are in figures that the skill's index does not transcribe, so I cannot give you the limit values.

**What was done** [Documented, C87; source S01, the AMS Phys. Rept. 894 review of the first seven years, full text read, Section 14 and Figs. 102-105]:
- Strangelet candidates are defined by Z/A < 0.1.
- Rigidity comes from the inner tracker (layers L2-L8) and velocity from the TOF. The finite TOF velocity resolution (0.01 < Delta beta/beta^2 < 0.02) limits the search to events with measured beta < 0.8.
- The Z/A distribution was measured for Z = 2 (80 million events) and Z = 3-8 (8 million events) nuclei. No events with Z/A < 0.1 were found.
- 95% C.L. flux upper limits were set for Z = 2 to 8.

**How strong the exclusion is** [Documented, C87, review-level statements]:
- The review says the Z = 2 limit is about two orders of magnitude more sensitive than PAMELA.
- It also says the Z = 2 limit is nine orders of magnitude below the helium flux.
- It says strangelets with 2 <= Z <= 8 are excluded well below the model prediction cited there.
- The limit values themselves are in figures and are not transcribed in the index. I will not quote a flux number or a Z-by-Z limit.

**Limits on the claim** (all from the C87 limitations):
- The flux limits assume a rigidity dependence similar to that of primary cosmic rays. They use a Monte Carlo estimate of the strangelet acceptance, and the index does not describe that acceptance. A different spectrum or acceptance would change the limit.
- The exclusion against a model is against one cited prediction, not a general statement. It covers 2 <= Z <= 8 with beta < 0.8 only. It does not cover heavier or lighter candidates, higher beta, or other exotic matter.
- The tail of any Z/A mis-measurement is a tail phenomenon. The index does not give the tail validation, so I cannot assess it.
- The data period is the review's first seven years, from 2011-05 to about 2018. The review is a summary. The index records no dedicated AMS strangelet research paper, and I did not check for one.
- The search is in a review (Tier 1 AMS collaboration, full text read 2026-09-21, index last verified 2026-09-20). I could not check for newer or superseding results, so I make no claim about currency. If you need the latest status, check INSPIRE-HEP and ams02.space.
