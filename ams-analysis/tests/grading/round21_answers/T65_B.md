**Short answer:** AMS reported a null strangelet search with 95% C.L. flux upper limits for Z = 2 to 8. The index records this only in the Physics Reports review of the first seven years, not in a dedicated main-article PRL. "Excluded" is therefore accurate only for those charges and for the assumptions below. It does not exclude strangelets in general.

**[Documented, C87, S01 (Phys. Rept. 894, 1, 2021; full-text read; data from 2011-05 to about 2018, the first seven years)]**
- **Signature:** strangelets are characterized by Z/A < 0.1, an anomalously low charge-to-mass ratio.
- **Method:** rigidity comes from the inner tracker (L2-L8). Velocity comes from the TOF. Because of the finite TOF velocity resolution (0.01 < Δβ/β² < 0.02), the search is limited to events with measured β < 0.8.
- **Samples:** the Z/A distribution was measured for Z = 2 nuclei (80 million events) and for Z = 3-8 nuclei (8 million events).
- **Result:** no events with Z/A < 0.1 were found.
- **Limits:** these are 95% C.L. flux upper limits for Z = 2 to 8. They assume a rigidity dependence similar to that of the primary cosmic rays. The acceptance for strangelets was estimated with Monte Carlo.
- **Strength, as the review states it:** the Z = 2 limit is two orders of magnitude more sensitive than PAMELA and nine orders of magnitude lower than the helium flux. The review also states that 2 ≤ Z ≤ 8 strangelets are excluded well below the model prediction it cites.

**How strong, and the caveats**
- The limit values themselves are in the review's figures (Figs. 102-105). The index does not transcribe them, so I give no numeric flux limit. The "orders of magnitude" figures are the review's own comparisons.
- **Conditional on assumptions:** the limit depends on the assumed primary-like rigidity spectrum and on the Monte Carlo strangelet acceptance. The index does not describe that acceptance model.
- **Limited coverage:** the search covers Z = 2-8 and β < 0.8 only. It says nothing about higher charges, or about strangelets with β > 0.8 where the velocity resolution cannot test Z/A.
- **Model-relative exclusion:** the exclusion is against one cited model prediction, not a general statement. Different strangelet models could still lie below the limit.
- **Null result, not a proof of absence:** it is not a claim about all exotic matter.
- **Source level:** the claim is review-level (Tier 1). The index records no dedicated main-article strangelet paper, and it does not rule one out. For "latest" or "is there a newer or superseding AMS strangelet result" I could not check INSPIRE or ams02.space (no browsing here).
