All four points are from the AMS Na/Al/N Letter (S59, PRL 127, 021101, 2021). The ledger read the main article only, not the Supplemental Material. My index was last verified 2026-09-20 and I did no live check, so I make no currency claim.

**1. Data sample and rigidity range** [Documented, C162]
- AMS collected 0.46 million Na and 0.51 million Al nuclei.
- The rigidity range is 2.15 GV to 3.0 TV.
- The data span the first 8.5 years on the ISS, 19 May 2011 to 30 October 2019. That period contained 1.50 x 10^11 cosmic-ray events in total.
- The flux is measured in 49 rigidity bins.
- Total flux errors at 100 GV are 5.0% for Na and 4.8% for Al.

**2. Background from interactions between L1 and L2** [Documented, C163]
- This is interaction in the material between tracker L1 and L2, which is the TRD and the upper TOF.
- It is evaluated by fitting the L1 charge distribution with templates of Ne, Na, Mg, Al and Si. Cuts on the L1 charge are then applied.
- The background varies smoothly with rigidity.
  - For Na it rises from 8% below 10 GV to 25% at 3 TV.
  - For Al it rises from 9% below 10 GV to 16% at 3 TV.
- Related items from the same claim:
  - Interactions above L1, in the carbon-fiber and aluminum-honeycomb supports, are estimated from simulation validated with AMS charge-changing cross sections.
  - Charge confusion from non-interacting Ne, Mg and Si is < 0.5% over the whole range.
  - The overall background-subtraction uncertainty for Na is 1.5% at 2 GV, 1.5% at 100 GV and 6% at 3 TV.

**3. Unfolding corrections for Na** [Documented, C164]
- Bin-to-bin migration is corrected by unfolding, with corrections (N_i - ℵ_i)/ℵ_i.
- For Na the correction is +20% at 3 GV. It falls smoothly to +6% at 10 GV, -1% at 100 GV, -10% at 300 GV and -20% at 3.0 TV.
- The paper says Al is similar, and I do not quote separate Al numbers.
- The unfolding is a migration correction. It is not part of the background subtraction, and the two are not double counted.

**4. Which flux the Na and Al spectral indices track** [Documented, C166]
- The spectral index is γ = d log Φ / d log R, obtained model-independently over rigidity intervals with boundaries 7.09, 12.0, 16.6, 28.8, 45.1, 80.5, 211.0 and 3000 GV.
- Below about 100 GV, the Na flux and spectral index follow the N flux and index.
- Above about 100 GV, the Al flux and index follow the N flux and index.
- The comparison N flux is the earlier AMS N result (Phys. Rep. 894), re-analysed over the same collection time. It is not a new N measurement in this Letter.
- The index values appear only in figures, which the ledger did not read, so I give no numbers.
- This is an empirical similarity. The physical reading (N as a mixed primary and secondary element, with Na secondary-dominated at low R and Al primary-dominated at high R) is the paper's interpretation, from the primary and secondary fits in C167.
