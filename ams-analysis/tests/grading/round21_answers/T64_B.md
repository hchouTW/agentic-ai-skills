All statements are from the AMS Na/Al/N Letter (PRL 127, 021101, 2021; source S59). The ledger entries were checked against the main article only. The Supplemental Material was not read.

**Data sample and rigidity range** [Documented, C162]
- The sample is 0.46 million sodium and 0.51 million aluminum nuclei.
- The rigidity range is 2.15 GV to 3.0 TV, in 49 bins.
- The data come from the first 8.5 years aboard the ISS, May 19, 2011 to October 30, 2019. In that time AMS collected 1.50 x 10^11 cosmic-ray events.
- Total flux errors at 100 GV are 5.0% for Na and 4.8% for Al.

**Background from interactions between L1 and L2** (TRD and upper TOF) [Documented, C163]
- This background is evaluated by fitting the tracker L1 charge distribution with Ne, Na, Mg, Al and Si templates, followed by a cut on the L1 charge.
- For Na it varies smoothly from 8% below 10 GV to 25% at 3 TV.
- For Al it varies smoothly from 9% below 10 GV to 16% at 3 TV.
- Interactions above L1 (carbon-fiber and aluminum-honeycomb supports) are a separate background, estimated from simulation. Do not merge it with the L1-L2 number.
- The overall background-subtraction uncertainty for Na is 1.5% at 2 GV, 1.5% at 100 GV and 6% at 3.0 TV.
- The overall background-subtraction uncertainty for Al is 1% at 2 GV, 1.5% at 100 GV and 5% at 3.0 TV.

**Unfolding corrections for Na** [Documented, C164]
- The bin-to-bin migration is corrected by unfolding. The correction is (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i, where N_i is the observed count in bin i and aleph_i is the unfolded count (aleph_i is the paper's symbol; I am writing it out here).
- For Na the correction is +20% at 3 GV, decreasing smoothly to +6% at 10 GV, -1% at 100 GV, -10% at 300 GV and -20% at 3.0 TV. The sign changes between 10 GV and 100 GV.
- The paper says Al is "similar".
- The rigidity-resolution systematic is separate. It is 3.5% at 2 GV, below 1% from 3 to 300 GV, and rises to 5% for Na at 3.0 TV (C165).

**Which flux the spectral indices track** [Documented, C166]
- The two elements do not track the same flux at the same rigidity.
- Below about 100 GV, the Na flux and spectral index follow the N flux and spectral index.
- Above about 100 GV, the Al flux and spectral index follow the N flux and spectral index.
- The spectral index gamma = d log(Phi)/d log(R) was obtained in a model-independent way. It was computed over non-overlapping rigidity intervals with boundaries 7.09, 12.0, 16.6, 28.8, 45.1, 80.5, 211.0 and 3000.0 GV.
- The comparison N flux is the earlier AMS N flux from Ref. [3] (Phys. Rep. 894). It was re-analysed over the same collection time. It is not a new N measurement in this Letter.
- Index values and flux shapes appear only in figures. I did not read numbers from the figures.
