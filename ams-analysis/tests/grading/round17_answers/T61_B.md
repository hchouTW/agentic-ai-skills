Source: AMS nitrogen flux letter (PRL 121, 051103, 2018; ledger S55), main article only. The Supplemental Material was not read, so per-cut definitions, charge-selection figures and the simulation validation of interactions are not covered here.

**Selection** [Documented, C138]
- Tracker: events must be downward-going, with a reconstructed inner-tracker track passing through L1. For R >= 1.3 TV the track must also pass through L9. The track fit must have chi2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate. Rigidity is measured by the tracker in the permanent-magnet field.
- Geomagnetic cutoff: the measured rigidity must exceed 1.2 times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff in the AMS field of view. The cutoff is found by backtracing from the top of AMS out to 50 Earth radii with the most recent IGRF model.
- Charge: the charge measured on L1, the upper TOF, the inner tracker and the lower TOF must be compatible with Z = 7. For R > 1.3 TV, L9 is also required.
- Charge confusion from noninteracting nuclei is negligible (< 0.1%) over the whole rigidity range.
- Data sample [Documented, C137]: 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events (after background subtraction), May 2011 to May 2016, 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, with a trigger efficiency above 98%. This is the Letter's number. The Phys. Rept. review quotes 3.9 million events from a later, larger dataset [C91].

**Purity** [Documented, C138]: overall purities of 90%-95%, depending on rigidity.

**Backgrounds from heavier nuclei (O, F, Ne) interacting in the detector** [Documented, C139]
- Interactions in the material between L1 and L2 (TRD and upper TOF):
  - Method: data-driven. The L1 charge distribution of events that the inner tracker selects as nitrogen is fitted with N, O, F and Ne charge templates. The templates come from L2, using noninteracting events in which L1 and L3-L8 measure the same charge.
  - Size: < 5% over the entire rigidity range.
- Interactions above L1:
  - Method: simulation.
  - Size: < 3% below 200 GV, rising to 6% at 3.3 TV.
- The overall uncertainty from total background subtraction is < 1.5% over the entire rigidity range.

**Caveats**
- The background fractions above are the paper's quoted bounds. Do not turn them into per-bin values.
- The simulation validation of nuclear interactions is in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.
- The nitrogen flux uses the inelastic N+C and N+O cross sections as a separate systematic (< 3% up to 100 GV, 4% at 3 TV, C141). This is a different effect from the background subtraction and should not be double counted with it.
