Source: AMS nitrogen flux Letter, PRL 121, 051103 (2018) (S55; main article only, Supplemental Material not read). No web access, so currency was not checked (index last verified 2026-09-20).

**Selection** [Documented, C138]
- Events are downward-going with a reconstructed inner-tracker track passing through L1. For R >= 1.3 TV the track must also pass through L9.
- The track fit must have chi2/d.o.f. < 10 in the bending coordinate.
- Geomagnetic cutoff: the measured rigidity must exceed 1.2 times the maximum geomagnetic cutoff within the AMS field of view. The cutoff is backtraced from the top of AMS out to 50 Earth radii with the most recent IGRF model.
- Charge: the measurements on L1, the upper TOF, the inner tracker, the lower TOF and, for R > 1.3 TV, L9 must all be compatible with Z = 7.
- Rigidity comes from the Tracker in the permanent-magnet field.
- Data sample [Documented, C137]: May 19, 2011 to May 26, 2016, 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, about 2.2 x 10^6 nitrogen events after background subtraction. Only seconds with normal detector operation, pointing within 40 degrees of zenith and the ISS outside the SAA are counted.

**Purity** [Documented, C138]
- Overall purities are 90%-95%, depending on rigidity.
- Charge confusion from noninteracting nuclei is negligible (< 0.1%) over the whole rigidity range.

**Backgrounds from heavier nuclei (O, F, Ne) interacting** [Documented, C139]
- Interactions in the material between L1 and L2 (TRD and upper TOF):
  - Method: fit the L1 charge distribution of events that the inner tracker selects as nitrogen, with N, O, F and Ne charge templates.
  - Templates are taken from L2, using noninteracting events that require L1 and L3-L8 to measure the same charge.
  - Size: < 5% over the entire rigidity range.
- Interactions in material above L1:
  - Method: estimated from simulation.
  - Size: < 3% below 200 GV, rising to 6% at 3.3 TV.
- Uncertainty on the flux from the total background subtraction: < 1.5% over the entire rigidity range.
- Caveat: the data-driven fit (estimate 1) and the simulation (estimate 2) cover different material regions. The validation of the simulated nuclear interactions is in the Supplemental Material, which was not read, so I cannot report it. The per-cut details and charge-selection figures are likewise in the Supplemental Material.
