Source: the AMS sulfur Letter, PRL 130, 211002 (2023), main article only; the Supplemental Material was not read.

**Primary fractions at 6 GV** [Documented, C173; Table I]
- C: 80 ± 1 %
- S: 82 ± 3 %
- N: 31 ± 1 %
- Na: 17 ± 2 %

C and S are results of this paper's primary-plus-secondary fits. N and Na are listed in Table I but come from an earlier AMS result (Ref. [36] in the paper), not from this Letter.

For context, the same table gives the fractions at 100 GV and 2 TV. C is 91 ± 0.5 % and 96 ± 0.5 %. S is 91 ± 1 % and 97 ± 1 %. N is 56 ± 1 % and 77 ± 3 %. Na is 35 ± 2 % and 62 ± 12 %.

**S/O fit** [Documented, C172]
- **Range:** the S/O flux ratio was fitted above 20 GV.
- **Fit function:** the paper's Eq. (3), with the break fixed at 86.5 GV. It is similar to Eq. (4) of Ref. [2].
- **Result:** δ = −0.05 ± 0.02.
- **Comparison value:** the average ⟨δ⟩ = −0.045 ± 0.008 of Ne/O, Mg/O and Si/O, previously published by AMS in Ref. [2]. It is not a result of this Letter. The two values agree within the quoted errors.

The S/Ne, S/Mg and S/Si ratios were fitted above 5.9 GV, which is a different range from the S/O fit.
