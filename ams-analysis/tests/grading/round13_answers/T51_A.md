**1. Hysteresis significance per rigidity bin below 11 GV (Table SA)** [Documented, C178]

For each bin, two intervals of 13 Bartels rotations (one before and one after 2014-2015) with the same normalized proton flux are chosen to give the most significant difference in the normalized antiproton flux. The table gives the ratio Phi_pbar^A/Phi_pbar^B, and sigma_hys is the significance of its difference from unity.

| Rigidity bin (GV) | Phi_pbar^A/Phi_pbar^B | sigma_hys |
|---|---|---|
| 1.00-2.97 | 1.22 +- 0.04 | 5.5 |
| 2.97-4.88 | 1.14 +- 0.02 | 5.8 |
| 4.88-5.90 | 1.12 +- 0.02 | 6.9 |
| 5.90-7.09 | 1.07 +- 0.02 | 4.0 |
| 7.09-8.48 | 1.10 +- 0.02 | 5.6 |
| 8.48-11.0 | 1.06 +- 0.01 | 4.2 |

Caveats:
- The text says the antiproton ratio differs from unity by more than 5 sigma at 1.00-2.97 GV, falling to 4 sigma at 8.48-11.0 GV, and that the ratio decreases with rigidity. The table is not strictly monotonic (1.07 at 5.90-7.09 GV, 1.10 at 7.09-8.48 GV).
- The significance method is only cited from an earlier AMS paper. Picking the most significant interval pair per bin brings a look-elsewhere effect. Treat these as the collaboration's quoted numbers, not validated global significances.

**3. Antiproton-electron slope k at 1.00-1.92 GV** [Documented, C179]

The relative variations V = (Phi - <Phi>)/<Phi> per Bartels rotation are fitted per rigidity bin with V_e- = k(e-, pbar) V_pbar (Eq. S1). Here <Phi> is the 11-year time-averaged flux.
- k(e-, pbar) = 1.78 +- 0.12 at 1.00-1.92 GV.
- It decreases with rigidity to 1.31 +- 0.08 at 5.90-7.09 GV.
- k is above unity with significance over 4 sigma, so the antiproton flux is modulated less than the electron flux.
- The relation is compatible with linear in the six bins from 1.00 to 7.09 GV shown.
- The per-bin fit errors and chi2/d.f. are in Fig. S6 and were not read.
