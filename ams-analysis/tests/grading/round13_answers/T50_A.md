All of this is from the AMS ten-year sulfur Letter (PRL 130, 211002, 2023), main article only [Documented, C173, C172].

**Primary fractions at 6 GV (Table I)**
- C: 80 ± 1 %
- S: 82 ± 3 %
- N: 31 ± 1 %
- Na: 17 ± 2 %

C and S come from this paper's fits of each flux to a weighted sum of a primary and a secondary flux. N and Na (and Al) are taken in Table I from the earlier AMS result, Ref. [36], not from this Letter's own fits. The fit assumes O or Si is the characteristic primary and B or F the characteristic secondary. It is a description of the fluxes, not a proof of mechanism.

**S/O fit**
- Range: the S/O flux ratio was fitted above 20 GV, using Eq. (3), a function with the break fixed at 86.5 GV. [Documented, C172]
- Result: δ = −0.05 ± 0.02.
- Comparison value: the average δ of Ne/O, Mg/O and Si/O, ⟨δ⟩ = −0.045 ± 0.008. This was previously published by AMS in Ref. [2]. It is not a result of this Letter.
- The two values are consistent, which supports putting S in the Ne-Mg-Si class.

The 5.9 GV fits in the paper are different ones. They are the S/Ne, S/Mg and S/Si ratios, not S/O.
