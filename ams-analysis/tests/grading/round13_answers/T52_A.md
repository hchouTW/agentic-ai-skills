**Neutron monitors (proton and helium time-structure paper, PRL 121, 051101, Supplemental Material Fig. SM 2 caption)** [Documented, C181]
- AMS compared the relative variation of its proton integral flux with the relative variation of the rate of the Oulu, Finland neutron monitor. Both were normalized to their averages from May 2011 to May 2017.
- The proton integral flux is integrated above a minimum rigidity. The figure uses minimum rigidities of 2.40, 3.64, 6.47, 10.10 and 21.10 GV.
- The AMS error bars are the quadratic sum of the statistical and time-dependent systematic errors.
- The caption states that the relative time-dependent variation of this neutron monitor matches the AMS proton flux only when the flux is integrated over rigidities greater than 6.47 GV.
- Caveats: this is caption text only. I did not read the plotted points, so no values are claimed from them. The statement applies to the Oulu monitor and these minimum rigidities. It is not a general statement about neutron monitors.

**Positron-to-electron ratio at the solar polarity reversal (electron and positron time-structure paper, PRL 121, 051102, Supplemental Material captions)** [Documented, C182; same value in C39 and C90]
- The duration of the transition, from 10% to 90% of the change in magnitude, is independent of energy at **830 ± 30 days**.
- The midpoint of the transition, t_1/2, does depend on energy. The effective reversal time t_rev is the authors' choice.
- Caveat: this is the caption's statement for the logistic fit of the ratio. No plotted value was read.
