**Neutron monitors in the proton and helium time-structure supplement (PRL 121, 051101, Supplemental Material Fig. SM 2)** [Documented, C181]

- The supplement compares the relative variation of the AMS proton integral flux with the relative variation of the rate of the Oulu (Finland) neutron monitor. Both are normalized to their averages from May 2011 to May 2017.
- The AMS flux is integrated above a minimum rigidity. The figure uses minimum rigidities of 2.40, 3.64, 6.47, 10.10 and 21.10 GV. The AMS error bars are the quadratic sum of the statistical and time-dependent systematic errors.
- The caption states that the relative time-dependent variation of this neutron monitor matches the AMS proton flux only when the flux is integrated over rigidities above 6.47 GV.
- Limits of this evidence: this is caption-level only, and I did not read any plotted values. The statement applies to the Oulu monitor and these minimum rigidities. It is not a general statement about neutron monitors.

**Positron-to-electron ratio at the polarity reversal (PRL 121, 051102, Supplemental Material Figs. SM 1-SM 7)** [Documented, C182; same value in C39]

- The supplement gives the duration of the transition, from 10% to 90% of the change in magnitude of R_e = Phi(e+)/Phi(e-), as 830 +- 30 days.
- This duration is independent of energy. The midpoint of the transition does depend on energy, and the effective reversal time t_rev is the authors' choice.
- The value comes from fitting R_e versus time at several energies with the logistic parameterization of Eq. (3) of that paper, as described in the figure captions. The captions show the polarity of the heliospheric magnetic field and mark the period without well-defined polarity.
- Limits of this evidence: this is the caption's statement for this fit, with no plotted values read.
