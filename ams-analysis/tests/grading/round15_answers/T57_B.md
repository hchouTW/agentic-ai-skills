**What the main text states [Documented, C134, C133, C131; main article only, Supplemental Material not read]**

At or near 3.3 TV for lithium:
- Rigidity resolution: the systematic rises to 8%-10% at 3.3 TV (below 1.5% under 200 GV). It comes from a maximum detectable rigidity (MDR) of 3.5 TV for Li with a 5% uncertainty, plus a 20% uncertainty on the amplitude of the non-Gaussian tails.
- Absolute rigidity scale: 5%-7% at 3.3 TV (below 1% up to 200 GV). It is driven by tracker misalignment, with 1/30 TV^-1 constraining it from the E/p of e+/e-.
- Inelastic cross section: below 3%-4% at 3.3 TV (below 2%-3% up to 100 GV).
- Unfolding (bin-to-bin migration) correction: -20% at 3.3 TV for Li. This is a correction to the flux, not an uncertainty.
- Background from interactions above L1: the simulated correction is 2% for Li at 3.3 TV. The paper quotes the resulting uncertainty on the flux from background corrections as below 1.5% over the whole rigidity range.

Quoted as bounds over the whole rigidity range, not specifically at 3.3 TV:
- Trigger efficiency: below 0.5%.
- Acceptance corrections from data/MC differences: below 5%.
- Reconstruction and selection: below 2%.
- Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: below 0.1%, and only quoted below 30 GV, so it is not relevant at 3.3 TV.

These are per-source figures, mostly upper bounds. The paper does not give a quadrature total for the 3.3 TV bin in the text. The only total error it quotes is 3%-4% at 100-108 GV [Documented, C131], which is a different bin.

**Caveats**
- I have not summed these. The sources are not simply independent. Some entries are "below X" limits, and the 3.3 TV bin is the last one, so the paper's own bin-by-bin breakdown is the only valid source for the total.
- Qualitatively, the rigidity resolution and the rigidity scale dominate at 3.3 TV, and the cross-section term is next. Treat that ordering as indicative only.
- The statistical error in the last bin is also large and must be added, because the Li sample is about 1.9 million nuclei over the full range.
- To get the exact number, use the Supplemental Material flux table for Li (the 3.3 TV row lists statistical, time-independent systematic and total error). Please check it against the paper before quoting.
