**Source:** AMS nitrogen flux paper, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), source S55, main article only (full-text level; the Supplemental Material was not read). 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV, 2.2 x 10^6 N events, May 2011 to May 2016. No web check was done, so nothing here claims currency.

**Bin-to-bin migration** [Documented, C140]
- Migration between rigidity bins is corrected by unfolding. The paper describes the procedure only by reference (its Ref. [20]), so I do not restate its details.
- The size of the correction, (N_i - aleph_i)/aleph_i, is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, -5% at 200 GV and -6% at 3.3 TV.

**Rigidity resolution** [Documented, C140]
- Tracker bending-coordinate resolution: 5.5 um, in agreement with simulation.
- MDR: 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty.
- The amplitudes of the non-Gaussian tails carry a 10% uncertainty.
- The resulting systematic error on the flux from the rigidity-resolution function is below 1% under 150 GV and 3% at 3.3 TV.
- The 5.5 um figure comes from the Supplemental Material of the paper's Ref. [16], which I did not read.
- The Gaussian-core resolution does not describe the tails. The tail amplitudes are what drive the migration and the quoted systematic.

**Rigidity-scale and cross-section systematics** [Documented, C141, main text only]
- Absolute rigidity scale (tracker misalignment, magnetic field map and temperature): below 0.6% up to 100 GV, rising to 5% in the last bin (1.3-3.3 TV).
- Inelastic cross sections (N+C and N+O): below 3% up to 100 GV, 4% at 3 TV.
- Other main-text systematics, for context:
  - Trigger efficiency: below 0.7%.
  - Geomagnetic cutoff factor varied from 1.0 to 1.4: below 0.1% under 30 GV.
  - Data/MC corrections to the effective acceptance: below 3% up to 500 GV, below 5% at 3.3 TV.
  - Reconstruction and selection: below 1%.
  - Background subtraction (C139): below 1.5%.
- The full breakdown is tabulated in the Supplemental Material, which I did not read.
- Total flux error: 4% at 100 GV [Documented, C137].
- The Phys. Rept. review quotes 3.8% at 100 GV for 3.9 x 10^6 N nuclei [Documented, C91]. That sample is larger than the paper's 2.2 x 10^6 events and the review does not reproduce this breakdown, so keep the two sets of numbers separate.

**Caveats**
- The migration and resolution numbers are from the main text only.
- Do not extend these values to other nuclei papers.
