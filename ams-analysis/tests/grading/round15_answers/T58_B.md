**Source.** AMS nitrogen letter, PRL 121, 051103 (2018), source S55 in the skill's ledger. The ledger level is full text of the main article only. The Supplemental Material was not read, and no web access was used. The nitrogen flux covers 2.2 GV to 3.3 TV in 66 bins, from the first five years of data (May 2011 to May 2016). [Documented, C137]

**Bin-to-bin migration.** [Documented, C140]
- Migration is corrected by unfolding. The paper describes the procedure only by reference to its Ref. [20], so the algorithm details are not in this summary.
- The size of the correction is quoted as (N_i - ℵ_i)/ℵ_i. It is +15% at 3 GV, +7% at 5 GV, -5% at 200 GV and -6% at 3.3 TV.
- The migration is driven by the rigidity resolution, so the unfolding and the resolution systematic below are the same response model. Count them once, not twice.

**Rigidity resolution.** [Documented, C140]
- The tracker bending-coordinate resolution is 5.5 μm, in agreement with simulation.
- The maximum detectable rigidity (MDR) is 3.5 TV, with a 5% uncertainty.
- The amplitudes of the non-Gaussian tails carry a 10% uncertainty. This matters because migration is driven by the tails as well as the Gaussian core.
- The resulting systematic error on the flux from the rigidity resolution function is below 1% under 150 GV and 3% at 3.3 TV.
- The 5.5 μm figure is not printed in the nitrogen letter itself. The ledger notes it comes from the Supplemental Material of the paper's Ref. [16], which I did not read.

**Rigidity scale and the other systematics quoted in the text.** [Documented, C141]
- **Absolute rigidity scale:** this covers tracker misalignment, the magnetic-field map and temperature. It is below 0.6% up to 100 GV and 5% in the last bin (1.3 to 3.3 TV).
- **Inelastic cross sections (N+C and N+O):** below 3% up to 100 GV and 4% at 3 TV. This is the cross-section systematic you asked about.
- **Trigger efficiency:** below 0.7% over the whole range.
- **Geomagnetic cutoff factor:** varying it from 1.0 to 1.4 gives below 0.1% under 30 GV.
- **Data-MC acceptance corrections:** below 3% up to 500 GV and below 5% at 3.3 TV.
- **Reconstruction and selection:** below 1% over the whole range.
- **Total background subtraction (related context):** below 1.5%. [Documented, C139]

**Overall error.** The total flux error is about 4% at 100 GV in this letter. [Documented, C137] The Phys. Rept. 894 review quotes 3.8% at 100 GV with a larger sample of 3.9 million nuclei. [Documented, C91] These are different data sets, so do not mix them.

**Caveats.**
- The values above are those printed in the main text. The full error breakdown by source and bin is tabulated in the Supplemental Material, which was not read.
- The ledger was last verified on 2026-09-20, with the nuclei papers read on 2026-10-02. I did not check for later nitrogen papers or errata.
- The Na/Al/N paper (PRL 127, 021101) re-analysed nitrogen over a different collection time. Do not attribute these numbers to it.
