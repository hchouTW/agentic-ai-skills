**Scope.** The Li, Be, B paper is PRL 120, 021101 (2018; source S54). It uses the first 5 years of data. The Ne, Mg, Si paper is PRL 124, 211102 (2020; S56). It uses the first 7 years. The values below are the main-text numbers only. The Supplemental Material was not read, so no per-bin totals are given. This is an offline answer, with index last verified 2026-09-20.

**Comparison** (all [Documented]; Li/Be/B from C134 and C132, Ne/Mg/Si from C146 and C144)

| Item | Li, Be, B | Ne, Mg, Si |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger efficiency | > 98% for 3 <= Z <= 5 over the whole rigidity range. Systematic < 0.5%. | > 94% for Ne, Mg, Si over the whole rigidity range. Systematic < 1%. |
| Inelastic cross-section systematic | < 2-3% up to 100 GV, < 3-4% at 3.3 TV | < 3.5% up to 100 GV, < 4% from 100 GV to 3 TV |
| Absolute rigidity-scale systematic | < 1% up to 200 GV, rising to 5-7% at 3.3 TV | < 1% up to 300 GV, 6% at 3 TV |

- **Trigger:** the Ne/Mg/Si trigger efficiency is lower and its systematic is larger. These are per-source sizes and are not totals.
- **Inelastic:** the Ne/Mg/Si cross-section error is larger, by roughly 1 percentage point up to 100 GV. At the top of the range the two are comparable (< 4% for Ne/Mg/Si versus < 3-4% for Li/Be/B). The ranges are quoted with different upper edges (3.3 TV versus 3 TV), so do not read them as a bin-by-bin comparison.
- **Rigidity scale:** both are sub-percent at low rigidity and grow to a few percent at the TV end. The scale error is amplified by the local spectral index and is the dominant systematic at the highest rigidity. The stated knee differs, at 200 GV for Li/Be/B and 300 GV for Ne/Mg/Si. The top-end values (5-7% at 3.3 TV versus 6% at 3 TV) are comparable.

**Where 1/30 TV^-1 comes from**
- It is the residual tracker misalignment error on the rigidity scale. It is estimated by comparing the tracker 1/|R| with the ECAL 1/E, that is the E/p ratio, for electrons and positrons.
- In the Ne/Mg/Si paper the value is explicitly **quoted from Ref. [26], Berdugo et al., NIM A 869, 10 (2017)**. It is a cited value, not measured in that Letter. [Documented, C146]
- In the Li/Be/B paper the same figure is stated as "tracker misalignment 1/30 TV^-1 from E/p of e+/e-", also not a fresh determination there. [Documented, C134] I did not read which reference that paper attaches to it.
- The 1/30 TV^-1 figure is not a fit result of either paper. Do not describe it as measured in these two Letters.
- The review (Phys. Rept. 894) describes the same method. It states a time-dependent correction after which the accuracy of the shift is within 0.033 TV^-1 (3% at 1 TV), limited by positron statistics. [Documented, C64] That is numerically 1/30, but it comes from a different, later calibration. Do not treat it as the origin of the Letters' number.
- A second rigidity-scale contribution in the Ne/Mg/Si paper comes from the magnetic-field map measurement and its temperature corrections. [Documented, C146]
- Because the same E/p method and the same tracker-misalignment value feed both papers, the rigidity-scale errors are largely common between them. They are not independent measurements.
