**Short answer: the total systematic error on the lithium flux in the 3.3 TV bin is not available from the sources I can cite, so I won't give a number.** The main article of the AMS Li/Be/B paper (PRL 120, 021101, 2018) gives only per-source size bounds. The per-bin systematic and total errors are in its Supplemental Material tables, which are not indexed here. A total can't be rebuilt from the per-source bounds, because the bounds are upper limits ("<") and may not be independent or added in quadrature.

What the paper does say about the sizes at or near 3.3 TV [Documented, C134, S54, main article only]:

| Source | Size |
|---|---|
| Rigidity resolution (MDR 3.5 TV for Li, 5% uncertainty, 20% on the non-Gaussian tail amplitudes) | 8-10% at 3.3 TV (< 1.5% below 200 GV) |
| Rigidity scale (tracker misalignment 1/30 TV^-1, from E/p of e+/e-) | 5-7% at 3.3 TV (< 1% up to 200 GV) |
| Inelastic cross section | < 3-4% at 3.3 TV (< 2-3% up to 100 GV) |
| Acceptance (data/MC differences) | < 5%, over the entire rigidity range |
| Reconstruction and selection | < 2%, over the entire rigidity range |
| Trigger efficiency | < 0.5% |
| Geomagnetic cutoff factor (varied 1.0-1.4) | < 0.1%, quoted only below 30 GV |

Backgrounds [Documented, C133]:
- Background from interactions above L1 is estimated from simulation, and for Li it is a correction of about 2% at 3.3 TV.
- The uncertainty on the flux from the background corrections is < 1.5% over the whole range.
- The L1-L2 interaction background is < 0.5% for lithium.

Caveats:
- The resolution and rigidity-scale terms dominate at 3.3 TV. Using the quoted ranges, these two are the largest by far, so the total there is far larger than the 3-4% total error quoted at 100-108 GV [Documented, C131].
- Several of these are upper bounds ("<") rather than the actual bin values.
- The paper's unfolding correction for Li at 3.3 TV is -20%. That is a correction to the counts, not an uncertainty, so don't confuse it with a systematic.
- The 3.3 TV figure is the upper edge of the last bin range (1.9 GV to 3.3 TV). If the exact bin edges or numbers matter, take them from the paper's Supplemental Material tables.

To get the exact total and per-source values for that bin, read the Supplemental Material flux table for lithium. I could not check it here, since there is no web access.
