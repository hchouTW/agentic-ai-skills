## Clusters and single-sample slips

- **Cluster, rubric-caused (T52 A and B): the 2-3 GeV electron-structure point is omitted.** The T52 critical requirements include "the 2-3 GeV electron structure is called model-dependent by the caption". The prompt asks only about neutron monitors and the transition duration. Leaving out an unasked caption detail is correct behaviour, so I made no deduction. See the rubric section.
- **Cluster, carried over from round 12 (T48 A and B): no reason given for "conversions off".** Both samples set `combo_level=0` and `energy_convert_level=0`. Neither explains that level-2 conversions for elements can exceed the data uncertainties, or that proton conversion is exact (C127). This is the same as round 12 and gets the same treatment: no deduction. The rubric still says "explains".
- **Cluster, carried over, no deduction (T46_B, plus the general phrasing in T46_A, T48_B and T49): verification date.** T46_B says both S02 and S03 are "at full-text level ... in the 2026-09-20 index". Both were read on 2026-10-02 (`sources.json` access_date). The round 12 narrow fix (in `references/source-index.md`, after "Access/verification date for every row: **2026-09-20** unless stated.", add "rows read later carry their own date (e.g. S02, S03, S09, S50-S60: 2026-10-02); give that date when citing them") has evidently not been applied. It is still the narrowest fix.
- **Single sample (T49_A): light-ratio hardening not attributed to earlier AMS papers.** A writes "The hardening seen in B/O (0.140 +- 0.025) is therefore also seen in F/Si". C160 says 0.140 +- 0.025 is the combined hardening of Li/C, Be/C, B/C, Li/O, Be/O and B/O, from earlier AMS papers (Refs. [10,11]). It is not the B/O hardening of this Letter: that Letter's own Eq. (4) fit gives Delta2 - Delta1 = 0.145 for B/O. A narrows the number to B/O and drops its provenance. That is the "numbers kept with their paper" requirement. B gets it right. Narrow fix: in `references/nuclei-and-isotopes.md`, the Tier 1 sentence "numbers a paper quotes from an earlier reference are marked as such in the claim" could add "(for example, the 0.140 +- 0.025 light-ratio hardening in C160 and the -0.045 +- 0.008 average in C172 are earlier AMS results quoted for comparison)". Single sample, so this is optional.
- **Single sample (T48_B): CRDB holdings stated as fact.** The Pitfalls section says "CRDB holds several AMS proton datasets (time-averaged, per Bartels rotation, daily)". No query was run. C129 supports only "AMS-02 time series" (per-rotation and daily, per its limitation). The time-averaged proton dataset in particular is an assumption. The source is `references/cosmic-ray-databases.md` step 3: "One experiment appears as several datasets (time-averaged, per Bartels rotation, per day, different periods and analyses)". It reads as a statement about AMS content. Narrow fix: append "; which AMS datasets a given release actually holds is [Unknown/needs input] until a query is run (C129 documents only that AMS-02 time series were entered)".
- **Single sample (T50_B): the empirical nature of the fit is not stated.** The T50 critical requirements include "fits are empirical descriptions". B gives the Eq. (3) fit with no such qualifier. A covers it ("a description of the fluxes, not a proof of mechanism"). C172's limitation already says it, so no reference fix is proposed.
- **Minor, no deduction:**
  - T50_A: "The two values are consistent, which supports putting S in the Ne-Mg-Si class" is A's own inference and is not labelled. C172 bases the class conclusion on the S/Ne, S/Mg and S/Si fits.
  - T49_B: "If heavy ... and light secondaries propagated the same way, the double ratio would be flat" is an unlabelled simplification.
  - T49_A: rephrases "heavy cosmic rays from F to Si" versus "light from He to O" as secondaries "from parents Ne-Si" and "He-O".
