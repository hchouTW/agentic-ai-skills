## Clusters and single-sample slips

- **Cluster (T33 A and B): no verification level for the alternatives offered.** The T33 entry requires "the verified alternatives with their level". Neither sample gives a level for S02, S03, S04 or S46, although `source-policy.md` step 3 already says to give "each one's verification level, publication date and data-taking period from its ledger row". Round 11 had levels in both samples, so this is an adherence regression, not a missing rule. No reference fix proposed; re-check in the next round before touching step 3.
- **Cluster (8/8 answers): verification date quoted as 2026-09-20 only.** S02, S03 and S09 were read at main-article level on 2026-10-02, and S50-S53 were accessed on 2026-10-02. Every answer gives "index last verified 2026-09-20". This is not wrong for the index as a whole, so I made no deduction. Narrow fix: in `references/source-index.md`, the sentence "Access/verification date for every row: **2026-09-20** unless stated." could add "rows read later carry their own date (e.g. S02, S03, S09, S50-S53: 2026-10-02); give that date when citing them".
- **Single sample (T33_A): no data-taking periods.** It gives neither S02's (2011-05-19 to 2012-12-10) nor S03's (to 2013-11-26) data period, although both are in C03 and C113. `source-policy.md` step 2 already requires them, and B gives them. An isolated slip, so no fix proposed.
- **Single sample, with a reference cause (T46_A): C110 missed.** A says "I have no indexed claim giving the 2013 paper's own wording about the high-energy trend". It also says that whether the rise excludes pure secondary production "is your model step, not an AMS-stated result in the claims I checked". Both are false. C110 (S02, full-text) states that the 10-250 GeV rise "is not consistent with only secondary production" and that "the behaviour above 250 GeV needs more statistics". That is the core of the user's "pure-secondary-production test" question. B found it. Narrow fix: in `references/antimatter-and-leptons.md`, the paragraph starting "[Documented, S02 (PRL 110, 141102) and S03 ...] **Early positron and electron measurements (2013-2014):**" has a sentence "Results: above about 200 GeV the fraction no longer increases (C113), ...". It never cites C110. Add a clause: "the 2013 paper states that the rise from 10 to 250 GeV is not consistent with only secondary production and that the behaviour above 250 GeV needs more statistics (C110)".
- **Single sample (T48_B; T48_A borderline): S41 cited for daily proton fluxes.** B writes "the daily proton fluxes and their periodicities (S43, S41)". S41 is the Bartels-rotation proton and helium fine-time-structure paper (C38), not daily, and it has no periodicity search. A writes "the daily and Bartels-rotation proton fluxes (S41, S43)". Taken as a set that is supported, but the order pairs "daily" with S41. `source-policy.md` step 7 already covers this case. No fix proposed.
- **Single sample (T47_B): "mainly".** B says the time-dependent systematic "comes mainly from" the unfolding's spectral-shape term. C121 lists that term but does not say it dominates the time-dependent budget.
- **Minor, both T33 samples:** first-person absence phrasing. A has "I can't locate it" and B has "I could not locate the paper in any source". With no web access, B's version overclaims a search. Step 8 of `source-policy.md` asks for "the index records no ...". No deduction beyond the source-discipline point already taken.

## Are the T46-T48 rubrics right?

- **T46: too strict in one place, under-specified in another.**
  - The prohibited claim "a number not in C03, C107-C119" would, read literally, forbid a ledger-backed pointer to later work, such as C04's 1.9 million positrons, which T46_A uses. I did not apply it that way. Suggested wording: "an AMS number not in the ledger, or a fraction number not in C03, C107-C119".
  - The critical requirements do not name C110's "not consistent with only secondary production" statement. It is the most directly relevant ledger sentence for a "pure-secondary-production test". Add "the 2013 paper's statement that the 10-250 GeV rise is not consistent with only secondary production (C110), attributed to S02, not S03".
- **T47: fine.**
  - "Tables S1-S140" matches C122 (S1-S139 per rotation, S140 the average).
  - Quoting the 4 sigma figure itself is allowed (C123 is numeric-allowed); the requirement is only to say that it rests on an unread supplement table. Both samples handled this correctly.
  - Optional addition: the 1.00-2.97 GV scope of the C123 correlation figures. Neither sample stated it.
- **T48: mostly right, one prohibited claim too strict.**
  - "claiming CRDB contains a specific AMS dataset without a query" conflicts with C129. The v4.1 abstract states that CRDB includes the AMS-02 time series. Suggested carve-out: "except as stated by the CRDB papers (C129), labelled as the database's own statement and unchecked".
  - The critical requirement "explains native axis, combo level and energy conversion choices (C127, C128)" is met only as "turn them off" in both samples. Neither says why (approximate conversions for elements can exceed the data uncertainties; proton conversion is exact). I treated "native axis, combos and conversion off" as sufficient. If an explanation is wanted, say so in the entry.
